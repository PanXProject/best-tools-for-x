## About
Seed Audio is an independent platform for exploring and creating with an all-in-one AI audio model. It helps creators and developers generate speech, original music, sound effects, foley, cross-lingual synthesis, and multi-character dialogue from a single prompt.

## Features
- Speech and voice generation
- Original music generation
- Cinematic sound effects and foley
- Cross-lingual synthesis
- Multi-character dialogue
- Web-based creator workflow
