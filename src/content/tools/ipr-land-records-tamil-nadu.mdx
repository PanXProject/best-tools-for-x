## About

Guide to land records in Tamil Nadu on India Property Register: Patta, Chitta, A-Register, how plots are identified, the local terms and where the records are held. TN e-Services shows patta, chitta, A-Register, FMB and urban TSLR by survey, patta number or owner name, in Unicode Tamil. DILRMP-MIS shows 100% on records, maps and linkage, and a Land Stack pilot started here in December 2025. TNREGINET offers free EC view and guideline values from 2002.

## Features

- Explains what the state calls its land records and what each shows
- Shows how plots are identified, from the state down to the village
- Lists the government portals and sources that hold the records
