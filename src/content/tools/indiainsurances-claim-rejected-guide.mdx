## About

A free IndiaInsurances guide for policyholders whose claim was rejected or cut: get the reason in writing with the exact clause, read that clause on the policy's page with the law beside it, complain in writing to the insurer's grievance officer, then escalate to Bima Bharosa, the Insurance Ombudsman (free, claims up to Rs 50 lakh, within one year of the insurer's reply) or the consumer commission.

## Features

- Get the reason in writing
- Grievance officer, then Ombudsman
- Ombudsman free up to Rs 50 lakh
