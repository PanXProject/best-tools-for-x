## About

A free eCourtsIndia guide to checking FIR status online in India: getting the free FIR copy under the BNSS, searching state police portals, then tracking bail and the criminal case in court. Part of eCourtsIndia, which lets lawyers, litigants and researchers search Indian court cases by CNR, party, advocate or judge, with daily cause lists. Not legal advice.

## Features

- Explains the free FIR copy under BNSS
- Points to state police portals
- Explains how to track bail and the court case
