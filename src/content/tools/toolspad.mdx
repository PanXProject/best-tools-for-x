## About
ToolsPad brings connected business apps into shared workspaces for small and medium businesses, agencies and consultants. Capture a client inquiry, assign the next action, and organize the notes and tasks needed to deliver the work. An ongoing Free plan is available alongside paid plans, with app-specific limits.

## Features
- CRM for customer and company records
- Tasks and notes for team follow-through
- Forms for collecting inquiries
- Appointment scheduling
- Quotes and invoices
- Shared workspaces for organizing business activity
