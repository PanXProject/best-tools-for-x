## About
Chemistry AI is an online chemistry solver that accepts typed questions, equations, images, and worksheets, then provides clear step-by-step solutions for students and tutors.

## Features
- Step-by-step chemistry problem solving
- Equation balancing and stoichiometry guidance
- Organic chemistry and equilibrium explanations
- Image and worksheet question support
- Clear visual learning assistance
