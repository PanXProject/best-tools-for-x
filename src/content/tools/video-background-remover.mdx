## About
Video Background Remover is an online AI tool that removes backgrounds from videos without a green screen. Creators can upload clips, isolate people or products, and download videos with transparent or custom backgrounds for social media, presentations, and e-commerce.

## Features
- Automatic AI video background removal
- Background removal without a green screen
- Support for MP4, MOV, AVI, MKV, and WMV videos
- Transparent and custom video backgrounds
- Browser access on desktop and mobile devices
