## About

Guide to land records in Himachal Pradesh on India Property Register: Jamabandi, Shajra Nasb, Shajra Kishtwar, how plots are identified, the local terms and where the records are held. HimBhoomi shows a public jamabandi and shajra nasb copy by district, tehsil, village and jamabandi year, with a tehsil-level owner name search; villages under Settlement operation are excluded.

## Features

- Explains what the state calls its land records and what each shows
- Shows how plots are identified, from the state down to the village
- Lists the government portals and sources that hold the records
