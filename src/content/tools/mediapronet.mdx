## About
MEDIAPRONET is a launch and discovery directory where builders list what they just shipped. Visitors browse newly launched startups, SaaS tools and online products by category, and each listing gets its own page with a link to the product. Makers can submit for free or pick a paid upgrade for faster publishing.

## Features
- Free product listing with an optional paid upgrade for faster publishing
- A dedicated listing page for every product with a link to its website
- Category browsing of newly launched startups, SaaS tools and online products
- Made for indie hackers and small teams who want their launch to be found
