## About
[Random Topic Generator](https://randomtopicgenerator.net/) is a free AI-powered impromptu speaking practice tool. Generate up to 10 random speech topics instantly, practice with a built-in timer, and get detailed AI feedback on grammar, pronunciation, fluency, filler words, and more. Perfect for Toastmasters, students, and professionals.

- --

## Features
- Random AI-generated impromptu speech topics (up to 10 at a time)
- Topics available in 40+ languages
- Custom topic requirements (audience, difficulty, scenario)
- Built-in 1, 3, or 5-minute practice timer
- One-tap timer start/pause with ±30 second fine-tuning
- AI speech analysis report after each session
- Full speech transcript (including fillers)
- Native rewrite for natural phrasing comparison
- Grammar corrections with original vs. corrected sentences
- Word-level pronunciation and clarity feedback
- Filler word counter (um, uh, like, you know, etc.)
- Pacing and rhythm analysis
- Pause detection and feedback
- Tone and vocal confidence evaluation
- 6-Dimension scoring: Grammar, Coherence, Vocabulary, Fluency, Filler Words, Confidence
