## About
ViralVideoAI is an AI tool where you upload a photo and apply viral video trend templates (for example a Tiny Desk trend) to generate your own version of the trending video. No prompt needed. Paid credit packs start at $9.99, no free trial.

## Features
- Upload a photo and apply viral trend templates
- Outputs 10-second vertical 9:16 MP4 clips
- No prompt writing needed
- Paid credit packs from $9.99
