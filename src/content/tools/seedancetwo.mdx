## About
SeedanceTwo is an AI video generator where you can use Seedance 2.5, Seedance 2.0 and Seedance 1.5 for text-to-video, image-to-video and reference-to-video. Seedance 2.5 makes clips up to 30 seconds at up to 1080p, with multi-shot storytelling and audio sync. New users get 20 credits after verifying, and pay-as-you-go credits never expire. SeedanceTwo is an independent site and is not affiliated with ByteDance or Seed.

## Features
- Text-to-video, image-to-video and reference-to-video with Seedance 2.5, 2.0 and 1.5
- Clips up to 30 seconds at up to 1080p with audio sync
- Multi-shot storytelling in 16:9, 9:16, 1:1 and more
- 20 Free credits for new verified users and credits that never expire
