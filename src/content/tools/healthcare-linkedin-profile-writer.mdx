## About
Healthcare LinkedIn Profile Writer is a free AI tool for nurses, CNAs, techs, therapists and every other healthcare worker. Enter your role, where you have worked, the licenses you hold and the job you want next, and it writes three LinkedIn headlines, an About section and the skills to add, in the words healthcare recruiters search for. It uses only the facts you give and never invents credentials. No sign-up.

## Features
- Three LinkedIn headline options
- An About section written from your own facts
- Skills to add, in recruiter search words
- Free, no sign-up, nothing stored
