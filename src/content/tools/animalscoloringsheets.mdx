## About
AnimalsColoringSheets turns an animal photo into a printable coloring page in seconds. Pick the Classic, Bold & simple, or Pencil style and download a PNG or PDF. Signing in with Google gives three free AI generations, and the site also has a free library of printable dog and dinosaur coloring sheets.

## Features
- Photo to coloring page conversion for animal photos
- Three line-art styles: Classic, Bold & simple, Pencil
- PNG and PDF download, ready to print
- Three free AI generations after Google sign-in
- Free printable dog and dinosaur sheet library with no account needed
- Printing tips and classroom ideas on each page
