## About
Scientific Figure AI helps researchers turn research ideas, abstracts, and sketches into editable scientific illustrations, flowcharts, and graphical abstracts. Refine labels, arrows, colors, and layout, then export the result. A free allowance is available with optional paid credits and plans.

## Features
- Generate figures and graphical abstracts from research ideas or sketches
- Edit labels, arrows, colors, and layouts after generation
- Export finished visuals as PNG, JPG, SVG, or PPTX
