## About

A free IndiaCos list of 9,214 corporate insolvency resolution process (CIRP) announcements published by the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of India, newest first. Each entry names the company or LLP, its CIN and the insolvency professional, and links to the company record on IndiaCos. Useful for creditors, suppliers and researchers checking whether a counterparty is under insolvency.

## Features

- Lists 9,214 CIRP announcements from IBBI
- Names the insolvency professional
- Links each entry to the company record
