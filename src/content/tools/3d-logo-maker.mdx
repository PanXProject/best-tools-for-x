## About
3D Logo Maker is a browser workspace for giving existing logo artwork depth. I am associated with this project, and this listing was prepared with AI writing assistance. Upload permitted artwork, adjust the appearance, and review the result in its intended context. The free 512-pixel PNG export includes a watermark; paid options offer additional capabilities.

## Features
- Import existing SVG, PNG, JPEG or WebP artwork
- Adjust shape depth and bevel in a live preview
- Compare materials, surface color and lighting
- Change camera angle, zoom and framing
- Export a PNG with a transparent or solid background
- Save a project to an account for later revision
