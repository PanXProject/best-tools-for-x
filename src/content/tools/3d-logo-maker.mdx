## About
3D Logo Maker helps people add depth to existing logo artwork in a browser. I am associated with this project; this description was prepared with AI assistance. Use permitted artwork and check the result in its intended setting. Free 512-pixel PNG exports are watermarked; paid options differ.

## Features
- Import existing SVG, PNG, JPEG or WebP artwork
- Adjust depth and bevel with a live preview
- Compare materials, lighting and camera angles
- Export an image with a solid or transparent background
