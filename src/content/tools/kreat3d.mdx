## About
Kreat3D Image to 3D gives makers a way to test a shape before committing to detailed modeling. Begin with a photo, sketch or concept image, preview the AI-generated model in a browser, and take GLB, OBJ, FBX or STL output into your next tool. Hidden surfaces are inferred, so review the result for your project.

## Features
- Generate geometry alone or geometry with textures
- Add multiple views where the selected model and plan support them
- Continue work in Blender, Unity, Unreal, Three.js or printing slicers
- Use Studio, Canvas, MCP or CLI to suit manual and automated workflows
- Try selected generation options with starter credits and no credit card
