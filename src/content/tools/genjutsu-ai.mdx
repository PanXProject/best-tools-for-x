## About
Genjutsu AI is a reference-guided video-to-video editor. Upload a clip, add up to nine reference images and describe the change to swap a character, outfit, product or background, or restyle the whole shot, while the original motion, camera movement and timing stay in place. Outputs 5-30 second MP4 at 480p-1080p with the source audio kept.

## Features
- Swap characters, outfits, products or backgrounds with reference images
- Restyle the whole shot while keeping the original motion and camera movement
- Add up to nine reference images to guide each edit
- Keep the source audio in the exported video
- Export 5-30 second MP4 files at 480p to 1080p
