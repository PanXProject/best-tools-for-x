## About

Guide to land records in Andhra Pradesh on India Property Register: 1B, Adangal / Pahani, FMB / LP map, how plots are identified, the local terms and where the records are held. Mee Bhoomi lists Adangal, 1B, survey number, LP map and e-passbook, but its form did not render on 11 Oct 2026. DILRMP-MIS shows 92.40% of villages computerised (16,267 of 17,605). Deeds run on the state CARD system; AP repealed its 2023 Land Titling Act in August 2024.

## Features

- Explains what the state calls its land records and what each shows
- Shows how plots are identified, from the state down to the village
- Lists the government portals and sources that hold the records
