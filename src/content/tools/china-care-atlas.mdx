## About

China Care Atlas is a comprehensive bilingual English-Chinese medical directory helping expats, travelers, and international families find verified hospitals, English-speaking clinics, and pharmacies across China.

## Features

- Dual English and Chinese hospital names and verified street addresses
- Filter healthcare providers by city, medical specialty, and emergency care
- Identify clinics with English-speaking doctors and international insurance
