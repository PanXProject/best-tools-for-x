## About
OnlyTron delegates TRON energy to your wallet for a set time, so a USDT TRC20 transfer no longer burns TRX. You send TRX to a platform address and the energy arrives within a minute. Your TRX stays in your wallet and no staking is needed.

## Features
- Energy packages of 65,000 and 131,000 units for one hour
- Pay in TRX, no account needed
- Telegram bot for rentals
- API for automated payouts
- Works with TronLink, Trust Wallet, TokenPocket and other TRON wallets
- Support on Telegram
