## About

Jammu & Kashmir: 20 districts and 6,419 villages. How to check land records, maps, registration and encumbrance in Jammu & Kashmir, and the government. J&K land records sit on the LRIS portal (Aapki Zameen Aapki Nigrani, launched 22 Oct 2021), which was under maintenance or returning HTTP 504 on 11 Oct 2026; the DoLR directory points at a department CMS instead. DILRMP-MIS shows 70.54% of villages with computerised records (4,832 of 6,850).

## Features

- Lists every district of the state with its village count
- Explains how to check land records, maps, registration and encumbrance
- Links the government portals that hold the records
