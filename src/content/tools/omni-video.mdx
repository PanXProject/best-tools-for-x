## About

Omni Video is a browser-based AI video generator that turns text descriptions and reference images into short clips. Creators and marketers can use it to explore visual ideas for social posts, product presentations, and campaigns.

## Features

- Text to Video: Generate clips from written scene descriptions.
- Image to Video: Animate an uploaded reference image.
- Prompt-Based Creation: Describe subjects, actions, and visual styles.
- Creative Iteration: Refine prompts to explore different results.
- Video Downloads: Download completed clips.
- Browser-Based Access: Use the tools without installing separate software.
