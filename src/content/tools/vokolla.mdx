## About
Vokolla is an all-in-one press release preparation and distribution platform for businesses and brands. AI assistance helps customers draft, review and refine their announcements, choose a distribution package, and track publication progress and media coverage in one workspace. Selected packages include outlets such as AP News, USA Today, Yahoo and Business Insider. Transparent, pay-per-release pricing starts at US$69, with no subscription required.

## Features
- AI-assisted drafting, review and refinement of press releases
- Distribution package selection in one workspace
- Publication progress and media coverage tracking
- Pay-per-release pricing from US$69 with no subscription
