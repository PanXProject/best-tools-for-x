## About
Transformer.gg is a marketplace for sponsored posts on X. A brand pastes its website and gets X creators who fit what it sells, each with a suggested price, then sends offers in a few clicks. Free to join, with a 20% fee on paid offers. Transformer.gg is part of North Bay Ventures (North Bay Opco I, Inc., Miami, Florida). The founder is Jon Oringer, who founded Shutterstock.

## Features
- Suggested X creators for your website, each with a suggested price
- Card charged only when a creator accepts an offer
- Money held until the post is live with X's Paid partnership label and our team confirms it
- Tracked link on every offer, so clicks and sales are credited to the creator who drove them
- Creators keep 80% and are paid by PayPal or USDC
- Runs from the web dashboard or from Claude, Codex or your own agent through the MCP server, CLI or REST API
- Free check of the value of one sponsored post on any X account, with no sign-up
