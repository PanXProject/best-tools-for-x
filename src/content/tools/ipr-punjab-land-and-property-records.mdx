## About

Punjab: 23 districts and 12,163 villages. How to check land records, maps, registration and encumbrance in Punjab, and the government portals that hold them. Punjab's jamabandi portal states that digital RoR is available since 2002, but its JavaScript search did not load on 11 Oct 2026, and the DoLR link is the department website. DILRMP-MIS shows 99.63% of villages computerised. Deeds run on Easy Registry (NGDRS since June 2017) and revenue-court cases on a separate RCMS.

## Features

- Lists every district of the state with its village count
- Explains how to check land records, maps, registration and encumbrance
- Links the government portals that hold the records
