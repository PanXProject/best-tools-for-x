## About
Curated TikTok slideshows and Instagram carousels for your brand on autopilot. Wavegrain writes and designs every slide from your brand and your photos, then schedules and posts them for you. Edit any slide, plan posts on a calendar, or export for TikTok and Instagram. AI agents can use it through an MCP server.

## Features
- Curated slideshows and carousels from your brand and your own photos
- Brand voice, logo and colours read from your website
- Image boards per brand, with "Collection only" or "Collection + AI"
- Reusable slideshow formats
- Slide text and image editor
- Content calendar, scheduling and posting
- 1080X1920 TikTok ZIP and vertical MP4; Instagram 4:5, 3:4, square and Story exports
- MCP server for Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor and other agents
