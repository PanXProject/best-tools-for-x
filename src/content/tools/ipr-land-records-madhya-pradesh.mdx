## About

Guide to land records in Madhya Pradesh on India Property Register: Khasra, Khatauni, Aabadi Adhikar Abhilekh, how plots are identified, the local terms and where the records are held. MP Bhulekh searches by survey number, plot number or bhumiswami within a village; certified khasra, khatauni and maps need a login, and the official host returned 504 on 11 Oct 2026. The DoLR link (mpbhulekhrecords.com) is a third-party blog.

## Features

- Explains what the state calls its land records and what each shows
- Shows how plots are identified, from the state down to the village
- Lists the government portals and sources that hold the records
