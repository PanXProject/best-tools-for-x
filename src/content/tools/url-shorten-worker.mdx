## About
Url-Shorten-Worker is a free, open-source (MIT) URL shortener and Pastebin that runs entirely on Cloudflare Workers + Workers KV. One index.js file, deployable in minutes on the free tier, with per-link expiry and CAPTCHA protection.

## Features
- Custom short links and paste mode (up to 10 MiB, ?raw=1)
- Self-hosted on your own domain
- Zero running cost on Cloudflare free tier
- English and Chinese docs
