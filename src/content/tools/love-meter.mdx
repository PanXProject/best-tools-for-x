## About

Love Meter is a free, playful compatibility tool. Enter two names to get a love score and couple type — no sign-up needed. Also includes Love Meter by Photo, FLAMES Calculator, and Love Oracle. Every result is free; optional AI readings unlock deeper insights with one-time credit packs.

## Features

- Love Meter by name — fixed score and couple type, same names always same result
- Love Meter by Photo — AI-powered visual vibe check from one or two photos
- FLAMES Calculator — classic letter-cancellation game with step-by-step breakdown
- Love Oracle — reflective reading inspired by Xiao Liu Ren
- Free result for every tool, no sign-up required
- Optional AI deep reading: strengths, challenges, communication tips & couple story
