## About

Guide to land records in Chandigarh on India Property Register: Jamabandi, Mutation, how plots are identified, the local terms and where the records are held. Chandigarh's revenue site offers jamabandi search by owner name, khewat number and khasra number, plus sanctioned mutations. DILRMP-MIS counts only 25 RoRs over 25 villages, all computerised, and ranks Chandigarh at 100% on its indicators.

## Features

- Explains what the state calls its land records and what each shows
- Shows how plots are identified, from the state down to the village
- Lists the government portals and sources that hold the records
