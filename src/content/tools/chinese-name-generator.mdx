## About
Chinese Name Generator is an AI tool that composes culturally appropriate Chinese names (surname + given name) from your English name and traits, with pinyin and a character-by-character meaning breakdown. No sign-up needed; the first three names per day are free.

## Features
- Culturally appropriate names instead of literal sound-alike transliterations
- Pinyin for every generated name
- Character-by-character explanation of radicals, origin and meaning
- Style options: classic, modern, poetic or literary
- Dedicated female Chinese name generator
- Showcase gallery and essays
