## About
Healthcare Interview Coach prepares nurses, CNAs, techs and therapists for interviews: pick your role and interview type, add the posting, and get likely questions, why they are asked, how to structure a strong answer, and feedback on your own answer.

## Features
- Likely questions, STAR answer structure and feedback on your answer
- Uses only what you write, no invented facts
- Free, no sign-up, nothing stored
