## About
Upscale Image (upscaleimg.org) is a browser AI image upscaler that enlarges low-resolution photos, recovers detail, and unblurs soft images. Upload JPG/PNG/WebP or batch files, choose 2x to 8x, and preview clearer results for portraits, products, artwork, and old photos.

## Features
- AI upscaling at 2x, 4x, 6x, and 8x
- Modes for standard, portrait, anime or art, and restore
- Batch queue for multiple images
- Before and after comparison preview
- Browser-based workflow with no install
- Freemium credits to start enhancing
