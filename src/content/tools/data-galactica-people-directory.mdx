## About

Browse 98,415,992 people by what they do, country, city and name, each with a permanent Galactica ID (DGP-) and a source card for the record. People can see, claim, hide or ask to remove their own record through Galactica Check. Part of Data Galactica.

## Features

- 98,415,992 People
- Browse by function, country, city and name
- Each with a permanent Galactica ID
