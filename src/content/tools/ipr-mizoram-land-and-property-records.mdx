## About

Mizoram: 11 districts and 828 villages. How to check land records, maps, registration and encumbrance in Mizoram, and the government portals that hold them. DoLR lists no RoR portal for Mizoram; e-ram sells application forms (periodic patta, LSC, transfer, partition) at about Rs 20 each rather than a lookup. DILRMP-MIS nevertheless shows all 640 villages computerised with 331,160 RoRs, a conflict the reports leave unresolved. Deeds run on NGDRS since 2019.

## Features

- Lists every district of the state with its village count
- Explains how to check land records, maps, registration and encumbrance
- Links the government portals that hold the records
