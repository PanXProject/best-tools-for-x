## About
Design Details is a hand-picked library of real website designs, with full-page desktop and mobile captures, individual sections, motion recordings, and fonts and colours measured from the live pages. It is free to browse, and subscribers get new sites in the Weekly Picks email.

## Features
- Full-page desktop and mobile website captures
- Individual website sections for design study
- Motion recordings from real websites
- Fonts and colours measured from live pages
