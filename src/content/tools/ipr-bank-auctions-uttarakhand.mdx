## About

A free India Property Register list of 1,396 bank auction property notices in Uttarakhand, newest first, showing the reserve price, the bank, the district and the listing date, for buyers looking at SARFAESI and bank-owned properties. India Property Register is an independent register of land and property in India built from public government records, with a page for every state, district, tehsil and village. Free to read.
