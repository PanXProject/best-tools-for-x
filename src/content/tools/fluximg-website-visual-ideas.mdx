## About
Fluximg helps website builders explore illustrations and section artwork from prompts or reference images. Its independent multi-model workspace offers options including GPT Image 2, Flux 2 Turbo and Seedream 5.0 Pro. Generate a draft, review its composition and refine the prompt to suit the page message. Free starting credits are advertised; continued use may require paid credits or plans. FLUX 3 is promoted as coming soon, not currently selectable.

## Features
- Prompt-based image creation for website visual concepts
- Reference-based editing for refining image ideas
- Multiple aspect ratios for different layout needs
- Iterative prompt refinement across available models
