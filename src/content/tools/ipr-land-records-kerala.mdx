## About

Guide to land records in Kerala on India Property Register: Thandaper / Basic Tax Receipt, Land information (ReLIS), Land parcel map, how plots are identified, the local terms and where the records are held. Kerala runs ReLIS (login, with an advertised 'Any Land Search') and Ente Bhoomi, an owner-login portal serving maps and land tax only in digitally surveyed villages. DILRMP-MIS shows 100% computerisation (1,666 villages, 2.82 crore RoRs).

## Features

- Explains what the state calls its land records and what each shows
- Shows how plots are identified, from the state down to the village
- Lists the government portals and sources that hold the records
