## About

A directory of parties (people, companies, government bodies and tribunals named as parties) built from Indian court records. Browse by state, district and court, or open a party's page to see its full case list, filterable by court, status and date. Free on the web; part of eCourtsIndia. Information only, not legal advice; check the court record before relying on it.

## Features

- Parties named in Indian court records
- Browse by state, district and court
- Full case list per party, filterable
