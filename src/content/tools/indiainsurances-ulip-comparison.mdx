## About

A free IndiaInsurances table of 271 unit-linked insurance plans (ULIPs) sold in India, comparing the lock-in period, premium allocation charge and policy administration charge from each plan's own documents. IndiaInsurances reads every policy from its own documents and the IRDAI registers, takes no commission and makes no sales calls.

## Features

- Reads each policy from its own documents
- Lets you sort columns or compare two policies
- Takes no commission and makes no sales calls
