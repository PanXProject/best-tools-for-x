## About

8ake is a directory submission service. AI agents submit a new app, SaaS product or AI tool to directories that publish, so it gets seen without manual form filling. It targets 122 verified directories and up to 60 live listings, with live listings within 30 days. Rejections are replaced until the live-listing target is met, and you keep every login.

## Features

- AI agents submit the app to directories that publish
- Up to 60 live listings targeted
- Live listings within 30 days
- Rejections replaced until the live-listing target is met
- You keep the login for every listing
- One payment, no subscription
