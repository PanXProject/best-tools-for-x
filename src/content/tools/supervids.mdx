## About
Independent filmmakers and storytellers have always been limited by budget: locations, actors, equipment and time all cost money. SuperVids AI Drama Generator offers a different starting point for visual storytelling. It is a workspace for AI filmmaking that runs from the first line of a screenplay to the final frame. Bring an idea or an existing script and develop the premise, rewrite dialogue and find the ending through a creative conversation.

## Features
- For each shot, you can set the framing, the camera movement and the action, generate a still, bring it into motion and compare versions until the scene feels right. Image and video versions are kept so you can choose your take, or bring in your own media when you have it. Then make the audience feel the story with dialogue and voiceover, layered with music, sound effects and ambience, using generated audio or your own recordings. Speech can become captions, and SRT or VTT files can be imported or written and timed by hand. Finally, arrange shots, trim pauses and set the rhythm, adding title cards, transitions, captions and audio with the whole film in view. Sign in to save the script, references and media with the project, then export the finished film.
