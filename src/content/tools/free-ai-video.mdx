## About
Turn a text prompt or a reference photo into a short video with Seedance 2.5. A limited daily trial makes a 4-second, 480p clip without sign-up, and no watermark is added.

## Features
- Text prompt to short video
- Animate one reference photo
- Daily free trial, no sign-up needed
- No watermark
- One-time credit packs for 10-second 720p videos (from $4.99)

Network and daily availability limits apply. Facts as of 2026-11-01.
