## About
Turn a text prompt or a reference photo into a short video with Seedance 2.5. A limited daily trial makes a 4-second, 480p clip without sign-up. No watermark is added. Optional one-time credit packs make 10-second, 720p videos; packs start at $4.99. Network and daily availability limits apply.

## Features
- Create short videos from a text prompt with Seedance 2.5
- Animate one reference photo with automatic aspect ratio
- Try a limited daily 4-second, 480p guest generation without sign-up
- Download videos with no watermark added by the site
- Buy one-time credit packs for 10-second, 720p videos
