## About
Cockatoo helps Australian sole traders and small businesses build mobile-friendly websites without coding. Choose an industry template and personalise ready-made sections. Free forever for one page with hosting, HTTPS, a Cockatoo web address, contact form and enquiry inbox. No credit card required. Optional Pro costs A$29/year to connect an existing custom domain and remove branding; domain registration and renewal are separate.

## Features
- Industry templates with ready-made sections
- Mobile-friendly website layouts
- Included hosting and HTTPS
- Free one-page site on a Cockatoo web address
- Contact form with an enquiry inbox
- Optional custom domain connection and branding removal
