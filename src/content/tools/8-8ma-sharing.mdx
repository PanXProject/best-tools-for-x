## About
8码分享 is a Chinese-language platform for creators and small teams to share original or authorized digital resources through purchase links. Available payment channels and service fees depend on the seller’s settings and current order rules. Buyers access the content after payment confirmation.

## Features
- Create purchase links for digital resource pages
- Share files, cloud links, text and card codes
- Track order status and access content after confirmation
- Connect existing applications through the platform API
