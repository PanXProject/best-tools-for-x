## About

Bank Statement Converter is a browser-based tool that turns bank statement PDFs and phone photos into spreadsheet-ready rows. Upload a single statement or a batch of up to 100 files and export the transactions as Excel, CSV or JSON.

## Features

- Converts statement PDFs and photographed statements into structured rows

- Batch processing of up to 100 files in one pass

- Exports as Excel, CSV or JSON to match how the data is consumed

- Separate files, a ZIP archive, or one merged export per period

- Keeps transaction date, description, debit, credit and running balance columns

## Use Cases

- Month-end reconciliation for a bookkeeper or accountant

- Cleaning a season of statements before importing them

- Checking that a statement total matches the sum of its own lines

- Producing a per-account summary without retyping anything

## How to Use

1. Export the statement from online banking where possible, rather than photographing a printout.

2. Upload the file, or a batch of them, and choose the export format.

3. Keep the columns you plan to check against, especially the printed running or closing balance.

4. Compare the last exported row against the printed total before anything downstream uses the data.

## Tips

- Do not sort or tidy the export before verifying it. The check is only meaningful on the file as produced.

- If the total does not match, find the difference first. Every aggregate below it inherits the error.

- Frequent causes of a mismatch: duplicated rows at page boundaries, a description split across two rows, a currency conversion read from the wrong column, and fees listed outside the transaction table.

- Record which statements needed manual entry. That list tells you which sources are worth re-exporting later.
