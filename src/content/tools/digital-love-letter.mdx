## About

Digital Love Letter is a browser-based editor for combining your words with photos and music. Write from scratch or revise an optional AI draft, then preview an opening-envelope experience. The editor can support anniversaries, birthdays, long-distance relationships and everyday notes of appreciation.

## Features

- Compose a personal letter in the browser.
- Start with your own words or revise an optional AI draft.
- Add photos and music to support the message.
- Preview an opening-envelope presentation.

## A practical workflow

Start with one real shared memory and explain why it matters. Replace generic phrases and invented details, choose media that supports the message and preview on a phone with sound off. The words should make sense without relying on decoration.

## Pricing and limits

Writing and preview are free; sharing or delivery may need a separate unlock, so check the current interface before planning delivery. Keep a plain-text copy and avoid sharing private details or other peoples images without permission.
