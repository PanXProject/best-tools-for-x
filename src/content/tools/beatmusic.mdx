## About
BeatMusic is an online AI music generator that turns text prompts, lyrics, or images into original, commercial-ready songs in minutes. It includes an AI Song Generator, AI Rap Generator, AI Background Music, Text to Music, Image to Music, AI Cover Generator, Vocal Remover, AI Lyrics Generator and more. Free to try, no music skills needed.

## Features
- Generate original songs from text prompts, lyrics, or images
- Create AI rap, background music, covers, and lyrics
- Remove vocals and transform images into music
- Make commercial-ready tracks in minutes without music skills
