## About

A free IndiaCos page listing 124 addresses in Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu used by two or more companies, LLPs or GST registrations, busiest first, by district. Each address shows how many companies, LLPs and GST registrations use it, which helps spot registered-office providers, virtual offices and business clusters before you deal with a firm. Free to browse at https://indiacos.com/offices/dadra-and-nagar-haveli-and-daman-and-diu.

## Features

- Lists addresses in one state shared by two or more businesses
- Groups them by district, busiest first
- Links each address to the companies, LLPs and GST registrations using it
