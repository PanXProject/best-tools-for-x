## About
Seed Audio 1.5 on TopMaker gives audio creators control over both performance and the final mix. ByteDance's model generates voices, music and sound scenes from text, audio or video context. Use reference recordings to guide speakers, then refine separate dialogue, ambience, effects and music tracks in your editor.

## Features
- Guide voices with up to six reference recordings
- Direct emotion, pacing, laughter and sighs
- Position dialogue, music and effects with timestamp instructions
- Generate up to six minutes across 30 languages and regional variants
- Continue post-production with separate audio tracks
