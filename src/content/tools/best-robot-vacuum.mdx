## About
Best Robot Vacuum is a free, independent robot vacuum comparison and review site with an AI assistant called Navi. Tell Navi your budget, floor type, pets and preferred brand and it shortlists three matches from 223 tracked models across 17 brands.

## Features
- Navi AI assistant that shortlists three robot vacuums for your home
- 223 Models across 17 brands with structured specs
- Independent review scores
- Side-by-side comparison table
- Buying guides for pet hair, carpet and hard floors
- Cost calculator and smart-home compatibility checker
- Free to use, no signup
