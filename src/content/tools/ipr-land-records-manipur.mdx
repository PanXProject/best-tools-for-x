## About

Guide to land records in Manipur on India Property Register: Jamabandi / RoR, Land tax record, how plots are identified, the local terms and where the records are held. The DoLR link for Manipur is an e-GRAS tax form; the real land records site, Loucha Pathap, takes land tax by patta and dag, shows RoR transaction status and advertises jamabandi download. DILRMP-MIS shows 96.36% of villages computerised (583 of 605). Deeds run on NGDRS since 2018.

## Features

- Explains what the state calls its land records and what each shows
- Shows how plots are identified, from the state down to the village
- Lists the government portals and sources that hold the records
