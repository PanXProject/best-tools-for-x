## About
Praising.ai helps local businesses get more Google and Trustpilot reviews and answer every one. After a sale or visit it emails the customer a review request, with reminders, and it gives you a review page and a QR code for the counter. On paid plans an AI agent drafts a reply to every new review in the tone you choose, and nothing is posted until you approve it.

## Features
- Automatic review requests by email after a sale or visit, with reminders
- A review page and a QR code for your counter
- AI reply drafts for every new review in a warm, professional or apologetic tone (paid plans)
- Nothing is posted until you approve the reply
- Google and Trustpilot reviews in one inbox, with 19 more review sites linked
- A Forever Free plan; paid plans cost $19, $29 or $49 a month, with a 14-day free trial
