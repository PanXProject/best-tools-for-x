## About
ModelSnap is an AI photo studio for apparel sellers. Upload a flat lay, mannequin shot, or product photo, choose a virtual model and scene, then generate on-model images for online listings. A one-time trial is available and ongoing plans use paid credits.

## Features
- Turn flat lays and mannequin shots into on-model photos
- Choose a virtual model and scene
- Make product images for online listings
