## About
Nestack Agent Care is a managed AI agent operations service from Nestack. It operates production AI agents across goals, workflows, tools, LLMs, evaluations, guardrails, human review and business outcomes, so failures are detected early and improvements are verified as conditions change.

## Features
- End-to-end tracing and evaluation runs for agent fleets
- Verified guardrails and monitored tool actions
- Staffed human review and verification loops
- 30-Minute SLA for critical incidents
- Catalogue of 5,814 documented failure modes and 23 industry playbooks
- Department playbooks for finance, HR, customer support, sales, IT and legal
