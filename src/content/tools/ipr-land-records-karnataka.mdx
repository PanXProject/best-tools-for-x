## About

Guide to land records in Karnataka on India Property Register: RTC / Pahani, Mutation Register (MR), Khatha extract, how plots are identified, the local terms and where the records are held. Karnataka's revenue portal offers free RTC and mutation register views, RTC search by survey number, owner or registration number, and paid i-RTC; SSLR serves cadastral maps as PDF and KMZ. DILRMP-MIS shows 99.77% of villages computerised.

## Features

- Explains what the state calls its land records and what each shows
- Shows how plots are identified, from the state down to the village
- Lists the government portals and sources that hold the records
