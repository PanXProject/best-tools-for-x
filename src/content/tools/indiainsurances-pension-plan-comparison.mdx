## About

A free IndiaInsurances table of 106 pension and annuity policies sold in India, comparing annuity options, minimum purchase price, deferment period and an example annuity rate for each policy. IndiaInsurances reads every policy from its own documents and the IRDAI registers, takes no commission and makes no sales calls.

## Features

- Reads each policy from its own documents
- Lets you sort columns or compare two policies
- Takes no commission and makes no sales calls
