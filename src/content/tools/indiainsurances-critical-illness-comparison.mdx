## About

A free IndiaInsurances table of 38 critical illness policies sold in India, comparing the number of illnesses covered, the payout, the survival period and whether multiple claims are allowed, each read from the policy documents. IndiaInsurances reads every policy from its own documents and the IRDAI registers, takes no commission and makes no sales calls.

## Features

- Reads each policy from its own documents
- Lets you sort columns or compare two policies
- Takes no commission and makes no sales calls
