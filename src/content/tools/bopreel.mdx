## About
Bopreel turns one or two photos into 15-second musical meme videos with the original song. Choose Hotel Lobby AI or Oouuh AI, also called What You Want Me To Do AI. Typical processing takes about 10-15 minutes. A video costs $9.99, with one-time bundles and no subscription. Submitted on behalf of Bopreel.

## Features
- Hotel Lobby AI recording-booth video template
- Oouuh AI kitchen duet video template
- Photo inputs matched to the selected template
- Fifteen-second clips with the original song
- One-time video purchases without a subscription
