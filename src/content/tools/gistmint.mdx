## About
GistMint is a free YouTube to transcript tool. Paste any YouTube link and get the full, accurate, timestamped transcript without signing up, then search it, jump to any moment and export it.

## Features
- Full timestamped transcript from any YouTube link
- No sign-up needed for basic use
- Search the whole transcript and jump to any moment
- Pick a subtitle language
- Copy or export as TXT or SRT
