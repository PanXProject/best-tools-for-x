## About
Merlua is a simple salon booking system for nail, lash, brow, waxing, and beauty businesses. It combines a web admin dashboard, customer mobile app, and staff mobile app for managing services, appointments, schedules, deposits, customers, and reminders.

## Features
- Online appointment booking
- Staff selection
- Service catalog and packages
- Deposit payments
- Calendar management
- Staff availability and scheduling
- Time-slot management
- Automatic appointment reminders
- Customer information collection
- Customer mobile app
- Staff mobile app
- Web admin dashboard
