## About
Hum to Search helps you find song candidates when you only remember the melody. Hum a tune, sing a few notes or record audio around you, and the app compares it against its music index to surface matching songs with artists and links to listen. It works in the browser, keeps recent searches, and is useful when a melody is stuck in your head but the title is gone.

## Features
- Identify song candidates by humming a melody
- Record ambient audio and search it
- Match results with artist and track details
- Link out to listen on streaming services
- Keep a history of recent searches
- Run in the browser without installing an app
