## About
ResumeInvited is an online resume builder for job seekers, including students and recent graduates. Choose from professional resume templates, add your experience and skills, and check your layout with a live preview. Download your resume as a PDF or an editable Word document. The current version is free to use, with no sign-up required. Drafts are saved locally in your browser.

## Features
- Professional resume templates with structured sections
- Live preview while editing your resume
- PDF and editable Word downloads
- Drafts saved locally in your browser
