## About
Flying Car AI turns one car or bike photo into a short cinematic stunt clip. Choose a ramp flight or a kickflip, upload a clear side view, and download a silent 16:9 video. Independent tool, not an official Tesla product.

## Features
- Make a ramp-flight video from a single car or bike photo
- Side-view kickflip and head-on kickflip templates
- Preview the reference motion before you generate
- Choose 480p or 720p and pay with credits
- Find finished clips later in My creationsAI video, photo to video, creative, stunts, generative
