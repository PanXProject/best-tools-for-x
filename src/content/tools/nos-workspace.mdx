## About
NOS Workspace keeps documents and HTML pages together. Paste an HTML report or presentation, then click text on the rendered page to edit it. Sign in to save the page and share a public link. Readers can open it without an account; saved changes appear when they reopen that same link.

## Features
- Preview and edit pasted HTML before signing in.
- Keep documents and HTML pages in one workspace.
- Share saved pages with readers who do not have a NOS account.
- Start with three active share links on the free plan; paid plans add capacity.
