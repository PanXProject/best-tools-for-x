## About

A free Data Galactica list of the best-known companies and organisations in Malta, ranked by how much its sources cover them and then by size (not by revenue). The list opens with Air Malta P.L.C., Partit Nazzjonalista, King.com Limited and 4A Games; each entry has a permanent Galactica ID, its status, industry and city, a data grade and the sources behind it.

## Features

- Lists the best-covered organisations in one country
- Gives each a permanent Galactica ID and a grade
- Shows the sources behind every record
