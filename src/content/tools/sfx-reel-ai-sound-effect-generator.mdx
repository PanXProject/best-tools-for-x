## About

SFX Reel is an AI Sound Effect Generator that helps creators generate realistic sound effects from videos and text prompts. Designed for filmmakers, animators, game developers, and AI video creators, it simplifies sound design with Video to SFX and Text to SFX workflows.

## Features

- **Video to SFX:** Generate sound effects from uploaded videos.
- **Text to SFX:** Create custom audio from natural-language prompts.
- **Video-Aware Audio:** Generate sounds that correspond to on-screen actions.
- **Environmental Sounds:** Create footsteps, rain, wind, impacts, and ambient effects.
- **Dialogue Preservation:** Support workflows that retain spoken dialogue.
- **Background Music Removal:** Help remove unwanted background music.
- **Creative Sound Design:** Generate effects for films, animations, games, and social media videos.
- **Browser-Based Workflow:** Create sound effects through an online interface.

## Use Cases

- Add realistic sound effects to AI-generated videos.
- Create custom audio for YouTube Shorts, TikTok, and Reels.
- Generate environmental sounds for games and animations.
- Enhance cinematic scenes with atmospheric sound effects.
- Simplify audio production for video editing projects.
