## About
Ozil Goal AI turns one photo into an AI remake of Mesut Ozil's famous 2016 chip-and-dribble goal. Upload a single photo and you become the scorer. Get a vertical 9:16 MP4 of 5, 10 or 15 seconds for TikTok, Reels or Shorts. No app and no prompt.

## Features
- Upload one photo and become the scorer in your own clothes
- Vertical 9:16 MP4 of 5, 10 or 15 seconds with the trend sound
- Stadium, keeper, defenders and camera stay from the original clip
- One-time credit packs, and failed videos return their credits
