## About
HeyAIBuddy AI Boyfriend lets you chat by text or voice with an AI boyfriend who remembers your past conversations. Pick a public persona or design a private one: his look, voice and personality. Free to start with 100 text messages and 50 voice replies, no card. Safe-for-work, 18+ accounts.
## Features
- Text or voice chat with replies spoken in his own voice
- Long-term memory across conversations
- Public AI personas or a private companion you design
- Send an image and he responds to it
- Safe-for-work, always identified as AI, 18+ accounts only
