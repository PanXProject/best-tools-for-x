## About

Guide to land records in Mizoram on India Property Register: Periodic patta, Land Settlement Certificate (LSC), House-site allotment, how plots are identified, the local terms and where the records are held. DoLR lists no RoR portal for Mizoram; e-ram sells application forms (periodic patta, LSC, transfer, partition) at about Rs 20 each rather than a lookup.

## Features

- Explains what the state calls its land records and what each shows
- Shows how plots are identified, from the state down to the village
- Lists the government portals and sources that hold the records
