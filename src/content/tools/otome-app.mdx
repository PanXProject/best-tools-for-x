## About
Play AI otome romance in your browser. Meet romanceable characters, write what you say or do, and explore relationships at your own pace. Female-lead stories; write your own replies, not only tap choices. Freemium.

## Features
- Play AI otome romance in your browser
- Meet romanceable characters
- Write what you say or do, not only tap fixed choices
- Explore relationships at your own pace
- Create a story from one sentence in Studio
