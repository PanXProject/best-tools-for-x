## About
Healthcare Interview Question Generator is a free AI tool for healthcare hiring managers: nurse managers, clinic and practice managers, home care agencies and HR. Name the role, the setting, the experience level and what matters most: it writes structured interview questions on clinical judgment, safety and escalation, teamwork and fit, each with what a strong answer shows and a follow-up, plus questions to avoid and a scoring tip.

## Features
- Free to use, no sign-up
- Nothing you enter is stored
- Built on Claude by Anthropic
