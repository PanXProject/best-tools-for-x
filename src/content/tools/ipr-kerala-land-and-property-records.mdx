## About

Kerala: 14 districts and 1,524 villages. How to check land records, maps, registration and encumbrance in Kerala, and the government portals that hold them. Kerala runs ReLIS (login, with an advertised 'Any Land Search') and Ente Bhoomi, an owner-login portal serving maps and land tax only in digitally surveyed villages. DILRMP-MIS shows 100% computerisation (1,666 villages, 2.82 crore RoRs). Encumbrance certificates and certified copies are high-volume paid PEARL services.

## Features

- Lists every district of the state with its village count
- Explains how to check land records, maps, registration and encumbrance
- Links the government portals that hold the records
