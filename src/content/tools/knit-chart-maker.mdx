## About
Knit Chart Maker is a free browser tool that turns a photo or a written idea into a knitting chart and shows it in real yarn. It covers colorwork, Fair Isle, lace, cable, and texture, with float and contrast checks. Designs stay in the browser. No sign-up.

## Features
- Turn a photo into a knitting chart in 2 to 12 colors
- Describe a motif and get four charts matched to real yarns
- Restyle the chart being edited, with undo
- Ask for new colorways while the background stays
- Export a PDF pattern, a PNG chart, or a CSV
