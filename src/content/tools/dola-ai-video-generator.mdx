## About
Dola AI is an independent AI video generator that turns text prompts and uploaded photos into short videos in seconds. Create product clips, social posts, and ads without editing skills. Free daily credits to start, watermark-free downloads.

## Features
- Text to video from a plain prompt
- Image to video from a product photo
- Watermark-free high-resolution downloads
- Free daily credits with no card required
- Multiple aspect ratios for Reels and Shorts
