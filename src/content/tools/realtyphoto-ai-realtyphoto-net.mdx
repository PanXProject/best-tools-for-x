## About

RealtyPhoto AI enhances interior and exterior property photos and virtually stages empty rooms. Upload up to 10 JPG, PNG or WebP photos per batch, compare results and download individually or as ZIP. An account and credits are required: eligible verified new accounts receive 3 trial credits, then one-time packs start at $12 for 10 credits. Purchased credits never expire; no subscription. Review all AI changes and disclose staging.

## Features

- Interior and exterior enhancement
- Virtual staging of empty rooms
- Six staging styles
- Up to 10 photos per batch
- Before and after comparison
- Individual or ZIP downloads
- One-time credit packs
