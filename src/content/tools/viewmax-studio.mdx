## About
ViewMax Studio is an AI video generator that turns one prompt into a ready-to-post vertical video with script, voiceover and burned-in captions. Use [viewmax studio](https://viewmaxstudio.net/) to make faceless Shorts, Reels, TikToks and UGC-style ads without a camera or editing skills, with the credit cost shown before every render.

## Features
- ViewMax Agent 2.0 plans the shot list, renders scenes, reads narration and times captions
- Faceless YouTube Shorts and TikTok videos with AI voiceover
- UGC-style product ads with a spoken hook to test several angles
- 60-Second explainers, product clips and long narration cut into scenes
- Six models from 480p to 1080p, vertical or wide
- Credit cost shown up front and automatic refunds on failed renders
- Commercial rights on every plan with no watermark
