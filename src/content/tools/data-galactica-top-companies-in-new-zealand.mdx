## About

A free Data Galactica list of the best-known companies and organisations in New Zealand, ranked by how much its sources cover them and then by size (not by revenue). The list opens with Air New Zealand, Fonterra Co-Operative Group Limited, Reserve Bank of New Zealand and New Zealand Exchange Ltd; each entry has a permanent Galactica ID, its status, industry and city, a data grade and the sources behind it.

## Features

- Lists the best-covered organisations in one country
- Gives each a permanent Galactica ID and a grade
- Shows the sources behind every record
