## About
BT Exam Practice helps candidates prepare for the RBT exam with a free diagnostic, six-domain quizzes and an 85-question mock exam with explanations. Free practice needs no account. An optional one-time $49 Study plan adds domain practice, spaced review, a daily plan and three more mock exams. Not affiliated with BACB or Pearson VUE.

## Features
- Free 85-question RBT mock exam
- Quizzes for all six task-list domains
- Explanations tied to task-list items
- Optional one-time $49 Study plan
- No sign-up for free practice
