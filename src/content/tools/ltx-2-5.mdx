## About
LTX 2.5 is a next-generation open-source AI video generation model designed to help creators, developers, filmmakers, and businesses transform ideas into high-quality videos faster and easier than ever before. By combining advanced generative AI technology with powerful video creation capabilities, LTX 2.5 enables users to turn text prompts, images, and creative concepts into cinematic visual experiences.

## Features
- Text-to-video generation from simple prompts
- Image-to-video conversion for animating still visuals
- Multi-image blending for richer, more controlled scenes
- AI image and video generation in one platform
- Fast rendering and web-based access for quick video creation
