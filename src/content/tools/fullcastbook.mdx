## About
FullCastBook turns a manuscript into an AI audiobook with a full cast: every character gets its own voice alongside a narrator. Upload an EPUB, PDF, DOCX, TXT or Markdown file, or paste a chapter. It detects chapters and speakers, then exports M4B with chapters or MP3. Registration is free; generating audio is paid.

## Features
- Separate AI voice for each character plus a narrator
- Automatic chapter and speaker detection
- Imports EPUB, PDF, DOCX, TXT and Markdown files
- Exports M4B with chapters or MP3
