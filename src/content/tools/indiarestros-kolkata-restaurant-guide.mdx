## About

A free IndiaRestros guide to 39,552 restaurants, cafes and sweet shops in Kolkata, West Bengal, with phone numbers, timings, menus with rupee prices, photos and cuisines, area by area. Filter pure veg, open late, or budget to fine dining. Part of IndiaRestros, a city-by-city restaurant guide for India with a free open API and MCP server.
