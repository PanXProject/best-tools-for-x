## About
Google Maps Scraper is a Chrome extension for lead generation. It captures business name, category, address, phone, website, email, social links, hours, reviews and ratings from Google Maps results, with automatic pagination and duplicate removal. Export to CSV or XLSX. Free plan includes 200 leads; Professional is $29.90/month; one-time option is $69.90.

## Features
- Extracts business name, category, address, coordinates, phone, website, email
- Pulls social links (Instagram, Facebook, X, LinkedIn, YouTube)
- Automatic pagination and duplicate removal
- Exports to CSV or XLSX
