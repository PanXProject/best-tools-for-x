## About

Kling 4.0 is an AI video generation tool for creating videos from text prompts, images, and visual references. It is designed for creators, marketers, and teams who want more control over motion, camera direction, scene style, and visual consistency.

## Features

- Generate videos from text prompts
- Turn still images into dynamic videos
- Create videos from visual references
- Control camera movement and scene direction
- Build cinematic short-form video content
- Create product and marketing videos
- Support character-focused video workflows
- Refine motion and visual consistency with prompts
