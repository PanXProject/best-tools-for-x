## About
iSaveTik is a free browser-based media downloader for public links from TikTok and more than 4,000 supported platforms. It helps users save videos, audio, covers, and slideshows for content they own or have permission to download.

## Features
- Download watermark-free HD videos when the source provides them
- Save audio, covers, slideshows, and video files
- Process single links or batches directly in the browser
- Use the service without installing an app or creating an account
- Support public links from TikTok and thousands of other platforms
