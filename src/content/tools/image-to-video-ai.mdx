## About
Image To Video AI is a browser-based workspace for creating videos from source images, text prompts, or first and last keyframes. Users can compare multiple AI video models in one bilingual interface.

## Features
- Generate short videos from still images
- Create video drafts from text prompts
- Build motion with first and last keyframes
- Compare multiple AI video models
- Start with a free plan in the browser
