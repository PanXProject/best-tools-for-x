## About
Tesla Roadster AI is an independent Tesla Roadster AI video maker. Upload one photo of a car, a toy car or any vehicle and get a 15-second AI video of it running the Roadster ramp flip. Two more templates put you or your pet at the wheel. No app, no prompt; failed videos are refunded.

## Features
- Ramp flip video from one vehicle photo in 15 seconds
- Traffic escape and police stop templates
- Videos have no watermark
- Failed videos are refunded
