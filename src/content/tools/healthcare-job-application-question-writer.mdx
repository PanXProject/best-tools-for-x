## About
Healthcare Job Application Question Writer is a free AI tool for anyone applying to US healthcare jobs. Hospital and clinic applications ask written questions before anyone interviews you (why this employer, why you are leaving, a time you handled a hard patient). Paste the questions, describe your work in your own words and pick a length: it writes an answer to each, using only your facts and the posting's words, and marks what to fill in.

## Features
- Free to use, no sign-up
- Nothing you enter is stored
- Built on Claude by Anthropic
