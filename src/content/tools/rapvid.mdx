## About
RapVid is an AI rap video maker that turns one photo into a lip-synced rap performance. Pick a viral rap effect, choose 5, 10 or 15 seconds, and generate a shareable rap music video with an original beat in minutes. No camera, no studio and no editing skills needed. Post it to TikTok, Reels or Shorts.

## Features
- Turn one photo into a lip-synced rap
- Pick from viral rap effects
- Choose 5, 10 or 15 second videos
- Original beat for every video
