## About
RapVid is an AI rap video maker that turns one photo into a lip-synced rap performance with an original beat. No camera, no studio and no editing skills needed. Post it to TikTok, Reels or Shorts.

## Features
- Turn one photo into a lip-synced rap video
- Pick a viral rap effect
- Choose 5, 10 or 15 seconds
- Original beat generated in minutes
