## About

Guide to land records in Meghalaya on India Property Register: Non-forest land certificate, how plots are identified, the local terms and where the records are held. DoLR lists no RoR portal for Meghalaya, and its DILRMP district report shows 0 RoRs and 'RoR available online for download = No' in every district. Community land under the Sixth Schedule is the working system. The NGDRS directory lists a URL but the national grid marks it data awaited.

## Features

- Explains what the state calls its land records and what each shows
- Shows how plots are identified, from the state down to the village
- Lists the government portals and sources that hold the records
