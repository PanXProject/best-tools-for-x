## About
Healthcare Offer Negotiation Email Writer helps nurses, CNAs, techs and therapists ask for a better offer. Enter the offer and why your ask is fair, and it writes a subject line and a collaborative email to the recruiter, plus what to check before sending. It never invents an offer or number.

## Features
- Polite, specific negotiation email for pay, bonus, shift or start date
- Uses only what you write, no invented facts
- Free, no sign-up, nothing stored
