## About
Angry Dance Filter is an AI web tool that turns a single photo into a vertical video of the subject doing the viral TikTok angry dance. It runs in the browser.

## Features
- Upload one photo and the tool generates the clip
- Free watermarked preview shows the result before you pay
- Real sample clips, photo guidelines and troubleshooting tips
- Comparison with TikTok and CapCut filters
