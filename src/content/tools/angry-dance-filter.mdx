## About
Upload one photo and the Angry Dance Filter turns it into a 15-second angry dance video. You get a free watermarked preview, then optional one-time video packs. It runs in the browser.

## Features
- One photo in, angry dance video out (15s samples)
- Free watermarked preview
- Sample clips and photo rules shown up front
- My videos page to track generation status
- No app install (web)
