## About
Healthcare Cover Letter Generator writes a short, specific cover letter for any US healthcare job. Pick your role (RN, LPN, CNA, tech, therapist), add your experience and the posting, and it ties your background to what the employer asks for. Anything it cannot know is left in [brackets].

## Features
- Role-aware for nurses, CNAs, techs and therapists
- Uses only what you write, no invented facts
- Free, no sign-up, nothing stored
