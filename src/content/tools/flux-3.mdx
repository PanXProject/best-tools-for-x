## About
FLUX 3 is an independent AI image and video workspace for creators, marketers, and small teams. Create visual concepts and short clips from text prompts or reference images, with controls for aspect ratio, style, resolution, and duration. Launched October 15, 2026. Not affiliated with Black Forest Labs.

## Features
- Text-to-image generation from written prompts
- Reference-image remixing for visual variations
- Text-to-video generation for short clips
- Image-to-video creation from still images
- Adjustable aspect ratio, style, resolution, and duration
