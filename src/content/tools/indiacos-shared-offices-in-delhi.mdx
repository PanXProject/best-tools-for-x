## About

A free IndiaCos list of 48,361 addresses in Delhi used by two or more companies, LLPs or GST registrations as a registered office or place of business, busiest first and grouped by district. IndiaCos is a free directory of Indian companies, LLPs and GST registrations built from public MCA, GST, RBI, SEBI and IBBI records.

## Features

- Built from public MCA, GST, RBI, SEBI and IBBI records
- Free to browse without a login
- Links each entry to the business record
