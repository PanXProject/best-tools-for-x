## About

RBI-registered NBFC Type I (no public funds, no customer interface) in India. 150 RBI-registered NBFC Type I (no public funds, no customer interface) from the Reserve Bank of India's list, with type, layer and the company record. Free to browse at https://indiacos.com/nbfc/nbfc-type-i-no-public-funds-no-customer-interface. - Lists RBI-registered companies of one NBFC type
- Shows the type and layer from the RBI list
- Links each entry to its company record
