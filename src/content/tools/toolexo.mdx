## About
ToolExo is a free, no-signup hub of online calculators and everyday utility tools for students, small-business owners, marketers, developers, and anyone who needs a quick calculation or file conversion. Every tool runs locally with a privacy-first, ad-free experience.

## Features
- Free online calculators for percentages, math, business, and finance
- SEO and text utilities including word count and metadata checks
- PDF and image tools for merge, compress, resize, and metadata removal
- Developer helpers and everyday browser workflows
- No signup, no ads, privacy-first local computation
