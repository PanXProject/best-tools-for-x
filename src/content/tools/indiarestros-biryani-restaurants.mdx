## About

A free IndiaRestros page listing 1,37,945 biryani places in India, most in Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Delhi and Mumbai. Of these, 1,25,683 list a phone number (90%), 1,06,908 have timings, 58,795 have a menu on file and 70,451 have photos. Browse by city from Bengaluru and Hyderabad to Lucknow and Kolkata. IndiaRestros is a free, open restaurant guide with no paid rankings and no commissions.
