## About

Guide to land records in Sikkim on India Property Register: Parcha / RoR, how plots are identified, the local terms and where the records are held. Sikkim's ILRMS is an application system that issues Parcha/RoR after a request, not a public search. DILRMP-MIS shows 83.25% of villages computerised (348 of 418). Sikkim has a blank cell on the NGDRS registration directory. Register coverage: 452 villages.

## Features

- Explains what the state calls its land records and what each shows
- Shows how plots are identified, from the state down to the village
- Lists the government portals and sources that hold the records
