## About
AION 2 Pets is a free companion for AION 2 players who collect pets. Browse the pet atlas with stats and sources, check regional spawn maps, and track which pets you have collected; your progress stays in your browser. Fan-made reference site; not a playable game and not affiliated with NCSOFT.

## Features
- Pet atlas with stats and sources
- Regional spawn maps
- Collection tracker saved in your browser
- Map and tracker on one screen
- Free, no account
