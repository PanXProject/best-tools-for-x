## About
A free browser extension that puts CareJobs.Now's healthcare job search one click away. Type the job and, if you like, a city or state (night shift RN, travel CNA in Phoenix), or type cj and a space in the address bar, or select a job title on any page and right-click to search it.

## Features
- Popular searches: registered nurse, CNA, LPN, medical assistant, travel nurse, therapists, techs
- Quick links to pay by job, new grad nurse jobs, travel nurse pay and job alerts
- Works in Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Brave and Opera (Manifest V3)
- No account and no tracking. Only permissions: the right-click menu and storage for the last search, kept on the device
- Free and open source (MIT): https://github.com/yumaheymans/carejobs-browser-extension
