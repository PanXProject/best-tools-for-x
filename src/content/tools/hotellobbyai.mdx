## About
Hotel Lobby AI is an AI video generator that recreates Quavo and Takeoff's viral orange-booth HOTEL LOBBY performance from your photos. Upload one clear portrait or two for a duet. Faces map onto the hanging-mic scene with lip-sync and hip-hop gestures. Choose 6 or 15 seconds and download a vertical 9:16 MP4 for TikTok, Reels, or Shorts. No prompts. First Fast Preview is free. Works for friends, couples, pets, and cartoon faces when photos are clear and consented.

## Features
- Upload one or two photos and get a vertical Hotel Lobby rap video
- Lip-sync and hip-hop gestures mapped onto the hanging-mic scene
- Choose 6 or 15 seconds and download a 9:16 MP4
- First Fast Preview is free with an account at 480p for 6 seconds
- Ready to post on TikTok, Reels, and YouTube Shorts with no editing
