## About

Assam: 35 districts and 16,382 villages. How to check land records, maps, registration and encumbrance in Assam, and the government portals that hold them. Dharitree shows uncertified jamabandi after choosing a district and village; certified copies are an RTPS service and missing records go to the circle office. DILRMP-MIS shows 90.13% of villages computerised (21,186 of 23,507), and Assam Bhunaksha publishes dag maps with ULPIN.

## Features

- Lists every district of the state with its village count
- Explains how to check land records, maps, registration and encumbrance
- Links the government portals that hold the records
