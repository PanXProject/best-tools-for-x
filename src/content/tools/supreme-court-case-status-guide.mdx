## About

A free eCourtsIndia guide to checking Supreme Court of India case status by diary number, case number, CNR number or party name on sci.gov.in or eCourtsIndia, and to reading Supreme Court orders and next hearing dates. Part of eCourtsIndia, which lets lawyers, litigants and researchers search Indian court cases by CNR, party, advocate or judge, with daily cause lists. Not legal advice.

## Features

- Explains search by diary number, case number or CNR
- Covers sci.gov.in and eCourtsIndia
- Explains how to read orders and next dates
