## About
Yap is voice dictation for Mac and Windows. Hold a shortcut, speak, and let go: Yap turns your speech into clean, punctuated text and types it into the app you are using. Speech recognition runs on your computer, so recordings are never uploaded. Free for 3 days with no account or card, then USD 6.90/month or USD 69/year.

## Features
- Types into any app, from email and docs to chat and terminals
- Runs on your Mac or PC, audio is never uploaded
- Works offline
- 25 Languages detected automatically
- Searchable local history
