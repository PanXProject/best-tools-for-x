## About

Bekadoo is an immersive focus space for work, study, reading, and creative thinking. It combines calming digital environments, ambient soundscapes, and a flexible Pomodoro timer to help you get into the right state of mind. Instead of just tracking time, Bekadoo makes focus feel like a place you actually want to return to.

## Features

- Immersive focus environments
- Ambient sounds for deeper concentration
- Flexible Pomodoro timer
- Focus sessions from 5 to 60 minutes
- Mix sounds like rain, ocean, forest, café, and more
- Add your own YouTube or Spotify audio
- Save and revisit your favorite focus spaces
- Designed for work, study, reading, and creative thinking
