## About
Mawthoq Post (موثوق بوست) is an Arabic and English sponsored publishing marketplace that connects advertisers with 28,000+ websites. Browse sites by niche, language, price and metrics, upload your article or use Mawthoq Writer, and track every order until it is published on a live link. Publishers list their sites, set their own prices and manage incoming orders.

## Features
- 28,000+ Websites in Arabic and English with prices and metrics
- Filters by niche, language, price and domain authority
- Instant campaign proposals and cart PDF quotes
- Mawthoq Writer for article writing
- Wallet top-ups with PayPal and local payment methods
- Order tracking until the article is live
- Publisher dashboard to list sites and manage orders
