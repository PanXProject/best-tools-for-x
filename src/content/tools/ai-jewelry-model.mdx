## About
AI Jewelry Model helps shoppers compare rings and jewelry sellers prepare product visuals. Upload a ring image and a hand photo for a virtual try-on, or use the photo studio to make on-model jewelry images from product photos.

## Features
- Preview a ring on a hand photo
- Compare ring styles before buying
- Create on-model jewelry images for a product listing
