## About
camera.wedding gives couples one QR code that wedding guests scan to upload their photos straight into a shared private album. Guests need no app and no login, and nobody has to chase people for pictures after the wedding. The couple can view and download everything afterwards. Standard ($49 one-time) includes 2 GB, 1 month of uploads and 1 year of hosting. Premium ($69 one-time) adds video, 5 GB, 3 months of uploads, 3 years of hosting and sub-albums. No subscription.

## Features
- One QR code collects wedding guests' photos in one private shared album.
- Guests can upload without an app or login.
- Couples can view and download everything afterwards.
- Standard is $49 one-time and includes 2 GB, 1 month of uploads, and 1 year of hosting.
- Premium is $69 one-time and adds video, 5 GB, 3 months of uploads, 3 years of hosting, and sub-albums.
- No subscription.
