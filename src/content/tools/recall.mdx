## About
Recall is an AI knowledge base and second , available as a web app, mobile app, and browser extension that saves, summarizes, and lets you chat with articles, PDFs,  videos, and podcasts. It links related ideas in a knowledge graph and uses spaced-repetition quizzes to make what you read stick. Used by 700,000+ people.

## Features
- Save, organize, and resurface what they read, watch, and learn
- Summarize content automatically with AI
- Connect ideas across their entire library
- Chat with everything they've saved
- Switch between frontier AI models mid-conversation. , GPT, Gemini, Grok, and DeepSeek, all in one place
