## About
MT Video is a web app for motion transfer AI video. Add a photo of the character you want to appear and a video that supplies the movement, then generate a new clip. It is for people who want to animate a portrait or recast a prepared trend scene.

## Features
- Motion transfer from a character photo and a reference clip
- Prepared trend templates with preview and Recreate
- Upload your own motion clip instead of a template
- Credit estimate shown before generating
- My creations history for playback and download
