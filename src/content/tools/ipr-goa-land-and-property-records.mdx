## About

Goa: 2 districts and 361 villages. How to check land records, maps, registration and encumbrance in Goa, and the government portals that hold them. DSLR shows Form I & XIV and Form D by subdivision for information only; a signed copy is a paid two-day Goa Online service from the Mamlatdar. DILRMP-MIS shows 100% computerisation (426 villages). NAKSHA URPRO cards were in progress in Cuncolim, Margao and Panaji in July 2026.

## Features

- Lists every district of the state with its village count
- Explains how to check land records, maps, registration and encumbrance
- Links the government portals that hold the records
