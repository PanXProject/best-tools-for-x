## About
Free wedding hashtag generator from ScanIt Events. Enter both partners' first names, an optional shared last name and year, pick a tone — classic, funny, punny or modern — and get a set of hashtag ideas to choose from. Tap any one to copy it. No signup, no email, no limit on how many times you generate; the hashtags are built in your browser and nothing you type is sent to a server.

## Features
- Classic, funny, punny or modern tone
- Uses both first names, optional last name and year
- Tap any hashtag to copy it
- No signup and no limit on generations
- Built in the browser — nothing is sent to a server
