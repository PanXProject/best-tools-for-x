## About
Raindance Maker is a Raindance AI video generator. Upload two photos and it recasts the viral two-person seaside-pier Raindance performance: photo 1 replaces the person seated at the start, photo 2 the one who walks in and takes the close-ups. Pick 5, 10, 15 or 30 seconds, a Seedance model and a resolution, and download a 4:3 MP4 with the reference soundtrack — no filming, editing or prompt writing.

## Features
- Two-photo Raindance pier video
- 5, 10, 15 Or 30 second clips, Seedance models
- 4:3 MP4 download with the reference soundtrack
- Plans from $9/month, one-time packs from $5; 5 free credits for new accounts
