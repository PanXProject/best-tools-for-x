## About
What is imgs-ai? An AI image and video workspace for creators using the browser, CLI and Agent Skills. These entry points share one account and credit balance.

## Features
- Create images from text and edit reference photos.
- Generate videos from text or images.
- Review task history and download completed results.

## Tips
Describe the subject, lighting and composition. For edits, say what should stay unchanged. Check the credit quote before generating.

## FAQ
Can I use it free? Daily manual check-in awards 1 free credit; new accounts start at zero.
Is a subscription required? No. Separate credit packs are available and memberships are optional.
Do tasks cost the same? No. The model, resolution and video duration affect the cost.
