## About
TranscribeText is a free AI transcription tool that converts audio and video into accurate text with timestamps, captions, and subtitles. It supports browser-based transcription, TXT and SRT exports, speaker labels, and subtitle translation for creators, students, researchers, and teams.

## Features
- Transcribe audio and video with AI-generated timestamps
- Export transcripts as TXT or SRT files
- Translate subtitles and identify speakers
