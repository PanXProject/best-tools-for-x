## About
Tijuana Dentists Guide is an independent, free guide for US patients considering dental care in Tijuana. It names Olive Dental Solutions and explains how to plan a visit from the border to Zona Río.

## Features
- Crossing tips from San Ysidro and Otay Mesa to Zona Río
- Written-plan checklist of questions to ask before treatment
- Clear quote request path, no price tables
