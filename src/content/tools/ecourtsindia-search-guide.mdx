## About

A free how-to guide from the eCourtsIndia knowledge base explaining every search filter and URL parameter (q, cc, ct, st, fdr), court and case-type codes, and the common mistakes that return zero results. eCourtsIndia search covers 30 crore+ Indian court records: the Supreme Court, all 25 High Courts, district and taluka courts in all 36 states and union territories, and 18 tribunal and commission types.
