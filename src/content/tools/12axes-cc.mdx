## About
12Axes.cc is a political ideology quiz for exploring your views across 12 dimensions. It presents political viewpoints in a multidimensional format for personal reflection and comparison. The results are for self-reflection rather than scientific diagnosis.

## Features
- Explore political beliefs across 12 dimensions
- Reflect on political viewpoints in a multidimensional format
- Compare your views with ideological profiles
