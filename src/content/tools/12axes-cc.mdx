## About
12Axes.cc is a free political self-assessment platform that helps you explore your views across 12 dimensions, rather than placing you on a single left–right spectrum. Choose a 36-, 60-, or 240-question test, explore political ideologies and public figures, and compare or share your results.

## Features
- Explore your political views across 12 dimensions
- Choose a 36-, 60-, or 240-question test
- Explore political ideologies and public figures
- Compare or share your results
- Use the remote MCP server with compatible AI assistants to guide questionnaires and score answers
- Look up profiles of ideologies, people, countries, political parties, and books through MCP
- Connect to MCP with no account or API key required
