## About

Sprite Maker is a browser-based [sprite ai](https://spritemaker.app/) workspace for exploring character concepts and refining their pixels. It combines AI generation with local editing and transparent PNG exports, helping creators separate a generated draft from the asset they decide to use.

## Features

- Generate character concepts as starting points for human review.
- Explore direction views and compare proportions across them.
- Create NPC sets while checking each character for consistency.
- Refine pixels locally instead of regenerating every small correction.
- Export transparent PNGs for the next stage of a project.
- Use free local editing and exports, with one free AI generation per account described on the site.

## Practical notes

Review silhouettes at the intended display size and compare exports against real backgrounds. Direction views are not automatically animation cycles or engine-ready packages. Additional AI generation uses paid credits, subscriptions or one-time packs; check current allowances and terms.
