## About
Healthcare Job Follow-Up Email Writer is a free AI tool for healthcare job seekers who are waiting: after applying, after an interview, after being turned down, while an offer or credentialing is pending. Pick where things stand, name the job and the person you are writing to, add anything new, and it writes a short, polite email with a subject line and when to send it.

## Features
- Free to use, no sign-up
- Nothing you enter is stored
- Built on Claude by Anthropic
