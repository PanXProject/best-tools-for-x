## About
Photo Tea is a browser-based AI photo editor for making changes to a source image with a written prompt. It also offers image generation and creative variations. Use it when you want to explore a different look or refine a photo without a manual editing workflow.

## Features
- Prompt-based photo editing
- AI image generation
- Creative image variations
