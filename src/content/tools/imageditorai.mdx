## About
ImagEditorAI is a free online AI image editor: upload a photo, describe the change in plain language, and edit without layers or sign-up. Free daily credits, no watermark.
## Features
- Remove or replace backgrounds in one prompt
- Remove unwanted people or objects
- Restore and enhance old photos
- Anime, 3D cartoon and comic restyles
- Expand and reframe images for new formats
