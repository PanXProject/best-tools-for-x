## About

A free IndiaInsurances table of 72 home insurance policies: building cover, contents cover, burglary and theft, and rent or alternative accommodation side by side, read from each policy's own documents. IndiaInsurances reads every policy from its own documents and the IRDAI registers, takes no commission and makes no sales calls.

## Features

- Reads each policy from its own documents
- Lets you sort columns or compare two policies
- Takes no commission and makes no sales calls
