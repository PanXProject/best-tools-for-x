## About
A CSOP calculator for UK employees: your grant's value against the £60,000 limit, the gain on exercise, and whether it falls 3 to 10 years after grant.

## Features
- A CSOP calculator for UK employees
- Your grant's value against the £60,000 limit
- The gain on exercise
- Whether it falls 3 to 10 years after grant
- A Company Share Option Plan grant checked against the £60,000 limit and the 3 to 10 year exercise window
- From ITEPA 2003 and GOV
