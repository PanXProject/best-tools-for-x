## About

What each Indian state calls its land records (khatauni, 7/12, RTC, patta, jamabandi, khatian), what they show and what the local terms mean. India Property Register has one guide per state and union territory (36), with the local record names, the admin hierarchy down to the village, how a plot is identified and the government portals that hold the records.

## Features

- Explains what the state calls its land records and what each shows
- Shows how plots are identified, from the state down to the village
- Lists the government portals and sources that hold the records
