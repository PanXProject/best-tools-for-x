## About
Genjutsu AI is a web app that turns a reference clip into a new video. Upload a 4–30s video or pick a community template, add up to 20 reference images, and it recreates the original motion, camera moves and timing through motion transfer, with output from 480p to 1080p. No subscription: one-time credit packs that never expire, and new users get 50 free credits.

## Features
- Motion transfer from any 4–30 second reference clip
- Up to 20 reference images for face, outfit, product and scene
- Output resolution from 480p to 1080p
- One-time credit packs that never expire, no subscription
- Failed generations are refunded automatically
- Exact credit cost shown before you generate
