## About

Youth4work Prep page for the AIIMS (medical entrance): a question bank of 7,854 practice questions and 5 full mock tests in the exam pattern (200 questions, 200 marks, 210 minutes), already attempted by 44,167 test takers, with a score and rank against others after each test.
