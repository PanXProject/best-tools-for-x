## About
Genogram Maker is a browser-based editor for turning supplied family notes into a relationship diagram. Add people and connections, keep contextual notes, use auto layout and optionally draft with AI. It is a discussion and teaching aid, not a diagnostic system.

## Features
- Create relationship diagrams from supplied family notes.
- Add and edit people within a project.
- Represent relationship connections and emotional bonds.
- Keep contextual notes beside the map.
- Arrange a diagram with automatic layout.
- Generate optional AI drafts for human review and correction.
- Export PNG from the free plan.
- Access additional document and editable-data formats on the professional plan.

## Practical notes
The current free plan describes 2 projects, up to 50 people per project, 5 monthly AI credits after sign-in, and PNG export. AI creation or updates use 1–5 credits. Professional access adds PDF, SVG, GEDCOM and editable JSON downloads. Check current plans before relying on allowances.
