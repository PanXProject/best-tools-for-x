## About

Guide to land records in Rajasthan on India Property Register: Jamabandi, Girdawari, Namantaran, how plots are identified, the local terms and where the records are held. Apna Khata gives free jamabandi copies of agricultural land as general information only; certified copies come from a prescribed kiosk and some mutations are not updated immediately.

## Features

- Explains what the state calls its land records and what each shows
- Shows how plots are identified, from the state down to the village
- Lists the government portals and sources that hold the records
