## About
Y2Mate at azk.com.pl is a free browser tool for converting one YouTube video URL at a time to MP3 audio or MP4 video. It works on desktop and mobile browsers without registration, an app, or an extension. It does not support playlist or channel conversion and is independent of other Y2Mate websites.

## Features
- Convert an individual YouTube video URL to MP3 audio or MP4 video
- Use directly in desktop and mobile web browsers
- Download without registration, an app, or a browser extension
