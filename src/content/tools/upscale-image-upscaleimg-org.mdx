## About
Upscale Image is an online image upscaling and enhancement workspace for portraits, product photos, artwork, and older photographs. Users can process individual images or batches and compare results before downloading.

## Features
- Enlarge images with selectable scaling levels
- Enhance photos and artwork online
- Process multiple images in a batch
- Compare original and enhanced images
- Upload JPG, PNG, and WebP images
