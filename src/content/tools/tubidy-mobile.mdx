## About

Tubidy.Mobile is a music discovery site with free in-browser audio tools: cut and join clips, convert MP3 and WAV, edit MP3 tags and record your voice. Everything runs locally in the browser, so files stay on your device - nothing is uploaded. No signup and no cost: open the site, drop in an audio file, and download the result.

## Features

- Cut and join audio clips in the browser
- Convert between MP3 and WAV
- Edit MP3 tags (title, artist, album, artwork)
- Record your voice and save it as an audio file
- All processing on-device: files never leave your computer
- Free, no account required
