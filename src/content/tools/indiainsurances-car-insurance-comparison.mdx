## About

A free IndiaInsurances table of 75 car insurance policies sold in India, comparing insured declared value (IDV) rules, depreciation on parts, no-claim bonus and the compulsory deductible, so car owners can see the clauses before they renew. IndiaInsurances reads every policy from its own documents and the IRDAI registers, takes no commission and makes no sales calls.

## Features

- Reads each policy from its own documents
- Lets you sort columns or compare two policies
- Takes no commission and makes no sales calls
