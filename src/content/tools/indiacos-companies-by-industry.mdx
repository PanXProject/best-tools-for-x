## About

Every Indian company tells the Ministry of Corporate Affairs its main activity. IndiaCos groups companies by industry and specific activity: 7,40,057 companies incorporated from February 2023 under NIC 2008 (87 industries) and 24,44,585 earlier companies under NIC 2004, kept apart so the codes are not mixed. Free to browse; part of IndiaCos.

## Features

- Companies grouped by main activity
- NIC 2008 (from Feb 2023) and NIC 2004 kept apart
- Browse by industry and state
