## About
Choicer Voicer is a free browser dubbing game: pick a ready-made Dub Pack, record each line with your microphone, replay or retake any take, preview the full scene and download the finished MP4. No account or download is needed to start.

## Features
- Free browser dubbing game with ready-made Dub Packs
- Line-by-line recording with replay and retake
- Preview the full scene and download the finished MP4
- Turn your own MP4 (up to 1 minute) into a dubbing project
- Private multiplayer dubbing rooms for up to five people
- Mimic Party voice-imitation party game for 3-5 players
