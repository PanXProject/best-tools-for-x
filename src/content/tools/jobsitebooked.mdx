## About
JobsiteBooked makes the paperwork side of the job easy. Estimates go out with a deposit, and JobsiteBooked texts the client if they go quiet. The final invoice takes the deposit off and comes with a pay link. Photos, hours and receipts land on each job, so you see what every job made. Built for owner-run contractors and home-service crews.

## Features
- Online booking: customers book an assessment or fixed-price service
- Request form: customers send photos and preferred times first
- Estimate options: up to three choices, plus add-ons
- E-signature and deposits: customer signs by link, pays a deposit
- Estimate follow-ups: automatic follow-ups at 2 and 5 days
- Drag-and-drop schedule: day and week views, map in route order
- Recurring jobs: repeat visits, several crew members per visit
- Crew app: Android app, or crew page in any browser
- On-my-way texts: crew sends the customer an ETA text
- Job photos: before, during and after photos, tagged per visit
- Time tracking: clock in and out per visit, timesheets
- Invoicing: invoices from jobs or visits, batch or monthly
- Card payments: pay-by-card link sent by text or email
- Shared inbox: texts, email, web chat, Facebook and Instagram messages
- Review requests: automatic request by email, then text
