## About
Contador de Palabras is a free Spanish writing and text analysis tool that runs in the browser. As you type or paste, it counts words and characters and reports sentence length, readability, keyword density and text rhythm, so Spanish writers can tighten drafts without switching apps. No account or installation is needed.

## Features
- Live word and character counts while you edit
- Sentence and paragraph breakdowns for Spanish text
- Readability and rhythm signals for clearer drafts
- Keyword density checks for repeated terms
- Runs entirely in the browser with no sign-up
