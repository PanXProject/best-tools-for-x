## About

I added a photo restore tool to DreamMMer after a friend asked me to fix a stack of creased family prints. You upload a scan or even a phone photo of the print, and the tool repairs scratches, stains and fading, sharpens faces and can colorize black-and-white shots with natural tones. It usually takes about a minute per photo, and if the result is off you can ask the built-in agent for changes in plain words instead of starting again.

## Features

- Repairs creases, scratches, stains and faded areas on old prints
- Sharpens blurry faces while keeping the person recognizable
- Optional colorization for black-and-white photos
- Works from a flatbed scan or a phone photo of the print
- Chat with the agent to adjust the result after the first pass
