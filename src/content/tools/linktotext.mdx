## About
LinkToText turns a public Instagram Reel or video URL into a clean searchable transcript you can scan, quote, caption, and reuse. No extension or download is required.

## Features
- Paste a public Reel link and get a clean transcript
- Whisper speech recognition with AI cleanup
- Draft captions plus SRT and VTT subtitle text
- Summarize, translate, and reuse the transcript
