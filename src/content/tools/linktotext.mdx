## About
LinkToText is a free Instagram transcript generator. Paste a public Reel or video link and turn it into clean searchable text with Whisper speech recognition and AI cleanup, ready for captions, quotes, and subtitles.

## Features
- Paste a public Instagram Reel URL and get a clean transcript
- Whisper speech recognition with AI cleanup
- Export-ready text for captions, quotes, and subtitles
- Free to use with no extension or download required
