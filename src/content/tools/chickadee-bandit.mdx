## About
Chickadee Bandit is a private, all-in-one family organizer: an encrypted hub with 100+ apps for calendars, chores, co-parenting and caregiving, at one price per household. Each household gets its own encrypted database, shared calendar, messages and 100+ apps for chores, health, money, co-parenting and more. Developers and AI assistants can build custom apps from a simple manifest while the platform handles auth, permissions, encryption, notifications and sync.

## Features
- Private encrypted database for every household
- Shared family calendar and messages
- 100+ Apps for chores, health, money and co-parenting
- One simple price per household
- Custom apps built from a simple manifest
