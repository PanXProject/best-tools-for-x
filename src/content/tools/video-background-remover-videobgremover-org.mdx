## About
Video Background Remover is a browser-based tool for removing backgrounds from uploaded videos. It helps users prepare presentation clips, product footage, and social content for further editing without a physical green screen.

## Features
- Remove backgrounds from uploaded videos
- Separate video subjects from their surroundings
- Upload supported formats including MP4 and MOV
- Download processed videos for further editing
