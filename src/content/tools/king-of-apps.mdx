## About
King of Apps is a public leaderboard where apps are ranked only by how much their makers paid. No algorithm, no voting, no editor: pay to climb, top up to defend, and every click goes straight to the app. A new listing starts at $4 as a one-time payment with no expiry; to take a rank you pay $1 more than the current holder. Category pages filter the same global board, and the board is published as Markdown, JSON and llms.txt for AI agents.

## Features
- Public leaderboard ranked only by amount paid
- One-time payment listings with no expiry
- Category pages filtering the same global board
- Board published as Markdown, JSON and llms.txt
