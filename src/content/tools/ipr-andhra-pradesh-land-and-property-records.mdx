## About

Andhra Pradesh: 26 districts and 14,563 villages. How to check land records, maps, registration and encumbrance in Andhra Pradesh, and the government portals. Mee Bhoomi lists Adangal, 1B, survey number, LP map and e-passbook, but its form did not render on 11 Oct 2026. DILRMP-MIS shows 92.40% of villages computerised (16,267 of 17,605). Deeds run on the state CARD system; AP repealed its 2023 Land Titling Act in August 2024.

## Features

- Lists every district of the state with its village count
- Explains how to check land records, maps, registration and encumbrance
