## About

Guide to land records in Arunachal Pradesh on India Property Register: Land Holding Certificate (LHC), how plots are identified, the local terms and where the records are held. DoLR lists no RoR portal for Arunachal Pradesh and DILRMP-MIS shows 0 RoRs and 0% computerisation over 1,412 villages. The LISA portal (launched 3 Feb 2025) handles land allotment, revenue payment and LHC verification only. Deeds moved to NGDRS in February 2025. Register coverage: 5,582 villages.

## Features

- Explains what the state calls its land records and what each shows
- Shows how plots are identified, from the state down to the village
- Lists the government portals and sources that hold the records
