## About
Coldleads turns Google Maps searches into call-ready B2B lead lists: phone, website and rating, deduplicated and sorted, with call status and callbacks. Built for agencies and freelancers doing lead generation and cold calling of local businesses in Germany, Austria and Switzerland. An alternative to Outscraper, PhantomBuster and Apollo.io for local business leads.

## Features
- Grid search on Google Maps by industry, city and radius
- One deduplicated lead list per workspace; re-found businesses cost no credits
- Phone, website, category and Google rating for each business
- Sales filters: no website, mobile numbers only, rating, call status
- Lead cards with call script, status, callbacks and appointments
