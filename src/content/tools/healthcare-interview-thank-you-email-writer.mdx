## About

Healthcare Interview Thank-You Email Writer helps nurses, CNAs, techs and therapists follow up after an interview: enter the employer and unit, who you met and one or two things you discussed, and it writes a warm, specific thank-you email with a subject line. Free, no sign-up.

## Features

- Short, specific thank-you email with a subject line
- Uses only what you write about the interview
- What to check before sending
- Free, no sign-up
