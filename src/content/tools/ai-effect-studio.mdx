## About
AI Effect Studio is an AI video effect aggregator. It helps you find and use AI video effects.

## Features
- Browse trending AI video effects by dance, meme, cars, sports, and fashion
- Turn one photo into a shareable MP4 in minutes with no prompts or editing skills
- Most videos are ready in 3 to 10 minutes and wait in My videos for 30 days
- Failed videos refund credits, and photos are deleted after 7 days
- Remake an existing video using a trending effect
