## About

Hotel Lobby AI Video turns two uploaded photos into a synchronized two-person performance clip. You pick a preset motion direction, the tool handles camera timing, frames the result at 4:3, outputs 720p, and generates the audio track. Everything runs in the browser, so there is nothing to install.

## Features

- Turns two uploaded photos into one synchronized two-person clip
- Preset motion directions you pick before generation
- Automatic camera timing so the framing stays steady
- 4:3 Framing with 720p output
- Generated audio included with the video
- Fully browser based, no install and no local rendering
- Free to try, with paid plans for longer clips and higher settings

## Use Cases

- Short lobby-style clips for a hotel, a venue, or a short-stay brand
- Storyboard tests before committing to a real shoot
- Social clips that need a quick 4:3 render with audio
- Client previews that must be delivered the same day

## How to Use

1. Upload one image for the left performer and one for the right
2. Choose a motion preset and a clip length
3. Let the tool generate the motion and the audio
4. Review the result in five passes before you use it

## Tips

- Match the camera distance and eye level in both source photos, or the motion reads as a slide
- Keep the background simple so the generated motion has less to invent
- Review every clip in five passes before committing to a longer series

## Pricing

The tool is usable for free, with paid plans for longer clips and higher output settings.
