## About
Free browser tool that clears water and dust from phone speakers with a sound sweep. Open the site, press play, and a low-frequency sweep between 165 and 230 Hz pushes trapped water and dust out of the speaker grille. Nothing to install, no app, no account. Works on iPhone, Android, Apple Watch and AirPods.

## Features
- Low-frequency sound sweep between 165 and 230 Hz
- Pushes trapped water and dust out of the speaker grille
- Nothing to install, no app, and no account needed
- Works on iPhone, Android, Apple Watch and AirPods
- Open the site and press play to start the sweep
