## About
Chrome extension that saves Shopee product videos without watermark, every image in full size and reviews to Excel, in one folder per product.

## Features
- Download Shopee product videos as the exact MP4, highest resolution, no watermark added
- Save every product image in full size, including variant images named after their option
- Export product info and all SKUs with price, stock and units sold to Excel and CSV
- Export reviews to Excel and CSV with an AI sentiment label per review
- Save review photos and review videos, or only 1 to 3 star reviews
- One folder per product with numbered subfolders, works on all ten Shopee sites
