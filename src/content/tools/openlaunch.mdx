## About
OpenLaunch is a curated weekly product launch platform. Every week we publish a drop of the best new products, tools, and side projects. Makers submit their work for free, the community votes, and top launches earn visibility and a permanent dofollow backlink. Built by Rachel - contact fishfish@agentmail.to.

## Features
- A fresh curated drop of handpicked product launches every week
- Free submissions open to makers, indie builders, and startups
- Community voting to surface the best new launches
- Permanent dofollow backlinks for top-voted launches
- Launch-directory discoverability for tools, products, and side projectsand side pro
