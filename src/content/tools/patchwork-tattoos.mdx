## About
Patchwork Tattoos is an AI tattoo and image generation tool for exploring patchwork tattoo ideas, sleeve inspiration, filler concepts, and design variations. Users can generate or remix visual tattoo concepts that fit their preferred style, body placement, and composition plan before preparing references for a tattoo artist.

## Features
- AI tattoo concept generation and remixing
- Patchwork sleeve and filler inspiration
- Style and body placement guidance
- Reference images to share with a tattoo artist
