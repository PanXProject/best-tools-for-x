## About
Dear Moment lets families capture and organize photos, videos, notes, and voice memories in a private, easy-to-use timeline. Each memory can be shared with specific people, or kept just for yourself. Your data is stored in the EU, never used for AI training, and there are no ads.

## Features
- Photos, videos, notes and voice memories in one timeline
- Share with specific people or keep private
- Private invites, no app or account needed to view
- Export your memories anytime
- Free up to 5 moments; $5.99/month or $39.99/year after a 14-day trial
