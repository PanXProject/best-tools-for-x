## About

Guide to land records in Maharashtra on India Property Register: 7/12 (Satbara), 8A, Property Card, how plots are identified, the local terms and where the records are held. Mahabhulekh shows 7/12, 8A and property cards by survey/gat number or name in Marathi, and digitally signed copies come from the Satbara portal. DILRMP-MIS shows 100% of villages computerised; 5,560 villages are city-surveyed while 119 Nagar Panchayats and 241 Nagar Parishads are not (2024).

## Features

- Explains what the state calls its land records and what each shows
- Shows how plots are identified, from the state down to the village
- Lists the government portals and sources that hold the records
