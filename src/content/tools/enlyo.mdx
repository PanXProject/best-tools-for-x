## About
Enlyo is video analysis software for coaches and clubs in team sports. Upload match footage from any camera, tag the match while it plays with one key per moment, turn bookmarks into clips and summaries, and record a spoken review with a laser pointer to share with one player or the whole squad. Free with 10 GB; paid plans add storage from €9 a month.

## Features
- Live tagging with one key press per moment
- Searchable bookmarks across matches
- Clips and stitched summaries
- Recorded video reviews with webcam and a fading laser pointer
- Share links that open without an account
- Starter tag buttons for around 40 sports
- Import tags from Sportscode, Nacsport, Dartfish or a spreadsheet
- Browser app plus desktop apps for Mac and Windows
