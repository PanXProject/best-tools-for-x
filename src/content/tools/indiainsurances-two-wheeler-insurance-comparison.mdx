## About

A free IndiaInsurances table of 86 two-wheeler insurance policies: insured declared value (IDV), depreciation on parts, no-claim bonus and the compulsory deductible side by side, read from each policy's own documents. IndiaInsurances reads every policy from its own documents and the IRDAI registers, takes no commission and makes no sales calls.

## Features

- Reads each policy from its own documents
- Lets you sort columns or compare two policies
- Takes no commission and makes no sales calls
