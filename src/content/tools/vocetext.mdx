## About
Turn recordings, meetings, podcasts and videos into accurate text in minutes. Free to start, no sign-up required. Upload audio or video (MP3, M4A, WAV, MP4, MOV, WebM and more); the browser prepares the audio before upload. 20 languages or automatic detection. Every transcript has punctuation, paragraphs and timestamps. Export TXT, Word (DOCX), SRT, VTT and JSON. Freemium: guests 1 file/day up to 10 minutes; free account 3 files/day up to 30 minutes and 10 hours/month; Pro $10/month or $96/year.

## Features
- Upload audio or video (MP3, M4A, WAV, MP4, MOV, WebM and more)
- The browser prepares the audio before upload
- 20 Languages or automatic detection
- Every transcript has punctuation, paragraphs and timestamps
- Export TXT, Word (DOCX), SRT, VTT and JSON
- Free to start, no sign-up required
- Uses a Whisper large speech recognition model
- Guests get 1 file per day up to 10 minutes
- Free account gets 3 files per day up to 30 minutes and 10 hours per month
- Pro is $10/month or $96/year, with no daily file limit, files up to 10 hours, and 100 hours per month
- Speaker identification for up to 10 hours per month on Pro
