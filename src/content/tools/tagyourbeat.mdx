TagYourBeat is an AI producer tag generator for beatmakers and music producers. Type a phrase, pick from 8 vocal personas, apply one-click studio FX presets, and export mix-ready 44.1 kHz WAVs straight into FL Studio, Ableton, or Logic.

## About
TagYourBeat is built for beatmakers, producers, and audio engineers who want a signature vocal tag without studio time or a vocal chain. All audio processing runs locally in the browser via Web Audio, and pricing is one-time credit packs with no subscription.

## Features
- 8 Vocal personas from husky and bold to vintage radio DJ
- 8 One-click studio FX presets: Clean, Deep, Radio, Dreamy, Slowed, Hype, Echo, Distorted
- Smart headroom carving so tags never fight the 808
- DAW-ready export: uncompressed 16-bit or 24-bit WAVs at 44.1/48 kHz
- Dry and wet stem ZIP exports on Creator tier
- 100% Local privacy: effects run in your browser
- One-time credit packs with no subscription
