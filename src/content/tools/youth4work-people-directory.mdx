## About

The Youth4work people directory: browse profiles of job seekers who took free online skill, aptitude and psychometric tests on Youth4work and published an assessed profile that employers can find. Youth4work is an Indian talent assessment and careers platform founded in 2012 and based in Jaipur.

## Features

- Assessed talent profiles
- Free skill tests
- Found by employers
