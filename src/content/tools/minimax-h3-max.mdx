## About
MiniMax H3 Max is an AI video generator for creating short videos from text prompts and images. It is built for fast creative iteration, helping creators test scenes, motion, camera direction, visual style, and audio without a traditional video production workflow.

## Features
- Text-to-video generation
- Image-to-video creation
- Native synchronized audio
- Fast creative iteration
- Camera movement control
- Visual style prompting
- Short-form video creation
- Social media video concepts
- Product and advertising visuals
- Cinematic scene generation
- Rapid video prototyping
- Browser-based workflow
