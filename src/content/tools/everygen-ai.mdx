## About
Everygen AI is a browser-based tool for creating short videos from text prompts or reference images. Choose a template or video model, preview examples, and review the credit cost before generating.

## Features
- Create short videos from text prompts or reference images
- Choose templates and video models including Veo, Seedance and Kling
- Review the credit cost before generating
