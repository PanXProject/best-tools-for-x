## About
GPT Plus helps people without a USD card subscribe to overseas AI tools. ChatGPT Plus, Pro 10x and Pro 20x, Grok's X subscription tiers, Cursor and Google AI Pro (Gemini) can all be ordered with Alipay and activated on your own account. The maximum service fee for each plan is listed on the site; the rest is the plan's own price. ChatGPT orders carry a 30-day guarantee, and company invoices and bank transfers are available.

## Features
- Pay with Alipay, no USD card needed
- ChatGPT Plus, Pro 10x and Pro 20x
- Grok X subscription tiers
- Cursor and Google AI Pro (Gemini)
- Activated on your own account
- 30-Day guarantee on ChatGPT orders
- Company invoices and bank transfer
