## About

Guide to land records in Bihar on India Property Register: Jamabandi register, Khatiyan, LPC, how plots are identified, the local terms and where the records are held. Bihar Bhumi shows the jamabandi register, mortgaged land, mutation and LPC status and revenue maps; scanned khatiyan and maps are on Bhu-Abhilekh after OTP registration, with a fee for signed copies. DILRMP-MIS shows 100% of villages computerised (45,857).

## Features

- Explains what the state calls its land records and what each shows
- Shows how plots are identified, from the state down to the village
- Lists the government portals and sources that hold the records
