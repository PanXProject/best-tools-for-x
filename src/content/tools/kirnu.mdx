## About
Kirnu is a free platform with 50 Arabic online tools for text processing, Hijri and Gregorian date conversion, financial calculators, PDF tools, image processing and developer tools. The tools run directly in your browser and process data locally, with no sign-up and no uploading of files to servers, for a faster experience and better privacy.

## Features
- Numbers and amounts in words: turns numbers into Arabic words following the grammar rules, with cheque formatting for seven Arab currencies.
- Hijri and Gregorian date conversion using the Umm al-Qura calendar, with age, difference between dates and working days.
- Calculators for VAT, zakat, discounts and percentages.
- Arabic text clean-up: removes diacritics and tatweel, normalizes text, and fixes right-to-left direction problems.
- Image tools: compress, resize, convert formats, and remove location data from photos.
- PDF tools: merge, split, rotate pages and turn images into PDF, without uploading files to servers.
- QR codes for links, Arabic text and Wi-Fi networks.
- Can be installed as an app and works offline, with an open-source engine under the MIT licence.
