## About

A free IndiaInsurances table of 6 whole life policies: claim settlement time promised, sum assured, death benefit and maturity benefit side by side, read from each policy's own documents. IndiaInsurances reads every policy from its own documents and the IRDAI registers, takes no commission and makes no sales calls.

- Reads each policy from its own documents
- Lets you sort columns or compare two policies
- Takes no commission and makes no sales calls
