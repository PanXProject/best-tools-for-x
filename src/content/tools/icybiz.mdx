## About
Icybiz is a free visual website builder and hosting service for small businesses, independent professionals and personal projects. Choose a layout, edit text and media, and publish at a stable address. No coding, credit card or subscription is needed. This listing is submitted by the operator.

## Features
- Visual website editing with ready-made layouts
- Free hosting at a stable icybiz.com address
- Text, images, video and downloadable documents
- Undo and redo while editing your draft
- Separate Publish action for the live website
- Interface available in English, German, French and Spanish
