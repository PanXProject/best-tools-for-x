## About
AIByTask helps people explore exterior concepts from photos and plan home and garden spaces. It combines free manual 2D/3D editors with credit-based AI exterior generation and presentation boards. Credit purchases are currently unavailable.

## Features
- Free manual 2D/3D room, house-module and garden planning editors.
- AI exterior concepts generated from photos using credits.
- Project comparison and presentation boards costing 2 credits each.
- First Google sign-in grants 10 welcome credits once, not a recurring allowance.
- Interior AI generation and model rendering are preview features.
- Exterior concepts are for inspiration, not construction plans or guarantees of buildability.
