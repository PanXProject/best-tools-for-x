## About
GTA6 AI Video is an independent web app that creates fan-inspired GTA videos from your photos. Browse a prepared scene, add a photo for each required role, or start from a prompt, still, or reference clip. GTA6 AI Video is not affiliated with or endorsed by Rockstar Games or the owners of Grand Theft Auto.

## Features
- Prepared fan-inspired scenes with preview
- Start from a prompt, image, or reference clip
- Credit estimate shown before generating
- My creations history for playback and download
- AI video, AI image, and AI dance areas
