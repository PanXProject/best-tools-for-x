## About

India Property Register page for Bhandara district in Maharashtra: 6 talukas, 871 villages and 18 pincodes, with land records, village maps and property information for every village, plus towns and wards. The page also shows government land rates (circle rates), e.g. a median of Rs 5.47 lakh per hectare for dry agricultural land (2026-27).

## Features

- Lists every tehsil and village of one district with village counts
- Shows pincodes, towns and wards
- Links land records and village maps for each village
