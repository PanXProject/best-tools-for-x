## About

A free IndiaCos page listing 14,972 public announcements published by the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of India: corporate insolvency resolution, liquidation and voluntary liquidation, newest first. Each entry shows the company or LLP, its CIN, the applicant and the insolvency professional, and links to the company record when its CIN is in IndiaCos. Sub-lists cover CIRP, claims, liquidation, pre-pack and resolution plans.

## Features

- 14,972 IBBI announcements
- CIRP, liquidation, voluntary liquidation
- Linked to company records
