## About
VidSnappy is a free online video editing toolkit. Convert video to GIF, MP3, or MP4, compress and crop clips with no signup and no watermark, and use AI to transcribe, remove backgrounds, and turn long videos into short clips.

## Features
- Video to GIF, MP3, and MP4 conversion in the browser
- Compress and crop clips with no signup and no watermark
- Free in-browser video editor for everyday creative jobs
- AI transcription, captions, and short-clip maker
- Image and video background remover plus watermark remover
