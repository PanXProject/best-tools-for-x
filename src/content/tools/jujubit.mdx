## About
JuJuBit is an AI-assisted custom collectibles studio. Upload a photo of yourself, your partner or pet, choose a style (chibi, TRPG miniature, big-head portrait and more), review the 3D preview, and receive a 3D printed, hand-finished figure worldwide.

## Features
- Turn a photo into a custom 3D figurine
- Choose from styles like chibi and TRPG miniature
- Review the 3D preview before production
- Hand-finished figures shipped worldwide
- Keychains, phone grips and other custom products
