## About
[Reeling](https://reeling.app/) is a social publishing workspace for preparing and scheduling content. It includes AI-assisted content and video-ad preparation, a publishing calendar, and per-channel settings. Publishing depends on each platform's permissions, and some steps may need to be completed manually.

## Features
- Prepare social content and video ads with AI assistance
- Plan and schedule posts with a content calendar
- Set publishing preferences for each connected channel
