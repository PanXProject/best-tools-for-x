## About
Chato is a browser inbox that puts many WhatsApp numbers in one place. Link each number by QR code like WhatsApp Web, share it with teammates by Google email, and let an AI chatbot answer customer questions from your PDFs. €5 per number/month, no per-seat fees, 7-day free trial with no card.

## Features
- Many WhatsApp numbers in one browser dashboard
- QR-code linking, no Business API approval
- Share a number by Google email: view-only or view-and-send
- No per-seat fees; pay per WhatsApp number
- Per-session AI chatbot trained on your PDFs/text
- External API, real-time messaging, full history and search
