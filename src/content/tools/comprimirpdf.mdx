## About
ComprimirPDF reduces PDF file size directly inside your browser, so the document never leaves your device. Pick a PDF, choose a compression level and download the smaller file without registration or uploads.

## Features
- Compress PDF files locally in the browser without uploading them
- Three compression levels from light to maximum
- Works in Spanish on desktop and mobile browsers
- No registration, no watermark and no hidden limits
- Instant download of the compressed PDF
