## About

A free Data Galactica page on talent in Coimbatore: the roles and skills listed by about 262,768 people in Coimbatore, shown as counts with the top roles first. Every Data Galactica record carries a permanent Galactica ID (DGC- for organisations, DGP- for people), a grade and its sources.
