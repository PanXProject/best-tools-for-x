## About
Research U.S. carriers using source-dated FMCSA records, saved lists, and CSV, with Pro REST, MCP, and filing-evidence tools.

## Features
- Filter carriers by state, operating classification, and reported fleet size.
- Inspect carrier profiles and save lists.
- Export permitted fields to CSV.
- Pro adds insurance filing evidence, scoped REST, hosted MCP, and signed webhooks.
