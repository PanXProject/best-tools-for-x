## About
Explore how your own room might look in a different style or with virtual furnishings. Compare the new concept with the original photo. The generator requires an account and has a limited trial. It creates visual ideas without dimensions or construction drawings.

## Features
- Generate interior design concepts from a room photo
- Preview furniture and decor in an empty room
- Compare generated concepts with the original image
