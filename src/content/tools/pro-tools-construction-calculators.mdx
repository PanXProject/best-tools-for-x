## About
Pro² helps builders estimate quantities and check dimensions. A roof with a 7 × 5 m plan area at 25° has about 38.62 m² of sloped surface before overhangs and material overlap. Try that example in the browser without an account. The iPhone, iPad and Mac app is free to download. Optional in-app purchases and subscriptions.

## Features
- Roof area from plan dimensions and slope.
- Browser tools for slab volume and frame diagonals.
- Save calculations, view diagrams and export results in the app.
