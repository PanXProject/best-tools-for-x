## About
VidCella is a web-based AI image and video generator. Pick from models like Seedream, Seedance, Wan, Kling and Nano Banana to create images, edit photos and turn text or images into video. Free credits on signup, then one-time credit packs from $10 that never expire.

## Features
- Text-to-image and AI image editing (Seedream, FLUX, GPT Image, Qwen Image, Nano Banana)
- Text-to-video and image-to-video (Seedance, Wan, Kling, MiniMax, Grok Imagine)
- Reference-image workflows for consistent characters and style
- Model catalog to compare results across 30+ models
- Photo tools: AI clothes changer, pose transfer, cosplay generator, anime to real life
- Free credits on signup; one-time credit packs, no subscription
