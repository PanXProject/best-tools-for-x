## About
YouTranscripts lets creators, students and researchers work with available captions from supported YouTube videos. Read, copy, download or translate a transcript, and use a free account to create AI summaries under the Fair Usage Policy. The current launch offer is free.

## Features
- Read and search transcripts from supported YouTube videos
- Copy or download available captions
- Translate transcripts to read them in another language
- Create AI summaries with a free account
