## About
AI Video Download is a browser tool for saving public YouTube clips you own or have permission to download. Choose MP4 video or MP3 audio for an individual link, without a website account or extension. Source availability can vary. Submitted by the website operator.

## Features
- Individual public YouTube links: MP4 video or MP3 audio.
- MP4 batches of up to 10 individual links, one per line or imported from a UTF-8 TXT file.
- Sequential processing with per-item progress; individual failures do not stop the remaining jobs.
- Save each completed file separately.
- Each output is limited to 10 minutes and 100 MiB; ready files expire after one hour.
- No full-playlist or channel expansion. Use only content you have rights to save.
