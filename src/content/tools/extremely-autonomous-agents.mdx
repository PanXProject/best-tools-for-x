## About
EXTREMELY Autonomous Agents is a free, open-source AI agency that runs on your own computer. Agents for sales, marketing, support and more work around the clock, build the tools they're missing and only ask you when you choose. Bring your own Claude, ChatGPT or local models.

## Features
- Autonomous AI agents that work on a schedule, around the clock
- The Agency Engineer builds the tools your agents are missing
- The Agency Doctor checks the fleet and gets problems fixed
- Agent packs for sales, marketing, support, finance, research, content, operations, engineering and recruiting, or build your own
- You choose per tool: Silent, Logged or Approval
- Bring your own AI: Claude, ChatGPT or Copilot plans, API keys or local models
- Connects to Gmail, Google Drive, GitHub, Sentry, Zoho and more
- Runs on your own computer; keys and data stay encrypted there. Free and open source.
