## About
Tooldex is a free AI tools directory for creators and builders. Browse by task, then compare pricing, free plans and known usage limits before you choose an app. A popular comparison can be opened directly from the homepage without creating an account.

## Features
- Find tools by task and category
- Compare prices and free plans side by side
- Check known usage limits before signing up
