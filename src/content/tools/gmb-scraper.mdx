## About
GMB Scraper is a Chrome extension that reads public Google Maps results and saves the business details already on the page. It collects names, phones, websites, hours, and ratings, then exports a clean CSV or XLSX for local prospecting.

## Features
- Scan public Google Maps results for a trade and a city
- Capture phone, website, hours, ratings, and Place ID
- Pull public emails from linked business websites
- Remove duplicate listings before you export
- Download the finished list as CSV or XLSX
