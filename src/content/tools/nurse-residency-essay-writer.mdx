## About
Nurse Residency Essay Writer is a free AI tool for nursing students and new graduate nurses applying to hospital RN residency and fellowship programs. Paste the essay question and describe your clinical experience; it writes an answer in your own facts at the length the program allows, with what to check before you submit. It never invents a credential or a program fact. No sign-up.

## Features
- Answers the exact question you paste
- Length matched to the program's limit
- Why it works and what to check before submitting
- Free, no sign-up, nothing stored
