## About

I built Inkown to turn a photo of handwriting into an installable TTF font. Upload handwriting on plain paper and preview basic Latin letters, numbers, and punctuation before deciding whether to buy the download. No printer or template is required.

## Features

- Upload a JPG, PNG, or WebP image.
- Preview basic Latin letters, numbers, and punctuation.
- Purchase an installable TTF font with a one-time payment.
