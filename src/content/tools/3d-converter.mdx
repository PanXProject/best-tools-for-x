## About

3D Converter is a free online 3D model converter and viewer that runs in your browser. Open a model, pick an output format, and see what carries through before you download. STL, OBJ, PLY, 3MF, FBX, 3DS, GLB, glTF, DAE and RBXM convert locally, so the model is parsed on your device instead of being uploaded, and no account is needed. Every export is reopened and checked, with a before-and-after report. Free plan covers unlimited local conversion; Pro is $9.9/month.

## Features

- Free browser-local conversion, no sign-up
- STL, OBJ, PLY, 3MF, FBX, 3DS, GLB, glTF, DAE and RBXM converted locally
- Files processed on your device for local routes
- Dependency-aware OBJ/MTL and glTF/BIN/texture packages
- Before-and-after validation report
- Route warnings that show what each format can't carry
- Free 3D viewer with solid/wireframe and mesh statistics
- STEP and IGES cloud conversion for verified accounts
