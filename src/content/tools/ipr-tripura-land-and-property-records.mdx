## About

Tripura: 8 districts and 836 villages. How to check land records, maps, registration and encumbrance in Tripura, and the government portals that hold them. JAMI serves khatian, sabek plot search, online mutation and certified RoR, and public BhuNaksha covers 18 of 32 circles. DILRMP-MIS shows 100% on records, maps and map linkage. Tripura is one of nine ULI land-record states and runs NGDRS since 2021.

## Features

- Lists every district of the state with its village count
- Explains how to check land records, maps, registration and encumbrance
- Links the government portals that hold the records
