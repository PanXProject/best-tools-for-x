## About

Guide to land records in Haryana on India Property Register: Jamabandi, Mutation (Intkal), Fard Badar, how plots are identified, the local terms and where the records are held. WEB-HALRIS runs at all 143 tehsils and sub-tehsils since 2018; jamabandi and mutations are searched by owner name, khewat and khasra, but jamabandi.nic.in returned 504 on 11 Oct 2026.

## Features

- Explains what the state calls its land records and what each shows
- Shows how plots are identified, from the state down to the village
- Lists the government portals and sources that hold the records
