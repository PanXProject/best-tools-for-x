## About

A free Data Galactica list of the best-known companies and organisations in Hong Kong, ranked by how much its sources cover them and then by size (not by revenue). The list opens with China Mobile Limited, China Unicom (Hong Kong) Limited, AIA Group and Hong Kong Exchanges and Clearing Limited; each entry has a permanent Galactica ID, its status, industry and city, a data grade and the sources behind it.

## Features

- Lists the best-covered organisations in one country

- Shows the sources behind every record
