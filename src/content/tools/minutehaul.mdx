## About
MinuteHaul bulk-exports your whole meeting-transcript archive from tl;dv and Fireflies (beta) in one go, as Markdown files plus a CSV index, so you keep your notes when you cancel or switch tools. Runs in your browser with your own API key. Free trial exports 10 meetings; full archive is a one-time $19, no subscription.

- Bulk export of tl;dv and Fireflies transcripts
- Markdown files plus a CSV index of every meeting
