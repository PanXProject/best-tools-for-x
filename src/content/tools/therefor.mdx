## About
[Therefor](https://therefor.us/) is a private AI remembrance app where people share memories and audio of someone they miss, then revisit conversations through text or synthesized voice.

## Features
- Create a private remembrance space with shared memories and audio.
- Revisit conversations through text or synthesized voice.
- Start with a free text-only tier for one person.
