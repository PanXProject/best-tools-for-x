## About
Free wedding seating chart maker that runs in the browser. Lay out the room, paste your guest list, seat guests by hand or with auto-arrange, then print the chart, signs, escort cards and table lists. No account, no watermark, and the plan stays in your browser.

## Features
- Round, oval, square, rectangular, long banquet, head and sweetheart tables
- Paste or import a CSV guest list; couples and families stay together as one party
- Auto-arrange keeps parties and groups together and keep-apart pairs apart
- Lock seats and see meal counts by table
- Print the room plan, signs by table or A to Z (up to 24 x 36 in), escort cards, place cards and table lists; download PNG and CSV
- UK version with a traditional top table in Debrett's order
- 12 Templates for 50 to 200 guests that open in the maker
- Free: no account, no watermark, no credit line on printouts
