## About

A free Data Galactica list of the best-known companies and organisations in Portugal, ranked by how much its sources cover them and then by size (not by revenue). The list opens with TAP Air Portugal, Sata Internacional - Azores Airlines, S.A., Comboios de Portugal and Euronext Lisbon - Sociedade Gestora de Mercados Regulamentados S.A.; each entry has a permanent Galactica ID, its status, industry and city, a data grade and the sources behind it.

## Features

- Lists the best-covered organisations in one country
- Gives each a permanent Galactica ID and a grade
- Shows the sources behind every record
