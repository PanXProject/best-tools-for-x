## About
Image to Layers AI uses AI to separate an image into editable layers for design, compositing, and creative workflows. It helps creators adjust visual elements independently and continue editing a flattened image as a more flexible composition.

## Features
- AI-assisted separation of image elements
- Editable layers for design and compositing
- Web-based workflow for creative projects
