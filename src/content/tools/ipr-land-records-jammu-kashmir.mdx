## About

Guide to land records in Jammu & Kashmir on India Property Register: Jamabandi, Girdawari, Mutation, how plots are identified, the local terms and where the records are held. J&K land records sit on the LRIS portal (Aapki Zameen Aapki Nigrani, launched 22 Oct 2021), which was under maintenance or returning HTTP 504 on 11 Oct 2026; the DoLR directory points at a department CMS instead. DILRMP-MIS shows 70.54% of villages with computerised records (4,832 of 6,850).

## Features

- Explains what the state calls its land records and what each shows
- Shows how plots are identified, from the state down to the village
- Lists the government portals and sources that hold the records
