## About
AnyScript offers [timestamped social video transcripts](https://anyscript.app/) for checking source wording, researching creative structure and drafting original scripts. It supports eligible YouTube, Shorts, TikTok and Instagram sources. Free plan available; source availability and advanced features depend on plan. Submitted by Jim Chandler, founder of AnyScript. Prepared with AI assistance. Verify important quotations against original recordings.

## Features
- Timestamped text helps locate relevant source passages
- Evidence-linked research compares hooks, proof and calls to action
- Original script drafts can build on source-linked research
- Translation and export options depend on the selected plan
