## About

A free IndiaInsurances library of claim forms, grievance forms and service request forms from every insurer in India, each with field-by-field instructions on how to fill it, what documents to attach and where to send it. Saves policyholders hunting across insurer websites when a claim or complaint is due.

## Features

- Collects claim and grievance forms of every insurer
- Explains each field step by step
- Says what to attach and where to send it
