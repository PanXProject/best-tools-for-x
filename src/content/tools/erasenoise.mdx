## About
EraseNoise is a browser tool that strips steady fan, AC, and electrical hum from speech in audio and video. Upload common formats, hear a free 30-second before and after preview, then download a cleaned WAV or MP4.

## Features
- Free thirty-second before and after preview in the browser
- Download the cleaned audio as WAV or the video as MP4
- Built for creators who record with fans, AC, or room hum
- Freemium, with Pro around 9.99 USD per month
