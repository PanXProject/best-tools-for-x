## About
EraseNoise is a browser tool that removes background noise from speech in audio and video. Hear a free thirty-second before-and-after preview, then download a cleaned WAV or an MP4 that keeps the picture. Freemium: the preview is free, and longer files use paid packs or Pro.

## Features
- Remove background noise from speech in audio and video files
- Free 30-second before-and-after preview before you pay
- Download the cleaned result as WAV or MP4
