## About
Closva offers an AI-powered workspace for M&A transactions, combining document preparation, data rooms and due diligence. AI organises and files documents, drafts deal materials and flags risks, so teams can focus on decisions. Sellers can prepare a data room free for 60 days; buyers and investors use it for due diligence.

## Features
- AI document organisation
- Sell-side data room
- Buy-side due diligence
- Risk flagging
