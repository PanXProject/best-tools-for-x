## About

AuraVMS is RFQ and procurement software for small and medium businesses. Buyers send a request for quotation to suppliers through private links; suppliers respond without creating an account. AuraVMS lines up every quote side by side (price, MOQ, lead time, terms), ranks them automatically as L1/L2/L3, supports anonymous bidding, and carries the winning quote into a purchase order. It is built for small procurement teams that run RFQs over email and spreadsheets today.

## Features

- Suppliers respond without creating an account
- Side-by-side supplier quote comparison (price, MOQ, lead time, terms)
- Automatic L1/L2/L3 price ranking
- Anonymous bidding
- RFQ to purchase-order workflow
