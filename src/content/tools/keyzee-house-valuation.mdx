## About
Keyzee House Valuation helps homeowners and buyers research property prices in England and Wales. Enter a postcode and property details to get an estimate and compare nearby sales using HM Land Registry sold-price data. It is free to use without an account. Estimates are research guides, not formal valuations.

## Features
- Free property value estimates for England and Wales
- Postcode and property-detail search
- Nearby comparable sold-property prices
- No account required to use the valuation tool
