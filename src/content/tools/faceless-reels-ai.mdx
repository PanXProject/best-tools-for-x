## About
Faceless Reels AI is a browser-based workspace for planning and reviewing faceless short videos. It coordinates scripts, scene maps, visuals, voiceover, captions, editing, export checks, and release review in one repeatable workflow. Creators remain responsible for factual accuracy, media rights, disclosures, and platform requirements.

## Features
- Structured script planning for concise short-form narration
- Scene mapping that aligns visuals with each spoken beat
- Voiceover review for pronunciation, pacing, and emphasis
- Mobile-first caption checks for timing and readable line breaks
- Visual source tracking for media-rights and disclosure review
- Pre-publish export checks for dimensions, audio, and final frames
