## About
CloneCut is an AI video tool that clones viral videos: upload a reference video and an image, then replace people, products, objects, or backgrounds while keeping the original motion, camera moves, and timing. Watermark-free output and free credits to start.

## Features
- Clone viral videos from a reference clip
- Replace people, products, or backgrounds
- Keep the original motion and timing
- Agent workspace plans scenes and audio
- Watermark-free 480p to 1080p output
