## About
Independent photo-to-video tool inspired by the Talk to Me Doja meme. Upload one clear, permitted adult photo to request a 14-second vertical video; each request costs 100 credits. Successful MP4 generations can vary in duration, framing, and visual similarity. One-time credit packs start at $9.99, and failed generations refund credits. The service is not affiliated with Doja Cat or the anime referenced by the meme.

## Features
- Request a short vertical video from one permitted photo.
- Download a successful generation as an MP4 file.
- Use one-time credit packs; failed jobs refund credits.
