## About

Nursing Specialty Finder suggests the nursing specialties that fit you: pick where you are (student, new grad, RN, LPN), the pace, patients and schedule you like, and what you enjoy or want to avoid, and it explains three specialties that fit, why, what a shift is like and how people get in. Free, no sign-up.

## Features

- Three specialties matched to your answers, plus two more to look at
- What a shift is like and how people get in
- Certification commonly pursued
- Free, no sign-up
