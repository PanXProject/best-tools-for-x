## About
ArchLet is an independent information platform for people renting, or considering renting, a railway arch in the UK. Its free tools give indicative estimates, not valuations or professional advice.

## Features
- Total Cost Calculator: occupation costs and cash needed to open (rent, rates, running costs, fit-out, deposits and fees)
- Find Areas Within Your Budget: compare up to five covered English areas against a monthly premises budget
- Is My Railway Arch Rent Fair?: compare an annual rent and floor area with area information
- Estimates for business rates and fit-out costs, and prompts for questions about lease terms
- A viewing checklist and practical guides for small businesses
- No account needed
