## About
AI Interior Design is a browser-based room planning tool that transforms an uploaded room photo into realistic redesign concepts. Choose a room type, design style and lighting direction, compare alternatives, and use the results as a visual brief before checking measurements, circulation and budget.

## Features
- Generate realistic redesign concepts from room photos
- Choose room types, interior styles and lighting directions
- Compare visual alternatives before purchasing furniture
- Explore ideas for living rooms, bedrooms, kitchens and offices
- Create a practical brief for measurement and budget checks
