## About

Kling 4.0 is an AI video generation tool for creating videos from text, images, and visual references. It is designed for creators, marketers, and teams who want more control over motion, camera direction, scene style, and visual consistency.

## Features

- Text-to-video generation
- Image-to-video generation
- Reference-based video creation
- Cinematic camera movement
- Motion control
- Character consistency
- Product video creation
- Social media video generation
- Visual reference support
- Prompt-based scene direction
