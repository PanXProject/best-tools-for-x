## About
Nah Crypto Tax Tracker is a spreadsheet template (.xlsx) plus a step-by-step PDF guide. Log buys and sells (swaps as a sell plus a buy) and get FIFO cost basis, realized gain/loss per year, and current holdings by token. FIFO only; not tailored to any country's tax rules. No wallet connection: your data stays in your own file. Price: 29 USDC one-time, paid on Solana. Not tax advice: it organises records and does not determine what you owe; consult a qualified tax professional.

## Features
- FIFO cost basis

- Current holdings by token
- No wallet connection: your data stays in your own file
- Airdrops, staking and transfers are explained in the guide but not calculated
