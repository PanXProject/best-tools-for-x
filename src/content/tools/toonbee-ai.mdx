## About
Toonbee is an AI cartoon video generator that turns a script or a one-line story idea into a finished animated video with AI voiceover and music, keeping the same character in every scene. Built for creators, marketers and teachers who need cartoon videos without animating by hand.

## Features
- Consistent characters across every scene of a story
- Script to video with AI voiceover and soundtrack
- More than ten cartoon styles including 3D, anime and storybook
- Landscape 16:9 or native vertical 9:16 output
- Commercial use rights on generated videos
