## About
Free event timeline builder from ScanIt Events. Enter the ceremony start time and get a standard day built around it — getting ready, ceremony, family photos, cocktail hour, dinner, speeches, dancing. Change any time or description, add what is missing, remove what does not apply, and shift the whole schedule at once when the venue moves. Then copy the timeline and paste it to your planner, photographer, caterer and band. No signup.

## Features
- Builds a full day from the ceremony start time
- Shift the whole schedule at once when the venue moves
- Edit, add or remove any row
- Copy the timeline to paste to planner, photographer, caterer and band
- No signup, no email
