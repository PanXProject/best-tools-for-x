## About

Guide to land records in Goa on India Property Register: Form I & XIV, Form D, how plots are identified, the local terms and where the records are held. DSLR shows Form I & XIV and Form D by subdivision for information only; a signed copy is a paid two-day Goa Online service from the Mamlatdar. DILRMP-MIS shows 100% computerisation (426 villages). NAKSHA URPRO cards were in progress in Cuncolim, Margao and Panaji in July 2026.

## Features

- Explains what the state calls its land records and what each shows
- Shows how plots are identified, from the state down to the village
- Lists the government portals and sources that hold the records
