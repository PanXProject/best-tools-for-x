## About

PSL Scale is an AI-powered facial attractiveness analyzer. Upload a photo or use your live camera to get a PSL score (0–8) mapped to community-standard tiers like CHAD, STACY, and NORMIE. Covers symmetry, harmony, skin quality, golden ratio, and more — plus a personalized improvement plan. Photos are never stored.

## Features

- AI-verified PSL score on a 0–8 scale with tier classification
- 8-Dimension breakdown: symmetry, harmony, proportions, skin quality, and more
- Multiple tools: Photo, Live Camera, Face Report, Golden Ratio, Face Symmetry, Hunter Eyes
- Gender-aware True Adam / True Eve seven-factor report
- AI Roast, Face Shape styling, and AI Hairstyle Changer
- Two-photo PSL Rating Compare
- Personalized improvement plan with natural, non-surgical tips
- Privacy-first: photos processed on-device and never stored
