## About
RiseMore helps business owners and marketing teams grow with an AI marketer. Four agents for SEO, GEO, Reddit and Social learn your business, discover relevant opportunities, and create content for review and publication. Research and drafts stay editable. A limited free plan is available; paid plans start at $20/month and all four agents at $60/month. Managed GEO and Reddit publishing has separate fees.

## Features
- Reusable knowledge of your business, products, audience and voice
- SEO keyword research, article creation and connected WordPress publishing
- GEO buyer-question research, article creation and AI mention and citation monitoring
- Reddit community and thread discovery, post and comment creation, and publishing on your behalf
- Social posts with images for review and scheduled publishing to X and LinkedIn
- Editable research and drafts with your approval before publication
- Search Console performance tracking for your connected website
