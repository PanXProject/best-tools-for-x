## About
CoinBoss is a live crypto market-data dashboard for traders and analysts tracking market stress across major exchanges. It brings together liquidations, open interest, and funding rates so readers can compare cross-venue positioning and squeeze pressure. The data is informational only, not investment advice or a guarantee of returns.

## Features
- Cross-exchange liquidation data and time-window comparisons
- Open interest for outstanding derivatives positions
- Funding rates for perpetual-futures market context
