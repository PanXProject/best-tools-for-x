## About
Capvant gives business owners one simple request for comparing business funding options from matched third-party providers. Comparing options is free and does not affect credit score. Capvant is a marketplace, not a lender.

## Features
- One simple request to compare business funding options
- Matched offers from third-party lending and finance providers
- Free to compare with no impact on credit score
