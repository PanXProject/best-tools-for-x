## About

Youth4work Prep page for the AMCAT (employability test practice): a question bank of 6,097 practice questions and 5 full mock tests, already attempted by 72,174 test takers, with a score and rank against others after each test. - Includes full mock tests in the exam pattern
- Shows your score and rank against other test takers
