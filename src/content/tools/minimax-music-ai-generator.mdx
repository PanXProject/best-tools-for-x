## About
MiniMax Music is an online AI music generator for turning text prompts and lyrics into songs. The site describes vocal and instrumental workflows, controls for genre, mood, instruments, song structure and vocal performance, plus multiple model versions. Paid credit packs are one-time purchases.

## Features
- Generate songs from prompts or supplied lyrics
- Create instrumental tracks without lyrics or singing
- Guide song structure with verse, chorus, bridge, hook and outro sections
- Shape vocals and arrangements with detailed creative direction
