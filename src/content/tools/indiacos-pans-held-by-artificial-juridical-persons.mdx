## About

763 PANs whose 4th character is "J" (Artificial Juridical Person), each with its GST registrations across states. Most registrations first. The 4th character of a PAN tells you the type of holder; this IndiaCos page groups GST registrations by that PAN so you can see every state where one entity is registered.
