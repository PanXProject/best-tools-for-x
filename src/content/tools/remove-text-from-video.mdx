## About
Remove Text From Video erases burned-in subtitles, captions, titles, date stamps, prices and text logos from your own videos. AI rebuilds the background behind the words instead of blurring or cropping. Every upload gets a free 10-second preview.

## Features
- Remove burned-in subtitles and captions from MP4 and MOV videos
- Remove titles, date stamps, prices and text overlays
- AI background reconstruction instead of blur or crop
- Free 10-second preview on every upload, no watermark
- Credit cost shown before the full job starts
- Uploads and results deleted after 24 hours
