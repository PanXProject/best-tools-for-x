## About
Discover Alone is a free map for finding places around you or planning a wander farther away. Open a place to find practical details, local history, pictures where available and Explorer Tom's cheeky account with tips for the visit. It began in Donegal and now covers thousands of places. Follow the map, find something surprising and head out to see it for yourself.

## Features
- Explore places on a free interactive map
- Read Explorer Tom's lively place stories and useful tips
- Find local history and practical details in one place
- Browse nearby discoveries and plan a day out
- See pictures and location details where available
