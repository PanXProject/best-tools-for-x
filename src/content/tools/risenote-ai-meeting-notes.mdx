## About
Risenote is an AI meeting notes app for iPhone that records your meetings, transcribes them, and turns them into clean summaries with action items you can share. Get the App Store version at https://apps.apple.com/us/app/risenote-ai-meeting-notes/id6756504124

## Features
- Record meetings and conversations on your phone
- Automatic AI transcription of what was said
- Concise summaries with key decisions
- Action items extracted automatically
- Searchable archive of past meetings
