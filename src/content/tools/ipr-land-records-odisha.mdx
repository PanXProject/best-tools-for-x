## About

Guide to land records in Odisha on India Property Register: RoR (Khatian), how plots are identified, the local terms and where the records are held. Bhulekh Odisha is a session-gated application: the DoLR RoRView link showed a session-timeout page on 11 Oct 2026, so the search fields were not verified. DILRMP-MIS shows 99.91% of villages computerised. Odisha is one of nine ULI land-record states; e-Registration last synced to NGDRS in June 2024.

## Features

- Explains what the state calls its land records and what each shows
- Shows how plots are identified, from the state down to the village
- Lists the government portals and sources that hold the records
