## About
RAADS-R Test Online is a private browser-based 80-question self-assessment for adults exploring recurring autistic traits. It presents an overall score, four domain summaries, research context, and responsible guidance that screening is informational rather than a diagnosis.

## Features
- Complete an 80-question adult autism self-assessment
- Review four domain-level result summaries
- Read research context and responsible interpretation guidance
- Use the assessment without creating an account
- Keep results private in the browser
