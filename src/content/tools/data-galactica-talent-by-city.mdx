## About

Data Galactica's talent-by-city pages show the roles and skills people list in 1,199 cities, as counts from profiles and directories with no person named. Delhi leads with about 20 lakh people, then Chennai, Mumbai, Bangalore, Pune and Hyderabad. Useful for hiring and market research. Part of Data Galactica.
