## About
Convertor Macha is a free collection of 100+ browser-based tools for converting and editing PDFs, images, and files. No signup required, no watermarks, and image tools run entirely in your browser.

## Features
- Merge and split PDFs
- Compress images
- Convert between formats
- OCR scanned documents, and more.
- Every tool works directly in the browser with no forced account creation and no watermarks on your output. Image processing runs client-side, so files never leave your device.
Free to use, with optional paid packs for heavier server-side processing.
