## About
WatermarkRemoverVideo helps creators remove watermarks, text and logos from MP4 videos they own or have permission to edit. AI reconstructs the marked area without cropping the frame. Paid credit packs start at $19.99 and new users receive 30 trial credits.

## Features
- Remove watermarks, logos and text from MP4 video
- Reconstruct the marked area using AI
- Upload MP4 files up to 40 MB and 10 minutes
- Remove video backgrounds up to 60 seconds
