## About

Wordle Solver helps you finish today's puzzle without guessing blindly. Type each guess, tap the tiles to match the green, yellow and gray colors from your game, and the tool narrows the remaining answers instantly and suggests strong next guesses.

Everything happens in the browser. There is no account, no download and no tracking of your puzzle history.

## Features

- Enter the letters you have already tried and mark each tile green, yellow or gray
- See the shortlist of words that still fit the pattern
- Get a suggested next guess that splits the remaining answers best
- Works on mobile and desktop with no sign-up

## Who it is for

Players who are stuck on a hard Wordle day, people learning the game, and anyone who wants to understand why a guess is strong instead of memorising answer lists.
