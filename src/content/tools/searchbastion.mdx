## About
SearchBastion is a free SEO audit and website analytics dashboard. Connect Google Search Console and Google Analytics, run technical SEO audits, track keyword rankings and uptime, and get a prioritized list of fixes in one place. Built for small businesses, marketers and agencies.

## Features
- Free technical SEO audit with prioritized fixes
- Google Search Console insights: queries, pages, CTR
- Keyword rank tracking
- Website uptime and performance monitoring
- Traffic and behavior analytics
- Scheduled SEO reports for clients
