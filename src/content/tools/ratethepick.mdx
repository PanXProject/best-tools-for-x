## About
RateThePick is an independent product research and comparison site. It publishes buying guides and reviews that summarize specifications, buyer feedback, pricing, and usability, explaining who each product suits and which tradeoffs matter. Affiliate relationships do not affect coverage, rankings, or scores.

## Features
- Category buying guides
- Scored product reviews
- Specification and pricing comparisons
- Buyer feedback analysis
- Clear recommendations and tradeoffs
