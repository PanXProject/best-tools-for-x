## About

OmniHuman 2.0 is an AI-powered video generator that transforms images and audio into lifelike animated videos. With natural lip sync, expressive facial movements, and character animation, it helps creators produce talking avatars, digital presenters, and engaging video content without complex editing software.

## Features

- **AI Image-to-Video Generation:** Transform static portraits and illustrations into animated videos.
- **Natural Lip Sync:** Synchronize mouth movements with speech or music.
- **Expressive Character Animation:** Generate facial expressions, gestures, and natural body movements.
- **Talking Avatar Creation:** Create animated characters and digital presenters.
- **Audio-Driven Animation:** Use audio tracks to guide character movements and expressions.
- **Text-Prompt Control:** Customize character actions and camera movements.
- **Multiple Character Styles:** Animate portraits, illustrations, and creative characters.
- **Simple Video Workflow:** Upload an image, add audio, and generate a video.
- **Creative Applications:** Produce content for social media, education, marketing, and storytelling.
