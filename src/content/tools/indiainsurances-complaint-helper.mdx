## About

A free IndiaInsurances tool for a delayed, cut or rejected claim: pick your insurer, your problem and your state to get the insurer's claim and grievance contacts (copied from its own pages, with the source), its forms, a ready-to-send complaint letter that quotes the rules, and the Insurance Ombudsman office for your state.

## Features

- Insurer grievance contacts with source
- Ready-to-send complaint letter
- Ombudsman office by state
