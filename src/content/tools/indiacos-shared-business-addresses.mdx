## About

A free IndiaCos index of 2,44,921 addresses in India used by two or more companies, LLPs or GST registrations as a registered office or principal place of business, across 36 states and union territories (Delhi 48,361, Maharashtra 41,704, West Bengal 24,955, Uttar Pradesh 21,124). Pick a state, then a district, or search any address to see which businesses share it.

## Features

- 2,44,921 Shared addresses
- 36 States and UTs
- Search any address
