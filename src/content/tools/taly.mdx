## About
Taly (تالي) is an Arabic queue and appointment booking app for salons, clinics, offices and service centers. Customers book a place in line or an appointment in seconds, or scan a QR code on site, and track their turn live from anywhere. Businesses manage their queue, appointments and branches, and get a booking link they can share on WhatsApp and Instagram. Free on Android and iOS.

## Features
- Book a place in line or an appointment in seconds
- Scan a QR code on site to join the queue
- Live tracking of your turn from anywhere
- Queue, appointment and branch management for businesses
- Shareable booking link for WhatsApp and Instagram
- Book more than one service in a single booking
