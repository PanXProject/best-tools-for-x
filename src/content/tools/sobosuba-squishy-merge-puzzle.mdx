## About
sobosuba is a free, ultra-squishy merge puzzle game you play right in your browser. Launch numbered blobs into the arena; two blobs with the same value merge into a bigger one. A gentle shot places a blob exactly where you want it, and a stronger shot shoves the pile to open a path to the next match. Keep space under the two switches at the top, because if both get pressed at once the round is over. Works on desktop and mobile, no download.

## Features
- Launch numbered blobs into the arena; matching values merge into a bigger one
- A gentle shot places a blob exactly where you want it
- A stronger shot shoves the pile to open a path to the next match
- Soft-body physics, a global leaderboard, and achievements
- Unlockable blob styles, and it works on desktop and mobile with no download
