## About

Billing Tiers helps SaaS founders and product teams compare up to four pricing plans in a browser. Model flat-rate, per-seat, usage-based or hybrid pricing, then change customer usage and costs to see which plans retain a healthy gross margin. The simulator is free to use without signup.

## Features

- Stress-test margins when a small group of customers uses substantially more than average.
- Compare modeled Stripe, Paddle and Polar fees and preview a customer-facing pricing page.
