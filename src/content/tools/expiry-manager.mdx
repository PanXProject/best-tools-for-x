## About
Expiry Manager tracks passports, insurance, certificates, warranties and subscriptions — then reminds you before they expire so nothing slips. Smart Scan AI extracts expiry dates from documents and photos. Freemium: Free / Premium $4/mo / Teams $6/mo.

## Features
- Track important expiry dates in one place
- Get reminders before documents and subscriptions expire
- Extract dates from documents and photos with Smart Scan AI
