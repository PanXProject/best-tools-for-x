## About
MiniMax Music 3.0 is an online AI music generator that turns prompts and lyrics into songs. The product page describes lyric generation, vocal and instrumental modes, song-structure tags, and controls for style, mood, instruments, and vocal direction. Tracks use credits.

## Features
- Create songs from prompts and supplied lyrics
- Generate lyrics from an idea before making a track
- Make instrumental tracks without vocals
- Shape song sections with verse, chorus, bridge, and outro tags
