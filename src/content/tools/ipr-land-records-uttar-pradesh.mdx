## About

Guide to land records in Uttar Pradesh on India Property Register: Khatauni, Khasra, Certified khatauni, how plots are identified, the local terms and where the records are held. UP Bhulekh shows a free khatauni copy behind a captcha and a paid certified copy, plus plot sale and dispute status by gata; maps are on UP Bhunaksha. DILRMP-MIS flags all villages computerised but its RoR count (110,326) is about the village count, so it is not a holding inventory.

## Features

- Explains what the state calls its land records and what each shows
- Shows how plots are identified, from the state down to the village
- Lists the government portals and sources that hold the records
