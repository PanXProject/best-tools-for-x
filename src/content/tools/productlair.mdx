## About

ProductLair is a dropshipping product research tool for people starting or running Shopify stores. It brings together product details, product scores, TikTok ad engagement data, and profit calculations. Higher plans include Shopify product copy, ad copy, and launch plans. Paid access starts at $49 as a one-time payment.

## Features

- Review dropshipping product details and product scores.
- Compare TikTok advertising engagement data for product ideas.
- Calculate product profit and selling margins.
- Prepare Shopify product copy with the Launch plan.
- Access a four-week launch plan with the Launch plan.
