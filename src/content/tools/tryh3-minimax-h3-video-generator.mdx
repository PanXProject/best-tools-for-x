## About
TryH3 is a free online AI video generator powered by MiniMax H3. Create native 2K, 4–15-second videos from text, images, first or last frames, and multimodal references with synchronized stereo audio.

## Features
- Native 2K video output
- Text, image, and frame-to-video generation
- Synchronized stereo audio
- Multimodal visual references
