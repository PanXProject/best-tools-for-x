## About
Healthcare Resignation Letter Generator writes a short, professional resignation letter for any healthcare job: role, unit, last day and notice, thanks to your team, and any handoff. It adds a checklist for notice rules, sign-on bonus repayment and returning equipment.

## Features
- Professional letter plus a checklist for notice and bonus repayment
- Uses only what you write, no invented facts
- Free, no sign-up, nothing stored
