## About
CopyrightChecker identifies the music inside a video or audio file before it gets claimed or muted on YouTube or TikTok. Every check returns the song, the artist and the label or distributor with ISRC and release year, a plain-language platform-risk level, and royalty-free swaps for the same slot. Full scans mark every 12-second segment. Free clip checks run in the browser; uploaded files are deleted after 24 hours.

## Features
- Names the song, artist, label or distributor, ISRC and release year
- Rates how likely YouTube or TikTok is to claim or mute the track
- Suggests royalty-free alternatives for the same slot
- Marks every 12-second segment of a full scan so you know what to cut
