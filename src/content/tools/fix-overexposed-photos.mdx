## About
Fix Overexposed Photos adjusts overly bright images with AI, balancing highlights, midtones and overall exposure. Upload JPG, PNG or WebP photos, compare the correction with the original, and download the result. Fully clipped highlights may not contain recoverable original detail. Initial free usage is limited; paid subscriptions or credit packs are available.
