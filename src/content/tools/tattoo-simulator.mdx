## About

Tattoo Simulator is a browser-based workspace for combining a body photo with separately supplied artwork. Adjust the display position, size, rotation and opacity, then export a PNG reference. The manual workflow is separate from an optional paid AI cover-concept feature.

## Features

- Import a body photo and a separate design image.
- Move and resize the overlay within the photo.
- Rotate artwork to compare its displayed direction.
- Adjust opacity to inspect the underlying photo.
- Mirror the supplied overlay when exploring alternatives.
- Reset the arrangement and download a PNG preview.
- Use local manual placement without generation credits.
- Explore optional paid AI cover concepts separately.
