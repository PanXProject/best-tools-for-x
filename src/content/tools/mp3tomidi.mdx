## About
MP3toMIDI.pro is a browser-based AI audio-to-MIDI converter for musicians, producers, songwriters, and creators. Upload an MP3, WAV, or M4A recording, let the service detect notes and timing, preview the result, and download an editable .mid file for your DAW or notation software. It works best with clear melodies, piano phrases, vocal lines, guitar riffs, and other single-instrument recordings. Guests and free users can test short previews before paying for longer conversions.

## Features
- Converts MP3, WAV, and M4A to MIDI
- Note and timing detection
- Preview before download
- Editable .mid export for any DAW
