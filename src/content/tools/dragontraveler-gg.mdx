## About
Independent Dragon Traveler wiki: all 85 Luminaries ranked T0-T4, best team compositions, redeem codes with dates and beginner guides.

## Features
- Tier list ranking all 85 Luminaries from T0 to T4, filterable by class and faction
- Character pages with skills, talents, divinity, relics, gear, counters and best teammates
- Dream Teams page with 14 example lineups, Overdrive order and budget substitutes
- Redeem codes with dates and occasions, plus how to redeem and why codes fail
- Reference pages for banner schedule, event skins, factions, status effects and Steam version
- About 90 guides on beginner tips, rerolling, star-up costs, divinity, Temporal Rift and meta
