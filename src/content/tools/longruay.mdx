## About

- *LongRuay** is an AI-powered life simulation game that lets you experience what it might be like to manage a **US$100 million fortune over 30 fictional days**. Instead of simply watching a story unfold, you make important decisions every day and experience the financial and personal consequences of those choices. The game combines AI-generated storytelling with a deterministic simulation engine, creating an interactive experience where your decisions shape the outcome.

## What Is LongRuay?

LongRuay is built around a simple question: **What would you actually do if you suddenly had $100 million?** At the beginning of the simulation, you choose an identity such as a Tech Founder, Family Wealth Heir, or Global Investor. Each identity starts with the same fortune but introduces different circumstances, opportunities, and challenges.

Throughout the 30-day journey, an AI character named **Mira**, your virtual chief of private office, presents daily briefings involving investments, acquisitions, luxury purchases, invitations, unexpected problems, and other life-changing situations. You normally choose one of several options, and the simulation engine calculates how your decision affects your wealth and personal metrics.

## Features

- **30-Day Simulation** — Play through one fictional choice per day for a full month, with each decision shaping your portfolio and personal life.
- **Six Unique Identities** — Start as a Tech Founder, Family Wealth Heir, Global Investor, or Crypto Billionaire, with the Value Investor and Thai Hotel Tycoon available as unlockable characters.
- **AI-Powered Briefings** — Mira, your chief of private office, delivers personalized daily scenarios written by AI, tailored to your current story and choices.
- **Deterministic Financial Engine** — A versioned rules engine—not AI—calculates every portfolio and life consequence, ensuring the math is always transparent and fair.
- **Unlockable Story Expansions** — Add depth with one-time unlocks like *The Celeste Entanglement*, a narrative arc blending personal relationships with financial decisions.
- **Personal Metrics & Final Report** — Finish with a full balance sheet and five personal metrics (e.g., Freedom, Trust) that determine your outcome archetype.
- **Progress Saved Automatically** — Pause at any day and resume on any device; credits are never charged for days the AI cannot deliver.
- **No Pay-to-Win** — Outcomes are driven entirely by your decisions, not purchases or randomness.

## How to Use LongRuay

Getting started is simple. First, visit the LongRuay website and begin a new simulation. Next, choose your preferred millionaire identity and start with **US$100 million**.

Each day, read Mira's briefing carefully and review the available choices. Select the option that best matches your strategy, personality, or goals. Continue making one major decision per day until you complete all 30 days.

At the end, review your **final private report** to see how your decisions affected your wealth, personal metrics, and overall legacy.

## Who Is It For?

LongRuay is ideal for people interested in **AI games, life simulations, decision-making, wealth fantasies, interactive storytelling, and casual strategy games**. It can also be a fun way to explore how different personal values might influence decisions when money is no longer the primary limitation.

## Conclusion

Overall, **LongRuay combines AI storytelling, financial simulation, and interactive decision-making into a unique 30-day experience**. It is not designed to provide real financial advice; the world, people, assets, and scenarios are fictional and intended for entertainment.

- *Start with $100 million. Make your choices. Live your 30-day millionaire life.**
