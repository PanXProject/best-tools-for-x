## About

Yap for Android is voice dictation for your phone. Turn on the Yap keyboard, tap Start, speak, and Yap types your words into any app. Speech is recognized on the phone with the NVIDIA Parakeet model, so your audio is never uploaded, and it works offline after a one-time model download.

## Features

- Dictate into any app with the Yap keyboard
- Speech recognition on the phone, audio never uploaded
- Punctuation and filler-word removal
- 25 European languages detected automatically
- Searchable history stored on the device
