## About
Rap Blaze is an AI rap video generator. Upload one photo and it creates a vertical rap video with the original track, sized for TikTok, Reels and Shorts. Pay once from $6.99, no subscription.

## Features
- AI rap video from one photo
- Original rap track included
- Vertical video sized for TikTok, Reels and Shorts
