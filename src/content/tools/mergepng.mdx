## About
MergePNG is a free, browser-based toolkit for combining images without sending them to a server. Merge PNG files or mix PNG, JPG, JPEG, and WebP images in horizontal, vertical, or grid layouts, preview the result, and download. No sign-up or watermark is required.

## Features
- Merge multiple PNG, JPG and WebP files into one image
- Horizontal, vertical, and grid layouts with live preview
- Adjustable spacing, padding, background, and borders
- Export as PNG, JPG, or WebP
- Convert one or more PNG files to PDF
- Runs locally in the browser with no upload or sign-up
