## About
DanseAI recreates the white-studio Alors on Danse keyboard meme from two photos. Photo 1 plays the mini keyboard and Photo 2 is the hype partner. Soundtrack included, no prompt needed.

## Features
- Turn two photos into an Alors on Danse meme video
- Choose 5 to 30 seconds and 9:16 or 16:9 up to 1080p
- Includes the soundtrack with Seedance or MiniMax H3
