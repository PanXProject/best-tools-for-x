## About
You don't have to write anything. Sendhour is an all-in-one short-form content tool for people marketing apps and small brands, from solo developers to agencies running many client accounts. Paste your app or product link and it makes and posts short-form videos for you; you can write or edit your own version on the site if you want.

## Features
- AI writes scripts in viral formats for your product
- AI studio creates photo slideshows or short videos with voiceover
- Posts to TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, Threads, Bluesky, X and LinkedIn
- Tracks analytics for what you post
