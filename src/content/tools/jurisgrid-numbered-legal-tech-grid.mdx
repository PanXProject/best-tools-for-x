## About

The grid view on JurisGrid (jurisgrid.com): 127 Indian legal tech companies, each with a grid number, its sector (from Court Data & Legal Research and Legal AI & Drafting to Contracts, ODR, Compliance & GRC and IP Tech), a one-line summary and a link to its website. Search by name or filter with sector chips. Free to browse; listings are free. Maintained by the eCourtsIndia team.

- Numbered grid of 127 companies

- Free listings
