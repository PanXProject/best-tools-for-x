## About

QwQ AI is an AI-powered Q&A assistant that searches the live web and answers with cited sources. Ask in plain language — QwQ AI reasons over real-time results and replies in seconds. Supports English, Chinese, Japanese, Spanish, Vietnamese, and Portuguese.

## Features

- Real-time web search for every question
- Cited sources with every answer for easy verification
- Transparent reasoning step you can inspect
- Supports 6 languages: English, Chinese, Japanese, Spanish, Vietnamese, Portuguese
- Follow-up questions with full conversation context
- No installation needed — runs entirely in browser
- Subscription-based with monthly and yearly plans
