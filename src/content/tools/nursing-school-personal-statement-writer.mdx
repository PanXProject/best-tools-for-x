## About
Nursing School Personal Statement Writer is a free AI tool for people applying to US nursing programs: BSN, ADN, accelerated BSN, LPN, RN-to-BSN, MSN, NP, CRNA and DNP. Paste the essay prompt as the application asks it, tell your story in your own words (why nursing, what prepared you, the nurse you want to become) and pick the length: it writes a personal statement from your own facts, with why it works and what to check before you submit.

## Features
- Free to use, no sign-up
- Nothing you enter is stored
- Built on Claude by Anthropic
