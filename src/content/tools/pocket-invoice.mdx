## About
Pocket Invoice helps freelancers and small businesses create professional invoices without paying for an invoicing subscription. Use the free web generator for a quick PDF, or manage invoices through the free iOS and Android apps. Its hosted MCP connection lets compatible AI assistants work with your account after authorization. Web, mobile and MCP features are all free.

## Features
- Create and export professional PDF invoices
- Choose customizable invoice templates
- Use free iOS and Android mobile apps
- Connect compatible AI assistants through hosted MCP
- Manage clients, products, quotes and receipts
- Start with the web invoice generator without signup
