## About
Healthcare Self-Evaluation Writer is a free AI tool for nurses, nursing assistants, techs, therapists and every other healthcare role. Write what you did this year in your own words and what you want to grow in; it writes a self-evaluation for your performance review, clinical ladder or promotion packet, using only your facts. No sign-up.

## Features
- Organized by patient care and safety, teamwork, teaching, quality and growth
- Examples and goals from your own facts
- Free, no sign-up, nothing stored
