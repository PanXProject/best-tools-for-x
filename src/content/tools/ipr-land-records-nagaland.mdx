## About

Guide to land records in Nagaland on India Property Register: Record of Rights, how plots are identified, the local terms and where the records are held. DoLR lists no RoR portal for Nagaland. The state land records department says survey and RoR preparation is done for 586 villages and digitisation and map integration are in progress (Kohima and Wokha rural survey upcoming); DILRMP-MIS shows 62.54% in its CLR table but 0% on its homepage ranking.

## Features

- Explains what the state calls its land records and what each shows
- Shows how plots are identified, from the state down to the village
- Lists the government portals and sources that hold the records
