## About
Physics AI is a browser-based tutor for high school and university students. Paste a physics question or upload an image to receive a structured solution that explains formula selection, algebra, numerical substitution, units, and the underlying concept. It supports mechanics, electricity, magnetism, waves, optics, thermodynamics, and other common topics, with a permanent free tier.

## Features
- Solve physics questions from typed text or uploaded images
- Explain each formula choice and calculation step
- Check units and numerical substitutions clearly
- Cover mechanics, circuits, waves, optics, and thermodynamics
- Choose Tutor Mode for learning or Solver Mode for quick checks
- Use a permanent free tier in any modern web browser
