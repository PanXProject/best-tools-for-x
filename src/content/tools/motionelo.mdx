## About
Motionelo is an AI video generator that turns text prompts and images into short videos. Direct the shot, pick a model such as Veo 3.1, Kling 2.6 or Seedance, and see the exact credit price before you generate.

## Features
- Text to video from a written prompt
- Image to video with optional start and end frames
- Choice of published video models with per-model controls
- Exact credit price shown before each generation
- 20 Free credits for new accounts
- Prompt library and guides for writing better shots
