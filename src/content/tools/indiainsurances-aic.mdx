## About

AIC: policies, claim record and complaints. Agriculture Insurance Company of India Limited: every general insurance product with its documents, claim settlement record, and how to claim or complain. Free to read at https://indiainsurances.com/insurer/aic. IndiaInsurances reads every policy from its own documents and the IRDAI registers, takes no commission and makes no sales calls.

## Features

- Lists every general insurance product of one insurer with its documents
- Shows the insurer claim settlement record
- Explains how to claim or complain
