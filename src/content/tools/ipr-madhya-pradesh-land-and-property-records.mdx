## About

Madhya Pradesh: 52 districts and 42,930 villages. How to check land records, maps, registration and encumbrance in Madhya Pradesh, and the government portals. MP Bhulekh searches by survey number, plot number or bhumiswami within a village; certified khasra, khatauni and maps need a login, and the official host returned 504 on 11 Oct 2026.
