## About
Chrome & Edge extension that saves Amazon product videos as source-quality MP4, HD images up to 3000px and full reviews to Excel in one click.

## Features
- Downloads product, creator and A+ videos as source-quality MP4 taken from Amazon's own stream, not screen recordings
- Saves gallery, variant and A+ images as HD originals up to 3000px, with free and unlimited image downloads
- Exports a listing's reviews to .xlsx and .csv with rating, full text, author, date, verified purchase and helpful votes
- AI review analysis clusters complaints and ranks them by star impact, separating product problems from listing problems
- One-click bulk download of a whole listing into folders named by ASIN and title, with a manifest.xlsx
- Works on 20+ Amazon marketplaces including amazon.com, .co.uk, .de, .co.jp, .fr and .ca, running locally in the browser
