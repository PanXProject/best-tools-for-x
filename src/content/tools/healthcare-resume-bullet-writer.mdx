## About
Healthcare Resume Bullet Writer turns what nurses, CNAs, techs and therapists do at work into strong resume bullets: action verbs, the setting, and the clinical skills applicant tracking systems look for. It never invents numbers and leaves [#] for you to fill in.

## Features
- ATS-friendly bullets and suggested skills for healthcare roles
- Uses only what you write, no invented facts
- Free, no sign-up, nothing stored
