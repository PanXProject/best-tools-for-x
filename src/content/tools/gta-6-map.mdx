## About
GTA 6 Map is a free interactive map of Leonida for GTA 6 players. Search and filter official and community-mapped locations, add your own pins and notes, and track progress with multiple local saves plus export and import for backups. Built on the GTA 6 Mapping Project community map (CC BY 4.0). Fan-made and free; no account required.

## Features
- Interactive Leonida map with search and filters
- Personal pins and notes
- Progress tracker with multiple local saves
- Export and import backups
- Free, no account
