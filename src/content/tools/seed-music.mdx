## About
Seed Music is an AI music generator that turns a prompt, lyrics, mood, or style note into a song draft with vocals or instrumentals. Exports are royalty-free under the site's terms. Free includes 10 welcome credits and short previews; Lite is $14.90/mo; Pro is $29/mo.

## Features
- Text to music and lyrics to song generation
- Guide vocal tone and language
- Extend existing audio into longer tracks
- Export MP3 or WAV for videos, ads, podcasts, and games
- Royalty-free exports under the site's terms
