## About

A free IndiaCos list of 8,529 non-banking financial companies and asset reconstruction companies registered with the Reserve Bank of India, A to Z. Each entry shows the NBFC type (investment and credit, P2P lending, NBFC-Factor, housing finance, microfinance and more), its regulatory layer, CIN and city, and links to the company record. Browse by type from core investment companies to account aggregators.

## Features

- 8,529 RBI-registered NBFCs and ARCs
- Type and regulatory layer
- CIN and city
