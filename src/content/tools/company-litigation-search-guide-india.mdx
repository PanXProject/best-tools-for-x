## About

A free eCourtsIndia due-diligence guide to finding court cases against a company in India: search its legal and former names across courts, NCLT, DRT and tax tribunals, then triage the red flags. Part of eCourtsIndia, which lets lawyers, litigants and researchers search Indian court cases by CNR, party, advocate or judge, with daily cause lists. Not legal advice.

## Features

- Explains searching a company by legal and former names
- Covers NCLT, DRT and tax tribunals
- Explains how to triage red flags
