## About

Bihar: 38 districts and 41,633 villages. How to check land records, maps, registration and encumbrance in Bihar, and the government portals that hold them. Bihar Bhumi shows the jamabandi register, mortgaged land, mutation and LPC status and revenue maps; scanned khatiyan and maps are on Bhu-Abhilekh after OTP registration, with a fee for signed copies. DILRMP-MIS shows 100% of villages computerised (45,857).

## Features

- Lists every district of the state with its village count
- Explains how to check land records, maps, registration and encumbrance
- Links the government portals that hold the records
