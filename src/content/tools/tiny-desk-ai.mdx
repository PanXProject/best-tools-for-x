## About
Tiny Desk AI turns your photos into your own version of the viral "Outstanding" Tiny Desk meme. Pick who plays the left, center and right singers, the lead singer and the sax solo, upload 1 to 5 photos, and get an 18-second vertical video for TikTok, Reels or Shorts. Anyone you don't replace is played by our default band. Pay once per video, no subscription. Unofficial fan tool, not affiliated with NPR.

## Features
- Swap 1 to 5 friends into the band: three singers, the lead singer and the sax player
- 18-Second vertical 480p MP4, ready for TikTok, Reels and Shorts
- The band sings in time with the meme, so the original sound lines up when you add it
- No watermark and no subscription, from $8.99 per video
