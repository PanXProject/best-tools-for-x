## About
Tiny Desk AI turns 1 to 3 photos into an about 15-second, 9:16, 720p Outstanding tiny-desk concert video with the song already on. Upload one photo to sing lead, add a second for the sax solo and a third for the keyboard. Solo, Duo and Trio cost the same. No login and no subscription. Packs are one-time: 1 video $9.99, 3 videos $24.99, or 10 videos $69.99. Videos are usually ready in 3-8 minutes, with no watermark, and there is no free personal preview.

## Features
- Upload 1 to 3 photos and become the lead singer, sax player or keyboardist
- About 15-second, 9:16, 720p concert video with the song already on
- Solo, Duo and Trio all use one credit and cost the same
- No login and no subscription
- One-time packs: 1 video $9.99, 3 videos $24.99, or 10 videos $69.99
- Videos usually ready in 3-8 minutes, 720p HD, with no watermark
- Watch and download on the private order page or from the email link
- Photos are deleted from storage within 7 days
- Finished videos stay on the private order page for 30 days and are not published to a gallery
- Unused credits never expire
