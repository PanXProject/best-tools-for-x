## About
A free AI tool that generates images and videos of the same character with an identical face across every output, without LoRA training or a credit card. It supports multiple art styles, lip sync video, and multi-character scenes for creators, authors, game developers, and marketers.

## Features
- Generate consistent character images across poses and outfits
- Create videos with lip sync and motion control
- Explore multiple art styles and multi-character scenes
