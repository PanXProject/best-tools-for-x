## نبذة
منصة SnapEnv — منصة متخصصة في إدارة متغيرات البيئة والأسرار البرمجية بأعلى معايير الأمان، مصممة لفرق التطوير.

بدلاً من تداول ملفات .env عبر تطبيقات المراسلة أو تضمينها في مستودعات الكود، تُخزِّن SnapEnv جميع المتغيرات مشفَّرةً، وتُتيح سحبها بأمر واحد من أي خادم أو منظومة CI/CD أو بيئة Kubernetes.

## المميزات

- 🔒 Encrypted at rest — AES-256-GCM on every value, per-project derived keys, decryption is server-side only.

- 🤖 AI-agent-safe secret injection — give Claude Code, Cursor, or CI a command to run, never the secret itself. Values inject straight into the process and get scrubbed from anything it prints.

- 👥 Team access control — workspace + project roles, per-environment read/write overrides, protected environments by default.

- ⏮️ Version control & rollback — full change history, one-click rollback to any version, compare versions and environments, change attribution.

- 🔑 Access tokens & 2FA — tokens scoped to one project/environment (read-only or read+write, revoked instantly), plus TOTP 2FA with single-use backup codes.

- 📦 Bulk import & export — .env/CSV/JSON import & export, bulk edit across a selection, copy variables between environments.

- 🔗 Webhooks — HMAC-SHA256 signed payloads, per-event filtering, delivery log with status codes — trigger deploys or alerts on change.

- ⏰ Expiry & rotation reminders — set expiry dates with email reminders, or let SnapEnv flag secrets that just haven't been touched in 90/180 days.

- 📋 Audit log — append-only record of every pull, push, change, and team action — SOC2 and compliance-review ready.
