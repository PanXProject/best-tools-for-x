## About
Paintit.ai helps homeowners and designers explore interior concepts from room photos, briefs and reference images, then refine them through chat. Yulii Cherevko is CEO and co-founder. Free credits are available after registration, with paid plans also offered. Visual concepts require separate checks against physical samples and measurements.

## Features
- Explore room design concepts from photos and written briefs
- Refine interior ideas through conversational edits
- Search paint catalogs by brand, color name, code or HEX
- Compare colors from Behr, Benjamin Moore, Dulux, Nippon Paint and Sherwin-Williams
