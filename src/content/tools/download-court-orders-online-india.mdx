## About

A free eCourtsIndia guide to downloading court orders and judgments in India from eCourts and sci.gov.in, or across courts on eCourtsIndia, and to knowing when a certified copy is needed instead. Part of eCourtsIndia, which lets lawyers, litigants and researchers search Indian court cases by CNR, party, advocate or judge, with daily cause lists. Not legal advice.

## Features

- Explains free downloads from eCourts and sci.gov.in
- Covers search across courts on eCourtsIndia
- Explains when a certified copy is needed
