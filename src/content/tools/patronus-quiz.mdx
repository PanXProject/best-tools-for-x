## About
Patronus Quiz is a free, unofficial fan-made personality quiz. Answer 10 short questions and it tells you which Patronus animal (out of 20) best matches your personality. Unofficial fan content: not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Warner Bros. or J.K. Rowling.

## Features
- Ten short multiple-choice personality questions
- Twenty possible Patronus animal results
- Description of the traits behind each Patronus
- Free to use in the browser
