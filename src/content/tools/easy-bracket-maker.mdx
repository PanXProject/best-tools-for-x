## About
A free tournament bracket maker that runs in the browser: single and double elimination, round robin and Swiss for 3 to 128 teams, with seeding, byes, scoring, standings, printing, exports and spectator links. No account, no watermark; brackets stay on your device.

## Features
- Single elimination, double elimination and Swiss for up to 128 teams or players; round robin up to 64
- Seeds follow the order you paste names in, or shuffle for a random draw; top seeds get byes when the field is not a power of two
- Click a match to enter the score: winners move on, losers drop to the lower bracket in double elimination, standings update
- Print the bracket, export it as an image, CSV or PDF, embed it on a website or send players a spectator link
- Printable blank brackets by number of teams, with the order of play and the number of games
- Award certificates (MVP, champion, most improved) for every player
- No account, no watermark, no limit on tournaments; brackets are saved in your browser
