## About
Background Noise Remover lowers fans, hum, hiss, wind and traffic behind spoken voice in audio files and videos. Press Preview to hear the first 30 seconds of your own recording cleaned with no account, then clean the whole file if you like the result.
## Features
- Free 30-second preview of your own recording with no signup
- Works with MP3, WAV, FLAC, M4A, AAC, OGG, MP4, MOV and WebM files
- Video stays on your device and only the audio track is uploaded
- Full and Gentle settings you can compare before using a credit
- Output level near -16 LUFS for podcasts and online video
- Uploaded audio and results are deleted automatically after 24 hours
