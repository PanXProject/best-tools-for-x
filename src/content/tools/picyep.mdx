## About
PicYep is a browser-based AI image and video studio. Upload reference photos or a prompt, pick from 20+ flagship models, and produce realistic portraits, product shots, group edits with consistent faces, GIFs, SVGs and short videos.

## Features
- 20+ Flagship image and video models
- Face consistency across up to 16 references
- 9-Frame GIF and vector SVG export
- 30 Free credits after email verification
