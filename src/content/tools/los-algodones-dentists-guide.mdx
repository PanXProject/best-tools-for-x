## About

An independent English guide for US and Canadian patients planning dental care in Los Algodones (Molar City), across the border from Yuma, Arizona. Not a clinic; no prices listed. Written by Danny Rojas.

## Features

- Crossing tips for Andrade and the return to Yuma
- Questions to ask any clinic before treatment
- Checklists to compare written treatment quotes
- Plain-language guides to implants, crowns and dentures
