## About
TinyCommand brings forms, tables, workflows, email and AI agents into one no-code platform. Capture leads, organize data and automate follow-ups without moving work between separate tools. Free and paid plans are available. Custom agents require Professional or Agency, and usage draws on workspace credits.

## Features
- Build forms and store responses in connected tables
- Automate work across app integrations on a workflow canvas
- Build AI agents that use tools and pause for human approval
- Embed agents on a website or run them inside workflows
- Send email as part of connected business processes
