## About

Need to call an airline, a bank, or someone who only has a landline? Instant Web Call lets you call supported mobile and landline numbers from your browser without installing a calling app. Enter the number to see a cost estimate before you dial. You pay for calls with prepaid credit.

## Features

- Make outgoing voice calls directly from your browser.
- Call supported landline and mobile numbers internationally.
- See a number-specific cost estimate before dialing.
- Pay per use with prepaid credit.
