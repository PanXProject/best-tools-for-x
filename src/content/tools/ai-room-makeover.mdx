## About

AI Room Makeover helps you explore room design ideas from a photo of your own space. Describe a complete makeover or a focused furniture, color or material change, and compare visual concepts before changing your room. Add reference images to communicate a style or item direction, or use the conversational design Agent to develop ideas step by step. The browser-based tool uses paid one-time credit packs. Trial availability varies.

## Features

- Explore full-room makeover concepts from a room photo
- Preview focused furniture, color and material changes
- Add optional reference images to communicate a design direction
- Develop ideas through a conversational design Agent
