## About
AI Genjutsu recasts any video with your own people, pets or products. Upload a short clip or pick a trend preset, add your photos, and generate a motion-transfer or object-swap video.

## Features
- Motion transfer from a reference video
- Object and product swap
- Ready-made trend presets
- Multiple models including Seedance and Kling
