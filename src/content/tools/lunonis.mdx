## About
Lunonis calculates your full birth chart for free and explains it in plain words: every planet with sign, degree and house, the angles and the tightest aspects, for your exact birth time and place. Our own ephemeris code is checked against Swiss Ephemeris. 11 languages, no account needed.

## Features
- Free natal chart with houses, angles and aspects
- Rising, Moon, Venus, Mars and Saturn return calculators
- Couple compatibility (synastry) between two charts
- Personal PDF books and monthly or yearly forecasts written for one chart
- Works in 11 languages with local prices
