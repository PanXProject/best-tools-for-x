## About
Free Short Music Video Generator helps musicians and short-form creators turn a text prompt or start and end frames into five-second, 480p videos. Choose vertical 9:16, landscape 16:9, or square 1:1, then preview and download clips for song teasers, Reels, and YouTube Shorts. Free with no sign-up, with one generation at a time.

## Features
- Generate videos from a text prompt or two frames
- Create five-second, 480p clips
- Choose 9:16, 16:9, or 1:1 output
- Preview and download the result
- Use unlimited generations one at a time
