## About
Free daily NFL and soccer trivia: 7 prompts, 25 seconds each. Name a real player nobody else thinks of — the rarer your answer, the more it scores. Three daily games (Mix, NFL, and soccer) use the same prompts for everyone and reset at 00:00 UTC.

## Features
- Three daily games (Mix, NFL, and soccer) with the same prompts for everyone, resetting at 00:00 UTC
- Scores range from 10–100 per prompt, with 700 a perfect run, plus a colour grid to share
- Unlimited mode from 1,243 prompts (686 NFL + 557 soccer) covering 32 NFL franchises, 53 soccer clubs, and 35 men’s national teams
- Play as a web app in the browser with no account required
