## About
VIEW is a searchable directory of public Telegram channels, groups and bots, with English, Chinese and Russian interfaces. Public browsing and resource inclusion review are free. Includes adult and other sensitive categories and homepage advertising. Generated summaries or scores may be inaccurate; audience counts may be outdated, and queries may be logged. Submitted by a project-affiliated representative.

## Features
- Search public Telegram channels, groups and bots
- Browse resources by topic and category
- Explore English, Chinese and Russian interfaces
- Compare resource descriptions, languages and audience estimates
