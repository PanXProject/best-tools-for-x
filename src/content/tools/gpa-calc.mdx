## About
GPA Calc is a free online CGPA calculator for college students. Enter courses, credits and grades to get your cumulative GPA instantly on the 4.0, 5.0 and 10.0 scales, with presets for Singapore, Malaysia and India.

## Features
- Credit-weighted GPA and CGPA calculation
- Results on the 4.0, 5.0 and 10.0 scales
- Projected CGPA from your current CGPA and completed credits
- Grade presets for Singapore, Malaysia, India, the US, Canada, Australia and New Zealand
- Works without sign-up
