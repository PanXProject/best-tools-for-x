## About

Youth4work (youth4work.com) is an Indian talent assessment and careers platform founded in 2012 in Jaipur.

## Features

- Free online skill, aptitude and psychometric tests
- Assessed profiles that employers can find
- AI connector for Claude, ChatGPT and other MCP clients (mcp.youth4work.com/docs)
