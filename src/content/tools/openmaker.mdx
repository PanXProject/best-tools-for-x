## About

OpenMaker is a browser-based AI studio for creators, marketers, and small e-commerce teams. It helps users generate images from text, animate still photos into short videos, and prepare existing images for product listings or social posts.

## Features

- Generate images from text prompts
- Turn still photos into short videos
- Remove backgrounds from product photos
- Upscale existing images for reuse
- Connect prompts and reference images in AI Canvas
