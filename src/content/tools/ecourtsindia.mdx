## About

eCourtsIndia (ecourtsindia.com) is a search for court cases in India across the Supreme Court, all 25 High Courts, district and taluka courts and tribunals.

## Features

- Look up cases by CNR number, party, advocate or judge
- Daily cause lists
- Case status and hearing history
- Free to search
