## About

Meghalaya: 12 districts and 6,821 villages. How to check land records, maps, registration and encumbrance in Meghalaya, and the government portals that hold. DoLR lists no RoR portal for Meghalaya, and its DILRMP district report shows 0 RoRs and 'RoR available online for download = No' in every district. Community land under the Sixth Schedule is the working system. The NGDRS directory lists a URL but the national grid marks it data awaited.

## Features

- Lists every district of the state with its village count
- Explains how to check land records, maps, registration and encumbrance
- Links the government portals that hold the records
