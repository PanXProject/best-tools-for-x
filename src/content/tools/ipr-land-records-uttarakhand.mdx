## About

Guide to land records in Uttarakhand on India Property Register: Jamabandi / Khatauni, Khasra / Gata, how plots are identified, the local terms and where the records are held. e-Bhulekh shows public RoR after a captcha, searched by khasra/gata, khata or khatedar name, with real-time mutation (dakhil-kharij) tracking; E-Bhunaksha gives the cadastral map and an RoR copy. DILRMP-MIS shows 99.23% of villages computerised (16,562 of 16,691). Deeds run on the state CRS stack.

## Features

- Explains what the state calls its land records and what each shows
- Shows how plots are identified, from the state down to the village
- Lists the government portals and sources that hold the records
