## About
Spreadsheet to App turns Excel, CSV, or Google Sheets into hosted web apps with AI. Describe your workflow, refine the interface in Studio, inspect generated code, test a private preview, and publish. Business records stay in Google Sheets. Uploaded files become Google Sheets in your account; existing sheets connect directly.

## Features
- Import Excel and CSV into your Google Sheets
- Connect existing Google Sheets as business data
- Build and refine app interfaces with AI
- Inspect and edit generated project code
- Test a private preview before publishing
- Publish hosted web apps for your workflow
