## About
Manag.io (مانجيو) is an Arabic web app for service businesses and agencies. It links income and expenses to every project and every client, so you see the real profit and margin of each one instantly, with tasks, team, invoices and quotes in one dashboard. It has a free plan, plus free online tools: an Arabic invoice generator, a quote generator and a profit calculator.

## Features
- Profit and margin per project and per client
- Income and expense tracking linked to projects
- Tasks and team management
- Invoices and quotes with shareable links and PDF
- Free Arabic invoice generator, quote generator and profit calculator
- Full Arabic (RTL) interface with English support
