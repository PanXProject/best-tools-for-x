## About

Sikkim: 6 districts and 452 villages. How to check land records, maps, registration and encumbrance in Sikkim, and the government portals that hold them. Sikkim's ILRMS is an application system that issues Parcha/RoR after a request, not a public search. DILRMP-MIS shows 83.25% of villages computerised (348 of 418). Sikkim has a blank cell on the NGDRS registration directory.

## Features

- Lists every district of the state with its village count
- Explains how to check land records, maps, registration and encumbrance
- Links the government portals that hold the records
