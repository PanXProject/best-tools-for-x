## About
Gangly is a sales workflow system for B2B reps and founders doing outbound. It connects signal detection, outreach writing, call prep, live call coaching, post-call notes, and CRM hygiene into one flow. Plans from $99/seat/month, with a 14-day free trial and no credit card required.

## Features
- Signal detection that ranks accounts by warmth
- Outreach writer tied to source-linked signals
- Call prep briefs with objections and battle cards
- Live call coaching on Zoom and Google Meet
- Post-call notes with CRM-ready summaries
- CRM hygiene engine for stale deals and missing fields
- Workflow sequencer connecting every stage
