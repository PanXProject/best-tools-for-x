## About
Ad Studio is an AI creative agent for ecommerce. It reads your product page, reviews and competitor sites, proposes an angle and format, then creates static ads, UGC videos and product videos for your approval.

## Features
- AI agent researches products, reviews and competitor sites
- Static ads with headlines, offers and brand styling
- UGC videos with AI presenters, voices and scripts
- Video editing with captions, music and sound effects
- Ad Radar surfaces long-running ads in your category
- Formats for 1:1, 4:5 and 9:16 placements
- Product links from Shopify, WooCommerce and Amazon
- Save products in a reusable catalog
