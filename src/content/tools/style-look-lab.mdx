## About
Style Look Lab helps people explore personal style with free quizzes, photo-based seasonal color analysis, and AI hairstyle previews. Results support personal exploration, with uncertainty and limitations explained. Free quizzes and limited previews are available, with optional paid reports and credit packs.

## Features
- Explore a likely color season with a no-photo, no-sign-up quiz
- Compare seasonal color palettes using photo-based analysis
- Preview selected haircuts and hair colors using AI
