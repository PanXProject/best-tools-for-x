## About
MotifDeck is an AI game art workspace for characters, props and scenes in multiple styles. Turn a character image into a motion video, inspect extracted transparent frames, then choose their order and timing. The free plan has a limited preview; generated-file downloads need a paid unlock.

## Features
- Generate motion video from a character image
- Extract and review transparent character frames
- Select frames and adjust their order and timing
