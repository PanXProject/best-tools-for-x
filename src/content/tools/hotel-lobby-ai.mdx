## About
Hotel Lobby AI turns two authorized portrait photos into an AI duet video on an orange studio stage. Choose left and right portraits, optionally describe movements or expressions, and download the generated MP4. It suits creators exploring short performance videos. Generation requires sign-in and payment per video; there is no free generation. Results vary, and a specific commercial song is not guaranteed.

## Features
- Two portraits with left and right positions
- Optional movement and expression directions
- Portrait and landscape layout choices
- Output quality selection before generation
- MP4 preview and download
