## About

7,864 companies and LLPs whose insolvency professional publishes a list of claims with the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of India, with the process each is. Useful for creditors, lenders and anyone checking a counterparty. Free to browse at https://indiacos.com/insolvency/claims. IndiaCos is a free directory of Indian companies, LLPs and GST registrations built from public MCA, GST, RBI, SEBI and IBBI records.

## Features

- Lists IBBI insolvency notices of one process type
- Newest first
- Links each to the company or LLP record
