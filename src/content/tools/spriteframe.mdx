## About
SpriteFrame is a sprite sheet maker for game developers and pixel artists. Upload a full-body character, describe the motion (idle, walk, run, attack, etc.), and AI generates the animation frames.

You can also combine your own PNG frames or extract frames from an MP4 in the browser. Export a PNG sheet or frame sequence with JSON timing for Godot, Unity, and other engines.

## Features
- AI animation frames from a full-body character image
- Combine your own PNG frames into a sprite sheet
- Extract frames from MP4 videos in the browser
- Export PNG sprite sheets or frame sequences
- JSON timing data for Godot, Unity and other engines
