## About
PersonWise is an AI course-creation engine that turns a finished slide deck, a topic, or source materials into an interactive course presented by a digital human, with learner voice questions answered from the course content.

## Features
- Turns a topic, materials, or your own PowerPoint into an interactive course
- Presents slides through a digital human narrator
- Answers learner voice questions grounded in the course content
- Includes assessments with progress tracking, resumable on any device
- Shares through a public link or an embed
