## About

JurisGrid (jurisgrid.com) publishes an llms.txt file so AI assistants and researchers can read its index in plain text: the 12 legal tech sectors with company counts, key market facts with sources (Tracxn counts more than 1,000 legaltech companies in India, about 800 active) and how to request a free listing. Maintained by the eCourtsIndia team.

- Plain-text index for AI assistants
