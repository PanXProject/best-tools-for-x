## About
AIMarket24 is a curated marketplace where creators sell AI agents, chatbots, n8n/Make workflows, MCP servers and prompt packs. Listing is free; creators only pay a commission on sales. Buyers get escrow-protected payments, verified listings and instant delivery. Launching Oct 30, 2026.

## Features
- Free listing for creators with commission only on sales
- Escrow-protected payments for buyers
- Verified listings of AI agents and workflows
- Instant delivery of purchased products
