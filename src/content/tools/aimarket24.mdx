## About
AIMarket24 is a marketplace for AI agents, automations (n8n, Make), prompt packs and small AI tools. Creators list for free and keep most of each sale; buyers get clear descriptions, demos and support from the maker. Built for indie AI builders who want a simple place to sell what they made.

## Features
- Free listings for creators who sell AI agents and workflows
- Clear descriptions, demos and support from the maker
- Marketplace for n8n and Make automations and prompt packs
- Simple discovery of tested AI tools for buyers
