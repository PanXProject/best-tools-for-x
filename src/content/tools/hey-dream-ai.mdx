## About

Hey Dream AI is a browser-based platform for creating videos from text prompts or photos and generating or editing images. Its free image-to-video tool lets you choose the opening and closing frames and describe the motion between them. Free tools are available alongside paid credit options.

## Features

- Generate videos from written prompts.
- Animate images using a start frame, an end frame, and a motion prompt.
- Generate and edit images with multiple AI models.
- Preview and download clips from the free image-to-video tool.
