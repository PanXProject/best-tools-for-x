## About
Flyne AI’s free video generator turns a text prompt or a pair of first and last frame images into a short video. It creates 5-second clips at 480p and supports 16:9, 9:16, and 1:1 aspect ratios. The page says no account, credits, or watermark are required, and it has no total generation-count cap.

## Features
- Generate a video from a written prompt without uploading images.
- Guide a clip with first-frame and end-frame images.
- Choose landscape, portrait, or square framing.
- Create 5-second, 480p clips without an account or watermark.
