## About

Land is a state subject in India, so each of the 28 states and 8 union territories keeps its own land records, maps and registration system. India Property Register covers all 36, with 783 districts and 5,86,031 villages, and shows the villages, plots and city properties on the register in each state. Free to browse.

- 36 States and UTs
