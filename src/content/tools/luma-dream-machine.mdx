## About
Luma Dream Machine is a browser-based AI video generator for creating cinematic clips from text prompts or uploaded images. Creators can generate polished 1080p videos quickly, explore styles, and turn ideas or photos into motion without editing experience.

## Features
- Text-to-video generation from written prompts
- Image-to-video animation for uploaded photos
- Cinematic visual styles and prompt controls
- Fast browser-based AI video creation
- Free credits to try before upgrading
