## About
Healthcare Resume ATS Checker is a free AI tool for anyone applying to US healthcare jobs. Paste the job posting and your resume: it shows which of the posting's licenses, certifications, unit experience and keywords your resume already has, which are missing, and how to word what you really have so a hospital's applicant tracking system finds it. It never suggests claiming a credential you do not hold. No sign-up.

## Features
- Matching and missing keywords, licenses and skills
- Required versus preferred items
- Wording fixes that stay true to your experience
- Free, no sign-up, nothing stored
