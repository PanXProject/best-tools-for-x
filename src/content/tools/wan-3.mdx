## About
Wan 3 takes an illustrated page as the reference and adds soft motion, a moon rising or leaves falling, while keeping the drawing style and characters consistent from page to page. Picture-book authors and parent creators can animate story illustrations into calm, short clips for bedtime reading. Each piece runs up to 30 seconds and a page can be extended when the text runs longer. It runs online with nothing to install, and a free start requires no card.

## Features
- Animate an illustrated page with soft, calm motion
- Keeps drawing style and characters consistent across pages
- Attach recorded narration as audio to shape a clip
- Clips up to 30 seconds that can be extended
