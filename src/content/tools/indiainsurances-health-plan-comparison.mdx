## About

A free IndiaInsurances table of 266 health insurance (hospitalisation) policies sold in India, comparing the pre-existing disease waiting period, specific illness waiting period and other clauses side by side. IndiaInsurances reads every policy from its own documents and the IRDAI registers, takes no commission and makes no sales calls.

## Features

- Reads each policy from its own documents
- Lets you sort columns or compare two policies
- Takes no commission and makes no sales calls
