## About

Max Maker AI helps content creators and online sellers turn text prompts or still photos into short videos for social posts and product demos. In the same browser workspace, users can generate images, remove backgrounds from product photos, and preview outfits on an uploaded photo.

## Features

- Generate short videos from written prompts
- Animate still photos into short clips
- Generate and edit images in a browser
- Remove backgrounds from product photos
- Preview outfits on uploaded photos
