## About
The AI team that moves your business forward. Ember is an AI team built for entrepreneurs and founders. It helps you think, decide and execute across strategy, fundraising, sales, content creation and business operations. Ember combines multiple AI models, your company context and end-to-end workflows in one platform.

## Features
- Second Brain: the central conversation that knows your project and reads your documents and connected apps
- Fund Your Growth: structure your strategy and prepare a funding file
- Creation: pitch decks, documents and images in your brand
- Lead Intelligence: define targets and prepare a credible approach
- Visibility: turn your expertise into consistent content
