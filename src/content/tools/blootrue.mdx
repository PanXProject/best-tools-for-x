## About
BlooTrue offers free review widgets for any website. Embed reviews from 21 platforms: Google, Trustpilot, Yelp, Facebook, TripAdvisor, Airbnb, Angi, Glassdoor, Agoda, BBB, OpenTable, RateMDs, Capterra, G2, Indeed, TrustRadius, Clutch, Expedia, Booking.com, Etsy and Google Play. Blend sources into one all-in-one widget. 100+ styles, auto-translated into 68+ languages. Add it to WordPress, Shopify, Wix, Squarespace, Webflow or any HTML site with one line of code. Free plan, unlimited views.
