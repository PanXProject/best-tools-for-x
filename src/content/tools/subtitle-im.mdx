## About
subtitle.im manages existing YouTube captions in Chrome. Choose follow YouTube, automatically on or automatically off across videos. Local OCR helps handle duplicate soft captions by your settings. Free 10-video trial; optional Pro. It does not generate, translate, download or remove burned-in subtitles.

## Features
- Keep YouTube captions on or off across videos
- Detect burned-in subtitles locally with OCR
- Handle duplicate soft captions according to your settings
- Restore soft captions anytime while watching
