## About
subtitle.im controls YouTube captions and handles duplicates with local OCR. Upcoming 1.6 adds transcript reading, search, seek, copy and downloads from available captions: free TXT; Pro SRT, VTT, Markdown and browser print/save-as-PDF. No caption-less transcription. Cumulative 10-video duplicate-handling trial, no signup; optional Pro adds continued handling and video/channel rules.

## Features
- Caption modes: Follow YouTube, Auto-open and Auto-close
- Local duplicate-caption handling and restoration
- Upcoming 1.6: read, search, seek and copy available captions
- Upcoming 1.6: free TXT downloads; Pro SRT, VTT, Markdown and print/save PDF
- No caption generation, translation or removal of burned-in text
