## About
BeatMV is an AI music video generator for musicians, creators and labels. Upload a song or paste a link, pick a visual style, and BeatMV creates a music video synced to the beat, with lyric videos, visualizers and cinematic scenes. Export for YouTube, TikTok, Reels and Spotify Canvas. Free to try.

## Features
- Turn a song or link into beat-synced music videos
- Add lyric videos, visualizers, and cinematic scenes
- Choose visual styles and export for YouTube, TikTok, Reels, and Spotify Canvas
- Create music videos without camera or editing skills
