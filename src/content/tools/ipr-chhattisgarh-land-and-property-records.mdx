## About

Chhattisgarh: 33 districts and 17,980 villages. How to check land records, maps, registration and encumbrance in Chhattisgarh, and the government portals. Bhuiyan's citizen interface searches agricultural, diverted and nazul land by village or khasra ID, by name fragment or khasra number, and accepts applications for digitally signed B-I and P-II. DILRMP-MIS shows 99.79% of villages computerised and BhuNaksha CG gives village maps and plot reports.

## Features

- Lists every district of the state with its village count
- Explains how to check land records, maps, registration and encumbrance
- Links the government portals that hold the records
