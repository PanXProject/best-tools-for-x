## About
videosi is a browser-based AI video and image tool for creators. Animate a photo, generate video from text, or pair a character photo with a reference clip for an AI body swap that follows its motion. The character swap tool replaces a subject in a still image.

## Features
- Generate clips from photos and text
- Transfer motion from reference video to a character
- Swap a subject in a still image
