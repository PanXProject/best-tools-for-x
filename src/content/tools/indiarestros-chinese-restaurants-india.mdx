## About

A free IndiaRestros directory of 2,92,503 Chinese and Indo-Chinese restaurants across Indian cities, with phone numbers, timings, menus with rupee prices and photos, by city and area. Find where to eat hakka noodles, manchurian or momos near you. Part of IndiaRestros, a guide to restaurants, cafes, cloud kitchens and dhabas with a free open API and MCP server.
