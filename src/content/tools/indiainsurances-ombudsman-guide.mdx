## About

A plain guide to the Insurance Ombudsman in India: go to it after the insurer rejects your complaint, does not reply within one month or replies and you are not satisfied, and within one year of that. It is free, you do not need a lawyer, and it can award up to Rs 50 lakh. Lists the offices and the steps. Information, not advice; part of IndiaInsurances.
