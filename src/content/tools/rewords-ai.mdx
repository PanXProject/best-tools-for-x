## About
ReWords AI is an AI writing assistant for rewording, rewriting, and polishing content while keeping the original meaning. It helps produce clear, natural reworded text for blogs, product pages, emails, and everyday writing.

## Features
- Rewrite and reword text while preserving meaning
- Polish writing for clarity and natural flow
- Works for blogs, product pages, emails, and daily writing
- Easy to use: paste text and get a clean reworded result
