## About
Ramalama AI creates Halloween costume walk videos from one photo of a person, cat, or dog. Upload a clear image, choose Solo Walk or Pet Walk, and pick an available costume. Results are private, watermark-free, vertical HD MP4s designed for TikTok, Reels, and Shorts.

## Features
- Create a costume walk video from a single photo
- Solo Walk and Pet Walk modes for people, cats, and dogs
- Low-angle entrance that rises to reveal the outfit
- Private, watermark-free vertical HD MP4 downloads
- One-off purchases with no account needed, from $6.99
