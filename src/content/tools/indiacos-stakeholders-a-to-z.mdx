## About

A free IndiaCos index of 40,11,792 names of directors, LLP partners and proprietors found in Indian company and GST records, A to Z, with the businesses listed under each name. Useful for KYC and background checks. IndiaCos is a free directory of Indian companies, LLPs and GST registrations built from public MCA, GST, RBI, SEBI and IBBI records.

## Features

- Built from public MCA, GST, RBI, SEBI and IBBI records
- Free to browse without a login
- Links each entry to the business record
