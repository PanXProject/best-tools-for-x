## About
PawLine gives pet owners research-backed AI guidance shaped around each pet's profile, including breed, age, weight and health conditions. It also keeps medication reminders and daily health notes in one place. Supportive information, not a replacement for professional veterinary care.

## Features
- Research-backed answers to everyday pet-care questions
- Personalized guidance based on each pet's profile
- Medication schedules, reminders and dosing history
- Daily health notes to share with your veterinarian
- Available on iOS, Android and the web
