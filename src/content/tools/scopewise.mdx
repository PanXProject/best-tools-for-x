## About
Scopewise gives freelancers practical browser-based tools for pricing projects, checking proposals, defining statements of work, managing scope changes and following up on overdue invoices. The free tools make commercial boundaries visible before work starts or payment becomes a dispute. Optional fixed-price human reviews are offered only after fit is confirmed.

## Features
- Calculates project prices, rates, retainers and scope-creep impact
- Generates structured proposals, statements of work and change orders
- Checks proposals for missing commercial boundaries and risky wording
- Creates GST invoices, payment terms and overdue-payment reminders
- Keeps core calculators and generators free without an account
