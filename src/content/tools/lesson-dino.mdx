## About
Lesson Dino is an AI lesson planner for teachers. Pick a subject, a year group and a topic, and it builds a matching lesson plan, a slide deck, a printable A4 worksheet with an answer sheet, and a short list of YouTube clips showing which part of each one to play. It needs no pupil data. Lesson Dino covers early years to sixth form, is free during the beta, and is made in Malta.

## Features
- Lesson plan from a subject, year group and topic (Word or PDF)
- Matching slide deck (PowerPoint download)
- Printable A4 worksheet with an answer sheet
- Short YouTube clips picked for the topic, with the part to play
- Researches the topic first; every fact numbered with its source
- Says so when it can't find anything
- No pupil data needed
- Early years to sixth form
