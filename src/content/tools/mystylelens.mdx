## About
MyStyleLens is a free AI face analyzer for exploring face shape, symmetry and proportions, with hairstyle and glasses guidance.

## Features
- Browser-based facial landmark analysis
- Face shape, symmetry and proportion tools
- Hairstyle and glasses guidance
- On-device scan photo processing
- English and Chinese pages
