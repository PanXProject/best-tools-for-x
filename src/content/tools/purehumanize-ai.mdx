## About
PureHumanize.ai is an AI text humanizer that rewrites drafts from ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini into natural-sounding prose while keeping your keywords and structure. Paste a draft, choose how strongly to rewrite it, and export the result to TXT, PDF or DOCX. Free to try, with paid plans for higher volume.

## Features
- Rewrites AI-generated drafts into natural-sounding prose
- Keeps your keywords and structure
- Choose how strongly to rewrite
- Export to TXT, PDF or DOCX
