## About
PureHumanize.ai provides an AI text humanizer that preserves keywords and structure while making text read more naturally. It works on blog posts, emails, reports, social captions and academic drafts. Content marketers use it to finish AI-assisted blog posts. Try it free; upgrade for higher limits.

## Features
- Preserves keywords and structure while humanizing text
- Works on blog posts, emails, reports and social captions
- Supports academic drafts and long-form content
- Downloads results as plain text, PDF or DOCX
- Free tier with upgrades for higher limits
