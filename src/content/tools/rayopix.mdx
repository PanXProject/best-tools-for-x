## About
Rayopix removes TikTok matcha-style color filters from uploaded photos and rebuilds natural-looking color, lighting, and texture with AI. It is made for creators and anyone who wants a realistic version of a stylized social-media image.

## Features
- AI reconstruction of natural photo color
- Matcha-filter green cast removal
- Restored texture and balanced lighting
