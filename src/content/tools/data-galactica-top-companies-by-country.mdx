## About

Data Galactica lists the best-known organisations of 239 countries and territories: the ones its sources say most about, up to 100 per country, each with a permanent DGC- Galactica ID, a grade and its sources. Sources include India's MCA master data, UK Companies House, the GLEIF LEI register, US SEC EDGAR and Wikidata.
