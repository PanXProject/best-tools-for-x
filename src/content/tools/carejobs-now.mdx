## About
CareJobs.Now is a free job board for everyone who works in US healthcare. It reads the career sites of 258 hospitals, health systems, clinic groups and care companies every day, so its 170,000+ openings are real and current, and it shows the pay each employer posts. Search by job, state, shift and pay, then apply on the employer's own site.

## Features
- 170,000+ Open healthcare jobs re-checked daily
- Pay posted by each employer
- Browse by job type, state, town or employer
- Apply directly on the employer's own site
