## About
Cleanup Pictures is a browser-based AI photo cleaner. Upload a picture, brush over an unwanted object, person, text, or blemish, and review the reconstructed background before downloading. On-device cleanup is available, with enhanced AI processing and larger credit plans for more work.

## Features
- Brush-based object and text removal
- AI background reconstruction
- Before-and-after result review
