## About

New India Assurance: policies, claim record and complaints. The New India Assurance Company Limited: every general insurance product with its documents, claim settlement record, and how to claim or complain. Free to read at https://indiainsurances.com/insurer/new-india-assurance. IndiaInsurances reads every policy from its own documents and the IRDAI registers, takes no commission and makes no sales calls.

## Features

- Lists every general insurance product of one insurer with its documents
- Shows the insurer claim settlement record
- Explains how to claim or complain
