## About
NewbornPhotos creates AI newborn photo scenes from one baby photo. Choose from 12 preset styles or describe a custom scene, generate previews in the browser, and pay for selected watermark-free downloads. Results are generated images, not an in-person photography session.

## Features
- Generate newborn photo scenes from a baby photo
- Choose 12 preset styles or describe custom scenes
- Preview results before purchasing selected watermark-free downloads
