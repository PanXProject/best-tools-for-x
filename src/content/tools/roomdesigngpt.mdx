## About
RoomDesignGPT is an AI room design tool that transforms a single photo of any room into photorealistic interior and exterior redesigns across 60+ styles while preserving the original layout.

## Features
- Upload one room photo and get instant AI redesigns
- Choose from 60+ interior and exterior style presets
- Preserves original room layout and structure
- Export high-resolution photorealistic renders
