## About
VideoCutout separates foreground subjects from MP4, MOV, WebM and GIF videos with AI. Export transparent WebM or add your own image background. Review fine edges and movement. Account and email verification required: five one-time trial credits, 50MB free file limit. Processing costs one credit per started second, minimum three; further use needs paid packs or subscriptions.

## Features
- AI foreground separation
- No green screen required
- MP4, MOV, WebM and GIF inputs
- Transparent WebM output
- Custom JPG, PNG or WebP background
