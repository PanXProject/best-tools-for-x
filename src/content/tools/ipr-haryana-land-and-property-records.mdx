## About

Haryana: 22 districts and 6,467 villages. How to check land records, maps, registration and encumbrance in Haryana, and the government portals that hold them. WEB-HALRIS runs at all 143 tehsils and sub-tehsils since 2018; jamabandi and mutations are searched by owner name, khewat and khasra, but jamabandi.nic.in returned 504 on 11 Oct 2026.
