## About
APOB AI is a web creative platform for personalized AI characters, images, short videos and talking avatars. Creators can use text or image inputs and lip sync workflows. An account and generation credits are required, with limited free access and optional paid plans.

## Features
- Create personalized AI characters and images
- Generate short videos from text or images
- Make talking avatars with lip sync
