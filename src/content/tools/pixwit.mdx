## About
Pixwit is an all-in-one AI video studio. The line on the site is Invent Reality: don’t only describe the scene, direct it.

Two main paths. Text to video turns a written scene, motion, and mood into a clip. Image to video takes a product shot, portrait, or illustration and adds camera and motion. Both are aimed at work you can use — ads, avatars, social clips, longer sequences — not a five-second toy.

## Features
- Text to video — Prompt a scene, motion, and mood; get a clip.
Image to video — Animate a product shot, portrait, or illustration with camera and motion.
Multiple models in one studio — Switch generation models and parameters without a new site per look.
Effect templates — Seasonal, dance, transformation, and similar looks without building every style from scratch.
Long-form / idea to video — Idea → script → shots; adjust characters, script, and sequence in conversation.
Script to video — Turn a full script into structured, scene-by-scene video.
Reference image to video — Keep visual consistency from a reference still.
Start–end frame — Transition between two images into a continuous clip.
Motion control — Direct camera and movement.
AI clip generator — Short standalone clips from text or image.

Several generation models sit in one studio so you are not opening a new subscription for every look. Switch models and settings for cinematic narrative, product motion, stylized animation, or a fast draft. The in-app list is current; the roster changes.

Longer work can go idea → script → shots. You can change character design, rewrite the script, and restage cuts in conversation. Effect templates (seasonal looks, dance, transformation, and similar styles) lock a look faster.

The same product also covers script-to-video, start–end frame transitions, motion control, UGC-style ads, product-link-to-video, and a built-in editor. Closer to a desk you work on than a generator that dumps one file.

It fits people who need a few ad or product tests, avatar clips with a reasonably stable face or character, storyboard-to-longer-form work, or text and image in the same tool. If you only want one vendor’s model at full depth, that vendor’s own site is usually more direct. Pixwit is for folding the workflow first.
