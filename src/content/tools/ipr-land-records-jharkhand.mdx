## About

Guide to land records in Jharkhand on India Property Register: Khata, how plots are identified, the local terms and where the records are held. JharBhoomi shows khatas by district, anchal and mauza, searchable by name, khesra, khata number or holder name, with a captcha. DILRMP-MIS shows 99.36% of villages computerised. Deeds run on NGDRS (Jhar Nibandhan) since 2018. Register coverage: 31,845 villages, 2,45,36,076 plots on the register.

## Features

- Explains what the state calls its land records and what each shows
- Shows how plots are identified, from the state down to the village
- Lists the government portals and sources that hold the records
