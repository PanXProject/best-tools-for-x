## About

Prove your application is safe and secure. Genbounty is the AI application safety platform for testing AI-assisted code, models, agents, and software integrations. Human-led testing, encrypted findings, and flexible payouts via self-serve program balance or managed fiat.

## Features

- [Genbounty for LLMs](https://genbounty.com/genbounty-for-llms.md): Full platform brief.
- [Home](https://genbounty.com): The AI application safety platform.
- [For companies](https://genbounty.com/for-companies): AI application safety and security testing for enterprise products.
- [AI Application Safety Platform](https://genbounty.com/ai-safety-bug-bounty-platform): Private invite-only programs.
- [AI-Generated Application Security](https://genbounty.com/ai-generated-applications): Dual-vector security testing for applications built with AI coding tools (Cursor, Copilot, Claude Code, Devin) and powered by AI models, agents, and MCP servers.
- [AI bug bounty](https://genbounty.com/ai-bug-bounty): Bug bounty programs for AI, LLMs, and agents.
- [Bug bounty for AI agents](https://genbounty.com/bug-bounty-for-ai-agents): Agent, tool, and MCP testing.
- [Independent AI adversarial testing](https://genbounty.com/independent-ai-adversarial-testing): External continuous testing.
- [Pricing](https://genbounty.com/pricing): Points-only, a 250 USDC private pilot, listed team rates in USDC, and LLM Hunter licenses in USDC.
- [Teams](https://genbounty.com/teams): Vetted AI safety testing teams. Each team lists a USDC rate.
- [Pay a hired team](https://genbounty.com/support/get-started/paying-a-hired-team-with-escrow): Bug bounty payments sit in the company's program balance contract on Base. Genbounty never signs a transaction. The company and the testing team move the USDC on-chain.
- [Collect a hire payment](https://genbounty.com/support/get-started/collecting-hire-payment): Testers collect their USDC share to a saved 0x address after both sides confirm.
