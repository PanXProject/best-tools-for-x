## About
Text Match Cut Art is a free browser-based text match cut generator. Enter any phrase up to 23 characters, pick a highlight color, and generate a short newspaper-style montage where your phrase stays locked in place while headlines flash around it. No account, ads, or watermark required.

## Features
- Type up to 23 characters and pick a highlight color
- Export 1:1, 9:16, or 16:9 aspect ratio clips
- Tune pace, zoom, blur, and sound or silence
- Export as MP4 or WebM depending on browser support
- No account, ads, credits, or watermark for the generator
