## About

Menlo Business Insurance Cost Calculator is a free public budgeting tool. It uses business details to show illustrative insurance premium ranges. The calculation is deterministic, not AI, and the estimates are not carrier quotes or offers of coverage. No account or payment is required to use it.

## Features

- Explore illustrative business insurance premium ranges
- Adjust business details to compare budgeting scenarios
- Use the public calculator without creating an account
