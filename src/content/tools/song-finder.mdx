## About

Hum to Search is an online song finder that helps identify music from humming, singing, or recorded audio. Use it when you remember a melody but not the song title, or want to identify music playing nearby.

## Features

- Humming Search: Search for a song by humming its melody.
- Singing Search: Sing a recognizable section to look for a match.
- Recording Recognition: Identify music from a recording.
- Audio Upload: Upload an audio clip for a song search.
- Two Search Modes: Choose humming or recorded music recognition.
- Browser-Based Access: Search through the website without a separate app.
