## About
As an integrated multimodal AI foundation, Flux 3 generates still images, moving clips and matched audio via one network. It supports image animation, video transformation, keyframe control, clip extension and multi-shot chaining with natural motion, sound and multilingual dialogue for short videos, storyboards and product visuals.

 Flux 3 unifies images, motion footage and native audio, supporting clip extension and multi-shot editing with consistent characters for ads, films and animations
