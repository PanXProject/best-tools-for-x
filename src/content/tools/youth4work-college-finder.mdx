## About

The college section of Youth4work, India's talent assessment and careers platform (founded 2012, Jaipur). Students explore and compare colleges and courses, use study material and ask questions in college forums, then take free online skill, aptitude and psychometric tests and publish an assessed profile that employers can find.

## Features

- Explore and compare colleges
- College forums
- Free skill tests
