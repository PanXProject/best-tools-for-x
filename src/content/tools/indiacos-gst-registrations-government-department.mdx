## About

List of 31,658 government department registrations in India under GST, with GSTIN, status and location. The page covers GST registrations whose constitution of business is government department, so you can see how many such taxpayers exist and look each one up by GSTIN. Free to browse at https://indiacos.com/constitution/government-department. IndiaCos is a free directory of Indian companies, LLPs and GST registrations built from public MCA, GST, RBI, SEBI and IBBI records.

## Features

- Lists GST registrations of one constitution of business
- Shows GSTIN, status and location
- Built from public GST records
