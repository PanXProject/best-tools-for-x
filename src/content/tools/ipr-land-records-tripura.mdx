## About

Guide to land records in Tripura on India Property Register: Khatian, how plots are identified, the local terms and where the records are held. JAMI serves khatian, sabek plot search, online mutation and certified RoR, and public BhuNaksha covers 18 of 32 circles. DILRMP-MIS shows 100% on records, maps and map linkage. Tripura is one of nine ULI land-record states and runs NGDRS since 2021. Register coverage: 836 villages, 12,02,901 plots on the register.

## Features

- Explains what the state calls its land records and what each shows
- Shows how plots are identified, from the state down to the village
- Lists the government portals and sources that hold the records
