## About
ChooseCompute is an evidence-backed buying guide and price comparison service for NAS systems and NAS hard drives sold new in the United States. It helps households, Plex users, and homelab builders choose an enclosure and exact drives, with dated retailer prices and clear limits. Contact: support@choosecompute.com

## Features
- Compare exact NAS systems and CMR hard drives side by side
- Dated price checks across verified US retailers
- Buying guides for home backup, Plex, and homelab
- Capacity calculations for drive and enclosure combinations
