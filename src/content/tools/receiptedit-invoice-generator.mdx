## About
ReceiptEdit Invoice Generator is a free browser tool for freelancers and small businesses preparing client invoices. Enter the work and billing details, review the document, and download PDF or PNG without creating an account. It is a document generator, not a payment processor or accounting ledger. Submitted by the ReceiptEdit Team.

## Features
- Edit business and client details, invoice dates, and item rows.
- Choose from ten invoice layouts and font styles.
- Add an optional business logo and adjust tax or discounts.
- Review changes in the live document preview before downloading.
- Download the finished invoice as PDF or PNG without signup.
