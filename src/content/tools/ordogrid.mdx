## About
OrdoGrid helps freelancers and small businesses manage Peppol e-invoicing, quotes, credit notes, receipts, customers, projects, Kanban boards and time tracking in one web application. Based in Namur, Belgium, it supports English, French, Dutch and German with EU hosting. The permanent free plan includes unlimited incoming Peppol invoices and one outgoing invoice per month. Paid plans start at EUR 9 per month excluding VAT.

## Features
- Send and receive Peppol electronic invoices
- Prepare quotes and credit notes
- Manage customer records and receipts
- Organize projects with Kanban boards
- Track working time
- Work in English, French, Dutch or German
