## About
RemoveText removes visible words from images and reconstructs the covered background with AI. It supports JPG, PNG and WebP, batches of up to 10 images, and optional instructions for selected text. Use it to clean product photos, posters, memes and screenshots. Initial free usage is limited; paid subscriptions or credit packs are available.
