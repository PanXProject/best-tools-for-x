## About
PiSpace Costs is a free online cost-of-living reference. It shows official rent, household budget and salary figures for more than 15,000 places, with city comparisons, an equivalent-salary calculator and moving-budget tools for people planning a relocation or comparing salary offers.

## Features
- Official rent, household budget and salary figures
- Coverage of more than 15,000 places
- Side-by-side city comparisons
- Equivalent-salary calculator
- Moving-budget planning tools
