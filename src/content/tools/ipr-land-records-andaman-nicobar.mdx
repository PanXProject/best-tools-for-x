## About

Guide to land records in Andaman & Nicobar on India Property Register: Property card (SVAMITVA), how plots are identified, the local terms and where the records are held. Dweep Bhoomi is a transaction portal behind signup (mutation, demarcation, subdivision, diversion, mortgage) with no public record search. DILRMP-MIS shows 100% computerisation (205 villages). Deeds run on NGDRS since 2018, and TNRERA covers RERA here.

## Features

- Explains what the state calls its land records and what each shows
- Shows how plots are identified, from the state down to the village
- Lists the government portals and sources that hold the records
