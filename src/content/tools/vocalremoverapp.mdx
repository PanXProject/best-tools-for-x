## About
VocalRemoverApp splits a song into two files: the singing voice (an acapella) and everything else (an instrumental). The free mode runs the AI model in your own browser, so the song never leaves your computer, with no account and no daily limit.

## Features
- Free mode runs in your browser with no upload, no account and no daily limit
- Full-length MP3 vocals and instrumental with no watermark
- HQ cloud mode with a newer model that works on phones and adds lossless WAV
- Free 30-second HQ comparison clip after a free run
- Audio and video input up to 500 MB, songs up to 10 minutes free and 30 minutes in HQ
- HQ files are deleted after 24 hours and never used for training
