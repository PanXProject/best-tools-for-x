## About

India Property Register page for Medinipur West district in West Bengal: 21 blocks, 5,659 villages and 53 pincodes, with land records, village maps and property information for every village, plus towns and wards. India Property Register is an independent register of land and property in India built from public government records, with a page for every state, district, tehsil and village. Free to read.

## Features

- Lists every tehsil and village of one district with village counts
- Shows pincodes, towns and wards
- Links land records and village maps for each village
