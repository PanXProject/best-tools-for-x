## About
RapVideo turns one or two character photos into AI rap performance videos. Choose a scene and style, add lyrics or a prompt, or upload your own rap audio.

## Features
- Create solo or duet rap videos from photos
- Generate AI vocals and beats
- Upload your own rap audio
- Choose scene and performance style
