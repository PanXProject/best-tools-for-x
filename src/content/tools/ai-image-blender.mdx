## About

AI Image Blender combines two to five photos into a new composition using AI. Upload your images, describe how you want them blended, and choose an aspect ratio. Explore photo mashups, artistic collages, product visuals, and social media artwork in your browser.

## Features

- Combine two to five images in one composition
- Guide the blend with a text description
- Choose an aspect ratio for the output
- Create photo mashups and artistic collages
- Work online without installing desktop software
