## About
WhisperX is an independent, unofficial hosted audio and video transcription app. Upload recordings, review word timestamps and optional English speaker labels in your browser, and export TXT, SRT, VTT or JSON. No local Python, CUDA or GPU setup is required. Verified accounts can try five minutes of English transcription once without a card. Paid plans and minute packs are available. This service is not affiliated with the upstream authors, OpenAI or pyannote.

## Features
- Review word-level timestamps alongside your recording
- Edit transcripts and rename speaker labels in your browser
- Export TXT, SRT, VTT and structured JSON files
