## About

Grok Imagine AI is a browser-based AI image and video generation platform that helps creators turn text prompts and existing photos into visual content. It supports creative workflows for digital art, short videos, marketing visuals, and social media storytelling without requiring advanced editing experience.

## Features

- **Text-to-Image Generation:** Create AI-generated images from written prompts.
- **AI Image Editing:** Transform and refine existing images using AI-powered tools.
- **Text-to-Video Generation:** Turn written scene descriptions into short AI videos.
- **Image-to-Video Animation:** Add motion to still images and photos.
- **Multiple AI Model Options:** Explore different supported image and video generation models.
- **Creative Style Exploration:** Experiment with artistic styles, visual concepts, and storytelling ideas.
- **Customizable Generation Settings:** Adjust available resolution, duration, and output options depending on the model.
- **Browser-Based Creation:** Generate and download visual content directly online.
- **Credit-Based Pricing:** Start with available welcome credits and purchase additional credits when needed.
- **Content Creation Workflows:** Develop visuals for social media, marketing campaigns, concept art, and creative projects.

Explore [Grok Imagine AI](https://www.grokimagineai.net/) to create AI images and videos from text prompts and photos.
