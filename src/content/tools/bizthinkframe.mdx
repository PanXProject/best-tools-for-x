## About
BizThinkFrame helps professionals analyze business problems and communicate their reasoning clearly. It combines thinking frameworks, work-reporting skills, and practical guides with contextual exercises in English and Simplified Chinese.

## Features
- Explore frameworks for business decisions and problem solving
- Prepare structured decision requests and work updates
- Practice concise reporting with timed rehearsal
- Follow practical guides with contextual exercises
