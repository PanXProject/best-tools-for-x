## About

Free study material on Youth4work University for students and job seekers preparing for college courses, aptitude tests and placement tests. Students can also explore and compare colleges and courses and ask questions in college forums. Part of Youth4work, an Indian careers and talent assessment platform running since 2012 with free aptitude, skill and psychometric tests and assessed profiles.

## Features

- Offers free study material for students
- Sits alongside college forums and comparisons
- Links to free aptitude and skill tests
