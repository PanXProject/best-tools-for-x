## About

AI Image Extender expands photos beyond their original borders with AI. Upload an image and choose an aspect ratio to add space around the original composition. Adapt photos for social posts, banners, product listings, and presentations without cropping out important details.

## Features

- Expand image borders with AI outpainting
- Choose an aspect ratio for your composition
- Upload JPG, PNG, or WebP images
- Create wider or taller versions of existing photos
- Work directly in your web browser
