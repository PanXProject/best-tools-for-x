## About
Sensei AI is a real-time AI interview assistant that listens to interview questions and suggests tailored answers based on your resume and the job description, helping job seekers practice and perform confidently in live interviews.

## Features
- Real-time suggested answers during live interviews
- Answers tailored to your resume and job description
- Help with behavioral, technical and follow-up questions
- Practice with realistic mock questions
