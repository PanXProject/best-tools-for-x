## About

A free Data Galactica list of the best-known companies and organisations in Luxembourg, ranked by how much its sources cover them and then by size (not by revenue). The list opens with ArcelorMittal, RTL Group S.A., Fendi S.A. and Luxair; each entry has a permanent Galactica ID, its status, industry and city, a data grade and the sources behind it. Every Data Galactica record carries a permanent Galactica ID (DGC- for organisations, DGP- for people), a grade and its sources.

## Features

- Lists the best-covered organisations in one country
- Gives each a permanent Galactica ID and a grade
- Shows the sources behind every record
