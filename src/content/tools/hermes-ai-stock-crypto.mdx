## About
Hermes AI: Stock & Crypto gives every stock, commodity and coin one AI score from 0 to 100, with five simple bars that explain it. It covers 10 stock markets plus commodities and crypto, in 11 languages, on iOS and Android. Free to download, no subscription, no ads. Not investment advice.

## Features
- DNA Score: one 0-100 score for every asset, with five plain-language bars
- AI Oracle: 10 AI Engines, 100 AI Agents, 10,000 Trade Simulations show where they agree and disagree
- Charts: one asset across five time frames, explained in plain words
- Hot Dips: the sharpest pullbacks in each market, every day
- Hermes Portfolio: a public $1,000 simulation with every trade visible
- Downloadable PDF analysis reports
- 10 Markets: US, Turkey, Germany, UK, India, Japan, Canada, France, Switzerland, Hong Kong
- 11 Languages, including Arabic and Persian
