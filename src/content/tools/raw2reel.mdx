## About
Raw2Reel turns a raw talking video into a finished reel with one button. It cuts pauses, ums and repeated takes from the transcript, then adds captions, b-roll and music in about a minute. Free to try: edit for free and see the full result; downloading the finished video needs a pack or a plan.

## Features
- One button: raw clip to finished reel in about a minute
- Cuts pauses, filler words and repeated takes
- Word-by-word animated captions
- Automatic b-roll and background music
- Auto zoom
- Arabic captions written right to left, plus English and Danish
- Works in the browser on phone and computer, no app
- Vertical 9:16 for Reels, TikTok and YouTube Shorts
