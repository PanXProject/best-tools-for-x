## About
Aureon is an AI intent layer for Mac. Say or type what you need and it creates the matching events, reminders, tasks, and notes in the apps you already use.
## Features
- Turns one spoken or typed passage into calendar events, reminders, tasks, and notes
- Create-only by design, and it asks when a time or destination is unclear
- Works on macOS 14 and up, with a 7-day free trial and no permanent free tier
