## About

Melodessa helps turn personal stories into custom song gifts for birthdays, anniversaries and other special occasions. Write or edit the lyrics, confirm them before making music, and share the finished song through a gift page.

## Features

- Write or edit your song lyrics
- Confirm the lyrics before making music
- Share the finished song through a digital gift page
