## About
ZombieCouple is an AI video generator for the viral zombie couple trend. Upload one photo of you and one of your partner, pick a story and a scene, and get a short cinematic video. Both faces stay recognizable. Ready in about 3-5 minutes.

## Features
- Two photos in, a finished cinematic video out, with no prompts to write
- Three stories: Zombie Couple, Zombie Love Story, and AI Zombie Hug
- Both faces stay recognizable through the whole video
- Pay once for credit packs from $9.99, with no subscription
