## About
Sharpniq improves blurry photos in a browser. Browser Free processes privately on the device without credits; optional credit-based Cloud Unblur offers AI enhancement. Users can compare and download results. Quality depends on the source image; missing detail may not be recoverable. Submitted by the product representative.

## Features
- Process photos locally with Browser Free
- Choose optional credit-based Cloud Unblur
- Compare before-and-after image results
- Download images without installing software
