## About
EzAiRapVideo is an AI rap video generator that turns one or two photos into a short rap performance in your browser. Pick a stage, add your own lyrics or an occasion, and the AI writes the rap for you. Videos download in HD with no watermark.

## Features
- Turn one or two photos into a rap video
- Solo or duo performances on preset stages
- Bring your own lyrics or let the AI write them
- Choose video model, resolution, length and aspect ratio
- Credit quote shown before you generate
- HD output with no watermark
