## About
What Pets Cost is an independent price guide to what pet services actually cost at the big US chains. It publishes grooming, vet visit, wellness-plan, boarding and training prices with sources and last-checked dates, so you can budget before you book.

## Features
- Grooming cost calculator by dog size and add-ons
- Banfield vaccine visit prices in six markets
- Vetco vaccine prices by ZIP code
- Boarding-stay cost calculator
- Every price page shows its source and last-checked date
