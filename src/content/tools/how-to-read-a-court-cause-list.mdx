## About

A free plain-English eCourtsIndia guide to reading an Indian court cause list line by line: item number, stage, coram, fresh versus part-heard matters, where to find today's list and what to do if it is missing. Part of eCourtsIndia, which lets lawyers, litigants and researchers search Indian court cases by CNR, party, advocate or judge, with daily cause lists. Not legal advice.

## Features

- Explains each column of an Indian cause list
- Covers fresh and part-heard matters
- Says what to do if the list is missing
