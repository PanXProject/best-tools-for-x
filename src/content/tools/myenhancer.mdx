## About
MyEnhancer is an online AI photo tool for creators, small businesses and personal photo collections. Improve clarity, noise, lighting and color; upscale images or restore older photos. Eligible single-image Standard tasks use a limited daily credit allowance. Pro processing and batch jobs require paid credits. Results vary; inspect faces, text and edges.

## Features
- Enhance photo clarity, lighting and colors
- Upscale images and reduce mild blur or noise
- Restore old photos and colorize black-and-white images
- Compare before and after results
- Work with PNG, JPG and WebP images
