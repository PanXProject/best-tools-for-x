## About
Tafadal answers visitors on your website like a front desk, using what you tell it about your business, and keeps a follow-up board of the people who got in touch so no one is forgotten. It is built for coaches and other solo business owners, in English and Arabic. Access is by invitation and starts free, with no card.

## Features
- Website chat added with one line of code, on WordPress, Squarespace, Wix, Webflow, Shopify or plain HTML
- Learns from a page of your website, and you approve the text before it is saved
- A follow-up board showing who wrote to you and what each person needs next
- English and Arabic
- A sample account to try at tafadal.app/demo, with no sign-in
