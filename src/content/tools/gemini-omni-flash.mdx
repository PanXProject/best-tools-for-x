## About
Gemini Omni (https://gemini-omni.dev) is an all-in-one multimodal AI image and video creation platform built for creators, marketers, designers, and visual artists. Powered by cutting-edge diffusion and generative models, Gemini Omni bridges the gap between text prompts and production-ready visual storytelling.

## Features
- Multimodal AI Creation: Seamlessly generate high-definition images and cinematic AI videos from text or image prompts.
- Multi-Image Fusion: Blend up to 3 reference images with consistent character identity, composition, and style.
- Flux Kontext Precision Editing: Modify details, lighting, camera angles, and art styles without altering image integrity.
- 4K Cinema-Grade Video Output: Generate smooth, high-framerate dynamic videos tailored for YouTube, TikTok, and advertising.
