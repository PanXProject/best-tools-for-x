## About
PhotoCraft Online is an online photo editor you can use in the browser. It lets you edit and craft photos without installing software.

## Features
- Browser-based photo editing with no account required
- Crop images and add text directly in the browser
- Inspect and edit layered PSD files
- Download your work as PNG or JPEG
