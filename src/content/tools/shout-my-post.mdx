## About
Shout My Post is a curated directory where makers list their websites, apps and open source projects for free, and visitors discover genuinely useful tools without wading through ads or sponsored noise.

## Features
- Free permanent listing with a link back to your site
- Human review keeps the directory clean
- Browse by category to find tools fast
- Simple submission, no badges or link-backs required
