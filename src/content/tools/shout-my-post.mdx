## About
Shout My Post is a free online directory where makers, founders and developers list their websites, apps and open-source projects. A human moderator reviews every submission before it goes live, keeping listings high-quality. Submitting is completely free - no paid tiers, no link-back requirements.

## Features
- Free permanent listing for websites, apps and open-source projects
- Human moderation of every submission
- Category-based discovery
- Public page with a link to your website
- No account tiers or hidden fees
