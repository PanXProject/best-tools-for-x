## About
BasePic is free image, video and file hosting. Upload a photo, a video or any file up to 2 GB without an account and get a short link and a direct link. Uploads are private: they are not listed on the site and their pages are hidden from search engines. It also has free browser tools for images and video, with no watermark.

## Features
- Upload any file up to 2 GB with no account
- Short link and direct image link in seconds
- Compress an image to an exact size such as 20 KB or 100 KB
- Resize and crop images in the browser
- Convert HEIC to JPG and photos to PDF
- Crop a passport photo and get a 4 x 6 sheet to print
- Compress video to 10 MB, 16 MB, 20 MB or 25 MB
- Free image hosting API with no key, plus an MCP server for AI agents
