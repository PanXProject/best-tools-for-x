## About
Makify AI is a multi-model creative platform for designers, marketers, creators, and e-commerce teams creating product visuals, campaign assets, and social content.

## Features
- Generate images with GPT Image 2, FLUX 3 Image, and Seedream 5.0 Pro
- Edit visuals with expansion, text removal, restoration, and background tools
- Create video concepts with Seedance 2.5, MiniMax H3, and Wan 3.0
