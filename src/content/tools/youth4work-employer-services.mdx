## About

Employer services from Youth4work, India's talent assessment and careers platform: employers can post jobs for free, search pre-assessed candidate profiles and contact candidates directly, or use yAssess, Youth4work's online assessment software, for bulk and campus hiring. Youth4work was founded in 2012 and is based in Jaipur.

## Features

- Post jobs free
- Search pre-assessed profiles
- YAssess for bulk hiring
