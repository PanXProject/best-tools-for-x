## About

Feldy is an online Feldenkrais program. Short, audio guided somatic lessons help you move with less pain, better balance and more ease, with no equipment and no fitness level required. Lessons are done lying down or seated, at home, at your own pace.

## Features

- Daily morning and bedtime movement rituals
- Guided lesson library for the back, hips, neck and shoulders
- Audio guided, no video or equipment needed
- Works in the browser and as a mobile web app
- Created by a Guild Certified Feldenkrais Practitioner
- Free trial, then a monthly or yearly subscription
