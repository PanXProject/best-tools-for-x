## About

AIGifGenerator turns a short scene description into an animated loop. Type something like "a pig dancing" and it returns a 512x512 GIF in 30 to 40 seconds.

## Features

- Four style presets: cartoon, anime, realistic and 3D
- Clip length 1, 2 or 3 seconds
- Aspect ratio 1:1, 16:9 and 9:16
- Optional transparent background and looping toggle
- A reference image can steer the motion
- One free GIF without a card; failed runs are not charged

## Pricing

Credit based: 10 credits per GIF. Packs start at .9 for 500 credits and never expire.
