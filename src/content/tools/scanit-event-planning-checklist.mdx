## About
Free event planning checklist from ScanIt Events. Twelve sections covering venue and date, budget and admin, guests, invitations, food and drink, photography, music, flowers and décor, attire, ceremony, transport and the day itself. Tick things off, add your own items, hide what is done, copy what is left or download it as a PDF. It saves automatically in your browser — no signup, nothing uploaded.

## Features
- Twelve sections from venue and budget to the day itself
- Tick things off and add your own items
- Hide what is done, copy what is left
- Download as a PDF with your event name
- Saves automatically in your browser — no signup
