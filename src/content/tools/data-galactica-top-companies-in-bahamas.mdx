## About

A free Data Galactica list of the best-known companies and organisations in the Bahamas, ranked by how much its sources cover them and then by size (not by revenue). The list opens with Bahamasair, Silversea Cruise Holding Ltd., SkyBahamas and Western Air; each entry has a permanent Galactica ID, its status, industry and city, a data grade and the sources behind it.
