## About

LuckPanda is business software for small businesses: quotes, invoices, CRM, purchasing, inventory, accounts and GST compliance on one record, with an AI agent that drafts while you decide. Drafts, branded PDFs and the free invoice, quote and receipt generators need no account.

## Features

- Quotes, invoices, proforma invoices, receipts, credit and debit notes, sales orders, delivery challans, recurring invoices
- Any currency; numbers and dates formatted by the document's country
- Payment links and payment reminders
- CRM: clients, contacts, leads, pipeline, forms, meetings
- Purchasing: vendors, RFQs, purchase orders, bills, expenses, vendor payouts
- Inventory: items, warehouses, stock movements

## Pricing

Free to draft. $4.99 per module per month (Rs 199 in India) to issue, send and take payment. Per module, not per user.
