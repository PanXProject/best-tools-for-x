## About

Youth4work University groups Indian colleges by course, so a student can pick a course first and then compare the colleges that offer it, alongside college forums and free study material. Part of Youth4work, an Indian careers and talent assessment platform running since 2012 with free aptitude, skill and psychometric tests and assessed profiles.

## Features

- Groups Indian colleges by course
- Lets students compare colleges for one course
- Links to free study material
