## About
Pack Leader is local SEO software for US service businesses and the agencies that serve them. It runs the live Google Maps searches your customers run, finds the top 3 businesses, and puts your Google Business Profile and website side by side with theirs: categories, reviews and review pace, photos, hours, directory listings, backlinks and website pages. Every gap becomes a fix you can act on, starting with an action plan of the most important ones.

## Features
- Side-by-side comparison of your Google Business Profile and website with the Google Maps top 3
- Local grid scans that show where you rank across your service area
- AI visibility check for ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI Mode
- Competitor review pace tracking
- AI-drafted review replies, Google posts and missing service pages
- Directory listing and backlink comparisons against the top 3
- Free Gap Audit and a free plan to start
