## About
Math AI is a browser-based solver that helps students and self-learners understand mathematics. Enter an equation or word problem, or upload a photo, to receive guided explanations across algebra, geometry, calculus, and statistics. Solution and tutor modes support answer checking as well as learning the reasoning.

## Features
- Step-by-step explanations for typed math problems
- Photo upload support for worksheet questions
- Algebra, geometry, calculus, and statistics coverage
- Tutor mode for guided mathematical reasoning
- Browser access with free starter credits
