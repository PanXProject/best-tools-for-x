## About

Uttar Pradesh: 75 districts and 96,061 villages. How to check land records, maps, registration and encumbrance in Uttar Pradesh, and the government portals. UP Bhulekh shows a free khatauni copy behind a captcha and a paid certified copy, plus plot sale and dispute status by gata; maps are on UP Bhunaksha. DILRMP-MIS flags all villages computerised but its RoR count (110,326) is about the village count, so it is not a holding inventory.

## Features

- Lists every district of the state with its village count
- Explains how to check land records, maps, registration and encumbrance
- Links the government portals that hold the records
