## About
Upscayl is a free online AI image upscaler that enhances blurry, pixelated, or low-resolution photos into sharper, high-definition results. Choose enhancement modes, preview, and download in JPG, PNG, or WEBP — right in the browser.

## Features
- AI upscaling from 1x up to 8x for 4K-ready output
- Modes for standard photos, portraits, anime and art
- Face restore and scratch restore options
- Batch upload support for multiple images
- Free to try online with no install required
