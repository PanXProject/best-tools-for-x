## About
Promotional information prepared on behalf of WAFST. WAFST is a business WhatsApp platform for preparing campaigns, organising customers who agreed to receive messages, and scheduling when messages are sent. This description is based on the current official website, not an independent product test.

## Features
- Prepare WhatsApp campaigns for customers who opted in to communication.
- Schedule messages for a selected day and time.
- Organise consenting customers into groups for campaigns.
