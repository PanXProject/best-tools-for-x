## About

Nagaland: 16 districts and 1,422 villages. How to check land records, maps, registration and encumbrance in Nagaland, and the government portals that hold. DoLR lists no RoR portal for Nagaland. The state land records department says survey and RoR preparation is done for 586 villages and digitisation and map integration are in progress (Kohima and Wokha rural survey upcoming); DILRMP-MIS shows 62.54% in its CLR table but 0% on its homepage ranking.

## Features

- Lists every district of the state with its village count
- Explains how to check land records, maps, registration and encumbrance
- Links the government portals that hold the records
