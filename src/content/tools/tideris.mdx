## About
Tideris is a free AI tool that explains Chinese astrology in plain English. Enter a birth date and it builds a BaZi (Four Pillars) chart, then writes a short reading you can follow without first learning classical vocabulary. Daily fortune notes describe the tone of the day. A compatibility reading compares two charts. Liu Yao and Qi Men readings sit next to the chart. Core charting stays free. Not medical, legal, or financial advice.

## Features
- Builds a BaZi Four Pillars chart from a birth date
- Writes a short plain-English reading of the chart
- Daily fortune notes describe the tone of the day
- Compares two charts in a compatibility reading
- Offers Liu Yao and Qi Men readings next to the chart
