## About
Artcraft AI is a browser-based studio for making and editing images and videos with AI, and a no-download [artcraft adobe alternative](https://artcraftstudio.org). Describe what you want or upload a photo, pick a model such as Nano Banana Pro, GPT Image 2 or Seedance 2.5, and download the result. PhotoCraft edits photos by sentence, FilmCraft makes and edits videos. New accounts get 100 free credits.

## Features
- PhotoCraft Online edits photos by describing the change, without layers or masks
- FilmCraft Online makes videos from a prompt or a photo, with sound
- AI Image Generator for product shots, posters and thumbnails in up to 4K
- Image to Video keeps faces, outfits and products the same from the first frame
- Video Watermark Remover paints out a logo or caption from a clip you own
- Works in the browser on a laptop, Chromebook, tablet or phone
