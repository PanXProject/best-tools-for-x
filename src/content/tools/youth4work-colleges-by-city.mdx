## About

Youth4work University lists colleges city by city across India, so students can find colleges in their city, compare courses and read and ask questions in each college's forum before choosing where to study. Part of Youth4work, an Indian careers and talent assessment platform running since 2012 with free aptitude, skill and psychometric tests and assessed profiles.

## Features

- Lists colleges city by city across India
- Lets students compare courses before applying
- Includes a forum for each college
