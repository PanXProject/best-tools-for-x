## About

Ladakh: 2 districts and 242 villages. How to check land records, maps, registration and encumbrance in Ladakh, and the government portals that hold them. The Ladakh land records portal describes a GIS system for jamabandi, girdawari and mutation but offers no citizen search. DILRMP-MIS shows 52.21% of villages computerised (130 of 249). Deeds run on NGDRS since 2021.

## Features

- Lists every district of the state with its village count
- Explains how to check land records, maps, registration and encumbrance
- Links the government portals that hold the records
