## About
Cuta brings popular AI models together in one creative workspace for writing, images, videos, characters, filmmaking and audio. Create product visuals, ads, social videos and short films with affordable model access, free signup credits and no subscription needed to get started.

## Features
- Text models including GPT-6 Astra, Sol and Luna, Claude Opus 5.5, Gemini 3.8 Flash and Meta Muse Spark 1.3, and more, for creative ideas, copy and scripts.
- Image models including GPT Image 2.5, Seedream 5.0 and FLUX.2, and more, for product visuals, posters, portraits and illustrations.
- Video models including Veo 3.1, Seedance 2.5, Kling 3.0, Wan 3.0 and Grok Imagine, and more, for text-to-video and image-to-video creation.
- Conversational image editing, inpainting, expansion, background changes, outfit swaps, angle adjustments, restyling and upscaling.
- Character, motion and style references, motion transfer, lip sync, character replacement, clip extension, relighting and video effects.
- An AI director and film studio for script-based storyboards, camera angles, depth of field, lighting, color and performance controls.
- Reusable characters and story worlds for recurring characters across multi-scene creative projects.
- A creative canvas with AI-assisted brainstorming, visual workflows, reusable templates and assets, and version comparison.
- MiniMax music and speech models, voiceovers, voice cloning and sound effects, alongside free sound assets to preview and download.
- Free signup credits, selected free tools and affordable model access; no subscription needed to get started with credits.
- Subscription credits issued monthly, with each grant valid for two months from its corresponding cycle start. Unused credits remain available next month, giving more time to refine your work without a month-end rush.
- Eligible referral rewards for both people after the invited friend joins and makes their first paid purchase.
