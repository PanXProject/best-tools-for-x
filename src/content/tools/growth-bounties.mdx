## About

Growth Bounties is a marketplace for outcome-based growth. Set a Domain Rating target, fund the reward, and pay only when the result is independently verified.

## Features

- **Ahrefs baseline**: A fresh Ahrefs baseline is frozen after acceptance, then the 30-day work period begins.
- **Independent checks**: Three qualifying scheduled checks validate the target.
- **Full guarantee**: If the locked outcome is missed, the full Growth Bounty charge is refunded.
