## About
Karaoke Maker is a browser workspace for turning an authorized song into a sing-along video. I am associated with this project; this listing was prepared with AI assistance. Explore the [karaoke maker](https://aikaraokemaker.com/) to review lyric timing, backing audio and export choices.

## Features
- Import eligible audio or video files up to the stated limits.
- Use vocal separation and review the resulting backing track.
- Generate or import synced lyrics and correct word timing.
- Adjust lyric colors, alignment, size and visual background.
- Preview the mix and timing before exporting a video.
- Export WAV audio or LRC and SRT lyric files.
- Save a project to a verified account and reopen it.
- Use editing and export without a processing charge.

## Practical notes
The homepage states that lyric timing costs one credit per started minute and vocal removal four; editing and export are free. Check current pricing before use.
