## About
[karaoke ai](https://aikaraokemaker.com/) is a browser-based tool for creating sing-along videos from a song. It combines vocal separation, lyric timing and visual customization in a single workflow. I am associated with the project; this description was prepared with AI assistance.

## Features
- Separate vocals from a recording to prepare a backing track
- Review and adjust the timing of on-screen lyrics
- Customize the visual background for a sing-along video
- Export video, audio or subtitles for your workflow
- Work in a browser without installing a desktop editor

## Practical notes
Results depend on the source recording, and lyrics and timing should be reviewed before sharing. Check rights for recordings, lyrics and imagery. The site offers free features and optional paid AI processing; consult its current pricing for limits.
