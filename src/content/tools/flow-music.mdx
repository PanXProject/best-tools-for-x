## About
Flow Music is an AI music generator that turns a sentence or lyrics into a complete song with vocals and instruments. Create royalty-friendly tracks in the browser for YouTube, podcasts, ads, and social content.

## Features
- Text to song generation from prompts
- Lyrics to music with AI vocals
- Multiple genres and vocal styles
- Track extension and AI cover tools
- Freemium credits to start creating
- Browser-based workflow with no install
