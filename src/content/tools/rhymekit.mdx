## About

RhymeKit turns a topic, a rap style such as Trap, Drill or Boom Bap, and a rhyme scheme into editable verses, hooks and bars. Every line stays editable, so you can regenerate, continue, copy or download the lyrics as TXT. The free tier gives you 3 generations a day without an account and 20 a day with a free account.

## Features

- Topic, style and rhyme-scheme driven lyric generation
- Trap, Drill, Boom Bap, Old School, Melodic and Freestyle styles
- Line-by-line editing, regenerate and continue
- Copy or download the finished lyrics as TXT
- Separate AI rap-video workflow with 9:16, 16:9 and 1:1 output

## Who it is for

Independent rap artists and songwriters who want a first draft fast and would rather edit lines than start from a blank page.
