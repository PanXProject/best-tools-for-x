## About

LabelPeel turns a spreadsheet into printable labels. Paste a list from Excel, Google Sheets, Numbers or a CSV and every row becomes a label on Avery-compatible sheets such as 5160 and L7160. Change the look by typing, then print or download a PDF. No Word mail merge, nothing to install.

## Features

- Paste from Excel, Google Sheets, Numbers or CSV files
- Avery-compatible 5160, L7160 and 80+ other sheets
- Change the look by typing what you want
- Print or download a PDF
- Free while in beta, no card needed
