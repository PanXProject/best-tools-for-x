## About

Guide to land records in Puducherry on India Property Register: Patta, Chitta, Settlement, how plots are identified, the local terms and where the records are held. Puducherry's Nilamagal searches patta by region, taluk, village and land type using patta or re-survey number; it returned 504 on 11 Oct 2026 and the DoLR link has no scheme. CSC Puduvai Sevai extracts cost Rs 40 each. DILRMP-MIS shows 100% computerisation (129 villages).

## Features

- Explains what the state calls its land records and what each shows
- Shows how plots are identified, from the state down to the village
- Lists the government portals and sources that hold the records
