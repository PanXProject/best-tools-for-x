## About
RoadsterVideo is a Tesla Roadster AI video generator. Upload one photo of your car, a toy car or any vehicle and get a 15-second AI video of it running the Tesla Roadster ramp flip. Two more templates put a person or pet in the driver's seat. No app, no prompt; failed videos are refunded.

## Features
- Ramp flip video from one car photo in 15 seconds
- Traffic escape and police stop templates for people or pets
- Credit price shown before you start
- Failed videos are refunded automatically
