## About
Flying Car AI turns one photo into a 15 second video of you lifting off over traffic in a flying car. Pick a scene, add your photo, and post it. Credit packs start at $4.99, one-time, with no subscription.

## Features
- Four ready-made flying car scenes from one driver photo
- Optional photo of your own car, otherwise a white electric roadster
- 15 Second MP4 with engine, thruster and street sound
- Vertical 9:16 or widescreen 16:9
- One-time credit packs and no subscription
