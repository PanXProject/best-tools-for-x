## About

JurisGrid (jurisgrid.com) is a free directory of Indian legal technology startups shown on one grid, so lawyers, founders, investors and researchers can see who builds what in Indian legal tech.

## Features

- Startups grouped by what they build: legal research, court data, contract tools, practice management and more
- Free to browse, no sign-up
- Led by the eCourtsIndia team
