## About

Guide to land records in Assam on India Property Register: Jamabandi, Dag record, how plots are identified, the local terms and where the records are held. Dharitree shows uncertified jamabandi after choosing a district and village; certified copies are an RTPS service and missing records go to the circle office. DILRMP-MIS shows 90.13% of villages computerised (21,186 of 23,507), and Assam Bhunaksha publishes dag maps with ULPIN.

## Features

- Explains what the state calls its land records and what each shows
- Shows how plots are identified, from the state down to the village
- Lists the government portals and sources that hold the records
