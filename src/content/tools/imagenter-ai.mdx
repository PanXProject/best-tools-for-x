## About
Imagenter AI is an all-in-one platform for AI video and image generation. Use Seedance, Kling, Veo, Wan, Seedream, FLUX and Nano Banana under one account and one credit balance, with pay-per-use credits and no subscription. Free credits to start, then one-time packs from $10 that never expire.

## Features
- Text-to-video and image-to-video (Seedance, Kling, Veo, Wan, MiniMax, Grok Imagine)
- Text-to-image and image editing (Seedream, FLUX, GPT Image, Nano Banana)
- One account and one credit balance for all models
- Photo tools: photo to anime, cartoon, sketch, action figure, character sheet, pose changer
- Pay-per-use credits with no subscription; credits never expire
