## About

TrueTestify is a video-first testimonial platform that helps businesses collect, manage, and showcase authentic customer reviews. With easy testimonial collection, multiple feedback formats, and embeddable website widgets, TrueTestify helps businesses build trust, strengthen credibility, and turn customer experiences into powerful social proof.

## Features

- Video Testimonial Collection: Collect authentic customer video testimonials using shareable links.
- Multiple Testimonial Formats: Support video, audio, photo, and written testimonials.
- Testimonial Management Dashboard: Manage customer testimonials in one centralized location.
- Website Testimonial Widgets: Display customer testimonials on websites and landing pages.
- Public Review Import: Manually import existing reviews from Google, Yelp, Facebook, and Trustpilot with source attribution.
- No Customer Signup Required: Allow customers to submit testimonials without creating accounts.
- Photo Testimonials: Showcase customer experiences through images and before-and-after photos.
- Social Proof Marketing: Build customer confidence by displaying authentic customer experiences.

TrueTestify helps agencies, service providers, healthcare practices, real estate professionals, consultants, and local businesses turn real customer stories into powerful social proof.
