## About

Lawyer pages built from Indian court case records: search a name, drill down by state, district and court, and open a lawyer's page to see the cases they appear in, filterable by court, case type, status and date. It complements the separate verified advocate directory. Free on the web; part of eCourtsIndia.

## Features

- Lawyer pages built from case records
- Drill down by state, district and court
- Filter a lawyer's cases by court, type, status and date
