## About
Military Medic to Civilian Healthcare Job Translator is a free AI tool for service members and veterans with military medical experience (Army 68W, Navy corpsman, Air Force 4N0X1 and more). Enter your branch, job code and duties; it names civilian healthcare jobs your experience points to, resume lines in civilian words and recruiter keywords. It never says a military credential replaces a civilian license. No sign-up.

## Features
- Civilian job matches with what each usually asks for
- Resume lines in civilian words
- Keywords recruiters search for
- Free, no sign-up, nothing stored
