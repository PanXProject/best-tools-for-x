## About

Guide to land records in Telangana on India Property Register: Land status, Pattadar Passbook (PPB), Encumbrance statement, how plots are identified, the local terms and where the records are held. Telangana has two land portals: Bhu Bharati (records updated 10 Oct 2026, but its status page was an Aadhaar widget) and the older Dharani search by survey or passbook number. DILRMP-MIS shows 99.36% of records computerised but only 0.39% of mapsheets digitised and 0% map linkage.

## Features

- Explains what the state calls its land records and what each shows
- Shows how plots are identified, from the state down to the village
- Lists the government portals and sources that hold the records
