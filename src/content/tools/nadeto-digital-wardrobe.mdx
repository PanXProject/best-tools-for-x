## About
Nadeto helps you organize your wardrobe. Add an item by photo or by a link from Wildberries, Lamoda or Ozon — the app recognizes it and saves it to your closet. Build outfits and capsule wardrobes from your own clothes. Free trial: 3 days and 15 items, then a subscription you can cancel in the app. The interface is in Russian.

## Features
- Add clothes by photo or by a Wildberries, Lamoda or Ozon link
- Build outfits from your own clothes
- Build capsule wardrobes
- Mini apps in Telegram, MAX and VK, a web version, and Android on Google Play and RuStore
- Items and outfits stay available to view if you stop paying
