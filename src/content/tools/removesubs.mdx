## About
RemoveSubs removes burned-in subtitles, captions and other visible text from videos using AI reconstruction. It supports MP4, MOV and WebM without cropping the frame or requiring manual frame-by-frame edits. Creators can preview results before downloading and adding updated captions in their editor. Paid credit packs and subscriptions cover further processing.

## Features
- Remove burned-in subtitles and visible text from videos
- Support MP4, MOV and WebM without cropping the frame
- Preview AI reconstructed results before downloading
