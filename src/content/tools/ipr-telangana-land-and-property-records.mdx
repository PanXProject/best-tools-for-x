## About

Telangana: 33 districts and 10,674 villages. How to check land records, maps, registration and encumbrance in Telangana, and the government portals that hold. Telangana has two land portals: Bhu Bharati (records updated 10 Oct 2026, but its status page was an Aadhaar widget) and the older Dharani search by survey or passbook number. DILRMP-MIS shows 99.36% of records computerised but only 0.39% of mapsheets digitised and 0% map linkage.

## Features

- Lists every district of the state with its village count
- Explains how to check land records, maps, registration and encumbrance
- Links the government portals that hold the records
