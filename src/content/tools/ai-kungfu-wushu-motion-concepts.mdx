## About
AI Kungfu helps entertainment creators and Wushu enthusiasts explore fictional martial arts scenes from one or two photos. Choose an action prompt or write custom instructions, then review and refine the generated clip. Use only consented source photos. This is a creative video tool, not fight training or proof of a real event. Videos cost one credit each, with one-time credit packs.

## Features
- One- or two-photo inputs for character action concepts
- Ready-made action prompts and custom instructions
- Up to two movement details for refining the scene
- Browser-based creation with pay-per-video credits
