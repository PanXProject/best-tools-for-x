## About
Yazhar helps businesses and online stores in the Gulf get found on Google and recommended by ChatGPT and Gemini. It writes expert SEO articles and sales pages in Arabic, English or both, publishes them on your own site, and every day asks ChatGPT and Gemini the questions your buyers ask. When they do not name you, Yazhar writes the page that answers that exact question.

## Features
- Arabic and English SEO articles and sales pages
- Publishes directly to WordPress, Shopify, Wix, Webflow and Zid
- Daily ChatGPT and Gemini visibility check
- New page written for every buyer question where you are missing
- Weekly technical SEO check
- Live dashboard and a plain weekly report
- Built for Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Kuwait, Qatar, Bahrain and Oman
- 7-Day free trial on the Zid and Wix app markets
