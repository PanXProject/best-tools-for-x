## About
BuildRoost connects construction and field service workflows for roofing, HVAC, plumbing, electrical, restoration and other contractors. Teams can manage customer leads, estimates, crew schedules, project records, job costs and invoices in a shared workspace.

## Features
- Customer relationship and lead management
- Estimates and change orders
- Crew scheduling and dispatch
- Field documentation and project management
- Job costing and invoicing
