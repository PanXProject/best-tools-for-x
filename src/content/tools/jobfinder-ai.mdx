## About

JobFinder AI turns your experience and preferences into a focused job search. Browse current openings by role, company and location, then let your agent match opportunities to your profile, prepare application materials, complete employer forms, and reach hiring managers from your own inbox.

## Features

- Set target roles and locations; your agent finds matching openings
- Prepares and completes applications
- Reaches hiring managers from your own inbox
- Tracks applications, replies and follow-ups in one place

Browsing current openings by role, company and location is free.
