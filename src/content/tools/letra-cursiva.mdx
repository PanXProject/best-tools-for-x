## About
Letra Cursiva turns ordinary text into about 30 cursive and fancy Unicode styles that you can copy and paste into Instagram, WhatsApp, TikTok, bios or names without installing a font or creating an account.

## Features
- Convert plain text into about 30 cursive Unicode styles
- Copy any style with one click and paste it anywhere
- Works in Spanish on desktop and mobile browsers
- No registration, no font install and no download
- Styles ready for Instagram, WhatsApp and TikTok bios
