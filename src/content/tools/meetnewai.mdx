## About
MeetNewAI is a free bilingual directory of new AI tools, browser-playable AI games and MCP servers, organized by the job you need done. Every listing has a short editorial note on what the product does and who it suits, and the guides cover topics like what MCP is and how AI coding assistants compare. No signup is needed to browse, and the site works in English and Chinese.

## Features
- AI tools grouped by task: coding, image and video, voice, writing, research and more
- AI games you can play directly in the browser
- MCP servers that connect AI assistants to apps and data
- A short editorial note on every listing
- Practical guides and comparisons, plus an AI subscription cost calculator
- Free submissions for founders, reviewed by hand
