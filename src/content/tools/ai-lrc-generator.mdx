## About
AI LRC Generator is a browser [lrc generator](https://ailrcgenerator.online/) for editable synced lyrics. Review words and line timestamps, adjust an offset, preview and export LRC or subtitles. Editing and exports are free; automatic audio processing needs a verified account and credits.

## Features
- Start from recognized audio or lyrics you supply.
- Correct words while reviewing the chosen recording.
- Edit individual line timestamps in the browser.
- Apply an offset for a consistent timing shift.
- Preview cues against the selected audio version.
- Export standard LRC for line-synchronized playback.
- Export Enhanced LRC when real word timestamps exist.
- Choose TXT or subtitle exports such as SRT and VTT.
