## About
Play Jean Phil is an AI video generator. Upload one photo of a person or pet and it creates a vertical 9:16 MP4 in one of eight scenes featuring the Jean Philanthrope character, with the original scene sound.

## Features
- Turns one photo of a person or pet into a Jean Phil video
- Eight scenes rebuilt from the original clips
- Choose wig, moustache, suit colour and video length
- Vertical 9:16 MP4 download with the scene sound
- No app and no prompt needed
