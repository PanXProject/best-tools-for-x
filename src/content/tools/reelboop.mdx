## About
ReelBoop turns your photos into short AI videos using templates for dancing, outfit changes, duets and product videos. Choose a template, add your photos, then play or download the finished MP4 from your private history.

## Features
- Templates for dance, outfit-change, duet and product videos
- Turn your own photos into short AI videos
- Play or download finished MP4 files
- Private history of all your videos
