## About

A free directory of advocates in India verified against their Bar Council enrolment, linked to the public case records they appear in across the Supreme Court, High Courts, district courts and tribunals. From public court records; not legal advice. Part of eCourtsIndia.

## Features

- Advocates verified against Bar Council enrolment
- Linked to public case records
- Supreme Court, High Courts, district courts and tribunals
