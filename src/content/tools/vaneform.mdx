## About
Vaneform is a free website traffic checker. Enter any domain to see its estimated monthly visits, the trend over recent months, top countries and where visitors come from. Basic lookups need no account, and the site works in English and Chinese.

## Features
- Estimated monthly visits and month-over-month change for any domain
- Traffic source mix across search, direct, referral and social
- Top countries for each website
- Leaderboards of the fastest-growing websites and most visited AI tools
- Side-by-side traffic comparison of several domains
- Public API and hosted MCP server so AI assistants can look up traffic
