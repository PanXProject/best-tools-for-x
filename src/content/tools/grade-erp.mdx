## About
Grade ERP is an all-in-one business platform designed for IT companies, digital agencies, and tech teams. It brings together project management, HR, recruiting, and financial tools in a single system, helping businesses streamline operations and improve efficiency. Real-time dashboards allow users to track budgets, revenue, costs, cash flow, and profitability.

## Features
- Project management for IT teams and agencies
- HR management and recruiting tools
- Real-time finance dashboards for budgets, revenue, and cash flow
- Workflow automation and task coordination
- Integrations with Slack, Google, HubSpot, QuickBooks, and Jira
