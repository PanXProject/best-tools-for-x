## About
Motion Control is a web app for making a motion control video from photos and a reference clip. The reference video guides action, timing and framing while your photos provide visual details. Preview prepared motion templates or upload a compatible clip of your own.

## Features
- Motion control video from photos and a reference clip
- Prepared motion templates with role slots
- Upload your own compatible clip
- Credit estimate shown before generating
- My creations history for completed videos
