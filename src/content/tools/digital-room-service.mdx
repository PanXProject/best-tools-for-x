## About
Hotel guest services and operations platform: Room QR ordering, service requests, staff tasks, one Command Center and AI Autopilot. Guests and staff need no app; they just scan and work.

## Features
- Room QR ordering and guest service requests
- Staff tasks and QR attendance in the browser
- One Command Center for managers
- AI Autopilot offers tasks to checked-in staff, with manager override
- 60 Days of free Grand access, plans from $59/quarter
