## About
Digital Room Service is a digital guest services and intelligent hotel operations platform. Guests scan a room QR code to order food with photos, request housekeeping, laundry, amenities or maintenance, and track each request in real time, with no app download. Behind the scenes, Smart Operations Autopilot assigns tasks to on-shift staff by workload, recovers unfinished team tasks and escalates delays automatically, while AI agent capabilities support proactive service coordination.
## Features
- Room QR ordering with photos and real-time request tracking
- Smart Operations Autopilot task assignment and escalation
- Kitchen Display System with delivery tracking
- Orders post to the room bill
- QR/PIN attendance, rosters and hotel analytics
- Free Hotel Staff Savings Calculator and ROI calculator
