## About

Guide to land records in Ladakh on India Property Register: Jamabandi, Girdawari, Mutation, how plots are identified, the local terms and where the records are held. The Ladakh land records portal describes a GIS system for jamabandi, girdawari and mutation but offers no citizen search. DILRMP-MIS shows 52.21% of villages computerised (130 of 249). Deeds run on NGDRS since 2021. Register coverage: 242 villages.

## Features

- Explains what the state calls its land records and what each shows
- Shows how plots are identified, from the state down to the village
- Lists the government portals and sources that hold the records
