## About
FreightTotals provides free browser-based freight and shipping calculators plus source-backed research on U.S. freight companies. It helps carriers, shippers, and operators calculate freight class, density, dimensional weight, pallet and container loads, fuel surcharges, CBM, and trucking cost per mile.

## Features
- Sixteen free freight and shipping calculators
- Searchable U.S. freight company directory
- Transparent formulas with dated public sources
- Browser-based tools with no account required
