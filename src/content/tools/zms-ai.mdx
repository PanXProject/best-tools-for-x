## About

ZMS AI is an online creative platform for generating AI videos and images from text prompts and visual references. It brings multiple AI models and creative workflows into one workspace, helping creators, marketers, designers, and businesses produce visual content for social media, marketing, storytelling, and other creative projects.

## Features

- Text-to-video generation from written prompts
- Image-to-video generation using visual references
- Text-to-image generation for AI-powered visuals
- Multiple AI models in one creative workspace
- Customizable generation settings for different creative tasks
- Credit-based pricing with one-time purchase options
