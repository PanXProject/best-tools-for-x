## About
jsonl.co is a free, local-first JSONL viewer and editor for developers working with large .jsonl, .ndjson, .json, and .txt files. It streams 1GB+ files in chunks and renders visible rows in the browser. Inspect, validate, edit, search, and export data without uploading files or creating an account. Includes converters, a formatter, validator, splitter, merger, and JSON flattener.

## Features
- Opens 1GB+ JSONL and NDJSON files directly in the browser.
- Streams files in chunks and renders only visible rows.
- Validates and edits records with raw and parsed views.
- Searches, converts, and exports data.
- Keeps files on-device without uploads or an account.
