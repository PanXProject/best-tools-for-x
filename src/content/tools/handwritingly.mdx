## About
Handwritingly is a free handwriting generator that turns typed text into a realistic handwritten page you can download as PNG or a multi-page PDF. It is used for cards, letters, worksheets, study notes and design mockups.

## Features
- 47 Open-source handwriting fonts, print and cursive
- Ruled, legal pad, grid and blank paper with blue, black, red or pencil ink
- Natural variation in tilt, height and ink for every letter
- PNG, transparent PNG and multi-page PDF downloads
- Write in your own handwriting by drawing a letter set
- Runs in the browser with no account and no watermark
