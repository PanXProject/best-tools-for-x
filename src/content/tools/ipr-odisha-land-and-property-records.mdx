## About

Odisha: 30 districts and 49,447 villages. How to check land records, maps, registration and encumbrance in Odisha, and the government portals that hold them. Bhulekh Odisha is a session-gated application: the DoLR RoRView link showed a session-timeout page on 11 Oct 2026, so the search fields were not verified.
