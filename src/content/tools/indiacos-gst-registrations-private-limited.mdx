## About

List of 2,97,258 private limited company registrations in India under GST, with GSTIN, status and location. The page covers GST registrations whose constitution of business is private limited company, so you can see how many such taxpayers exist and look each one up by GSTIN. Free to browse at https://indiacos.com/constitution/private-limited-company.

## Features

- Lists GST registrations of one constitution of business
- Shows GSTIN, status and location
- Built from public GST records
