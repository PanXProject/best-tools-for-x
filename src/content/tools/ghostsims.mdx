## About
GhostSims is a UK privacy brand that ships a secure encrypted phone: a Google Pixel running GrapheneOS, set up with encrypted messaging apps and paired with an encrypted, no-ID SIM card. Built for executives, journalists, lawyers and anyone who wants a hardened phone without Google tracking.

## Features
- GrapheneOS pre-installed on a supported Pixel with verified boot
- Encrypted SIM card with global data and no ID required
- Secure apps such as Threema and Armadillo pre-configured
- Pay by card or crypto
- Setup support from a UK team
