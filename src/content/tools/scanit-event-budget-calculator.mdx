## About
Free event budget calculator from ScanIt Events. Enter your total budget (and optionally a guest count) and get it split across venue and catering, photography, music, flowers and décor, attire, stationery, transport, rings, cake, favours and a buffer for surprises, using standard shares. Adjust any category and see what is left update. No signup; nothing you type is uploaded — the budget stays on your device.

## Features
- Splits a total across venue, catering, photography, music, décor and more
- Optional guest count
- Adjust any category and watch what is left update
- Standard allocation table with real-dollar examples
- No signup — the budget stays on your device
