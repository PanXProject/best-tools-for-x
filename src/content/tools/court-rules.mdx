## About
Court Rules is a free reference for U.S. federal court practice: the Federal Rules of Civil Procedure, district local rules, individual judge standing orders and court holidays, organized by court. The deadline calculator counts calendar, business and court days and applies each district's holidays. A public API and an MCP server at courtrules.app/mcp serve the same data to legal-tech tools.

## Features
- Federal Rules of Civil Procedure by court
- District local rules and judge standing orders
- Filing deadline calculator for calendar, business and court days
- Court holidays applied per district
- Public API and MCP server at courtrules.app/mcp
