## About
Speak and get text you can edit, copy or download as .txt. Speech is recognized in your browser with OpenAI's Whisper model, so your voice is never uploaded. Free, no sign-up, no limit on recordings.

## Features
- Speech recognized in your browser with OpenAI's Whisper model
- Your voice is never uploaded
- English mode, or Any language mode with 99 languages and automatic detection
- Up to 2 minutes per recording, as many recordings as you like
- Edit, copy or download the text as .txt
