## About
Healthcare Job Posting Decoder reads a nursing or healthcare job posting like an experienced colleague: what the job really is, the schedule and pay it states, must-have requirements, watch-outs such as floating or a repayable sign-on bonus, and questions to ask the recruiter.

## Features
- Plain-words summary with watch-outs and questions for the recruiter
- Uses only what you write, no invented facts
- Free, no sign-up, nothing stored
