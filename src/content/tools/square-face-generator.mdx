## About
Build a square face, save a 256×256 PNG, then check how it crops as a round Discord or X avatar before you upload. Square Face Icon Generator brings together free browser-based avatar makers for square and oval faces, pixel art, characters and cats, plus a printable cube. Choose and recolor parts, then export PNGs with no watermark. No account needed.

## Features
- Preview the round crop for Discord and X
- Save face and profile picture makers as 256×256 PNGs
- Build pixel-art avatars, characters and cats
- Create chibi characters or a fold-up printable cube
- Recolor parts and export PNGs without a watermark
