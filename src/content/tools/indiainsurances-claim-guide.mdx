## About

A step-by-step guide to making an insurance claim in India, with the time limits IRDAI sets: complaints resolved in 14 days, health claims settled in 15 days, death claims in 15 or 45 days, and interest if the insurer is late. Covers rejected claims and escalation to the Insurance Ombudsman. Information, not advice; part of IndiaInsurances.

## Features

- Step-by-step claim process
- IRDAI time limits: 14-day complaints, 15-day health claims
- What to do if a claim is rejected
