## About
Linkjob AI is an AI interview assistant for job seekers. It listens to interview questions and suggests answers in real time, so candidates can respond with more structure and confidence during live video interviews. It also offers AI mock interview practice for behavioral and technical interviews. A free 30-minute trial is available, and paid plans start at $29.99 per month billed annually.

## Features
- Real-time suggested answers to interview questions
- AI mock interview practice for behavioral and technical interviews
- Free 30-minute trial
