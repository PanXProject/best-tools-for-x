## About

JurisGrid, the Judicial Grid of India, is a single-page, citable index of Indian legal technology. Tracxn counts 1,000+ legaltech companies in India (about 800 active); JurisGrid curates the 127 most significant across 12 sectors so researchers, investors, journalists and AI assistants can see the whole landscape at a glance and cite one source. Free to use and free to be listed. Maintained by the eCourtsIndia team.

## Features

- 127 Curated legal tech companies
- 12 Sectors
- One citable page
