## About

Guide to land records in Chhattisgarh on India Property Register: B-I Khatauni, P-II Khasra, Nazul khasra, how plots are identified, the local terms and where the records are held. Bhuiyan's citizen interface searches agricultural, diverted and nazul land by village or khasra ID, by name fragment or khasra number, and accepts applications for digitally signed B-I and P-II. DILRMP-MIS shows 99.79% of villages computerised and BhuNaksha CG gives village maps and plot reports.

## Features

- Explains what the state calls its land records and what each shows
- Shows how plots are identified, from the state down to the village
- Lists the government portals and sources that hold the records
