## About

IndiaCos lists 3,009 liquidation notices published by the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of India (IBBI), newest first, each linked to the company or LLP record. Useful for creditors, lenders and anyone checking a counterparty. Free to browse at https://indiacos.com/insolvency/liquidation. IndiaCos is a free directory of Indian companies, LLPs and GST registrations built from public MCA, GST, RBI, SEBI and IBBI records.

## Features

- Lists IBBI insolvency notices of one process type
- Newest first
- Links each to the company or LLP record
