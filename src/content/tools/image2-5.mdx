## About

Image2.5 is a free AI image generator and photo editor powered by GPT Image 2.5. Describe what you want or upload a photo, and get results in seconds with sharp detail, natural lighting and precise edits. Use Fast or Thinking modes, blend reference images and circle to edit only the area you mark. Output up to 4K.

## Features

- Text-to-image generation with GPT Image 2.5
- One-sentence photo editing
- Fast and Thinking modes
- Multi-image blending with reference images
- Circle to edit for localized changes
- Up to 4K output
- Readable text in posters, logos and thumbnails
- Photo restore and colorize
