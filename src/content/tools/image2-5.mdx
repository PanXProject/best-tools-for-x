## About
Image2.5 is an AI image generator and photo editor powered by GPT Image 2.5. Describe what you want in plain words, or upload a photo you want to change, and get results in seconds. Choose Fast or Thinking modes, blend multiple reference images, and use circle to edit to change only the area you mark. Output goes up to 4K.

## Features
- Text-to-image generation in plain words
- Photo editing from uploaded images
- Fast and Thinking modes
- Blend multiple reference images
- Circle to edit a marked area only
- Output up to 4K
- Pricing: Freemium from $9.9/mo
