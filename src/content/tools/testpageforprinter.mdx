## About
Free printable test pages that show exactly what is wrong with your printer. Precision test sheets (color, CMYK, black and white, nozzle check, alignment, scale, photo, duplex, blank) as true vector PDFs in US Letter and A4, plus plain-English troubleshooting guides for streaks, fading and blank pages, brand how-tos for Epson, Canon, HP and Brother, and tools like a scale calibration checker and an ink cost calculator. Free, no account.

- Precision test sheets in color, CMYK, black and white, and more
- Nozzle check, alignment, scale, photo, duplex, and blank test pages

- Plain-English troubleshooting guides for streaks, fading, and blank pages

- Scale calibration checker and ink cost calculator tools
