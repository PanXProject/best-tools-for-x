## About

Travel Nurse Contract Checker reads a travel nursing or allied travel offer the way an experienced traveler would: it lays out the taxable hourly rate, housing and meals stipends and the weekly total, showing the math, and what the offer states about cancellation, guaranteed hours and floating. Free, no sign-up.

## Features

- Taxable rate, stipends and weekly total with the math shown
- Cancellation, guaranteed hours, missed-shift and floating terms
- Red flags and questions to ask your recruiter
- Free, no sign-up
