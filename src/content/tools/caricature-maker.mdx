## About
Caricature Maker is a browser-based AI portrait tool for exploring playful interpretations of a photo or text idea. I am associated with this project; this listing was prepared with AI assistance. Visit [https://caricature-maker.online/](https://caricature-maker.online/) to explore styles and review results before sharing.

## Features
- Start a portrait from a photo or a written idea.
- Compare multiple caricature styles for different occasions.
- Adjust exaggeration to explore a gentler or bolder interpretation.
- Review facial expression and recognizable details before selecting a result.
- Use editing options to refine a generated image.
- Try a free 1K generation before deciding whether to continue.
- Prepare digital portraits for a profile, greeting or personal project.
- Check the current interface and pricing before additional usage.

## Practical notes
Generated images vary and should be reviewed for artifacts and suitability. Ask permission before uploading another person’s photo, and consult the current privacy and usage terms. A caricature is an interpretation, not a guaranteed likeness or independent evaluation.
