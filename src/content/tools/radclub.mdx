## About
Radclub (radclub.gg) is the club route to EU Minecraft server hosting and also Valheim server hosting, so a friend group can share one world. Friend group game servers are organised as an invite-only club. Chip-in happens together on a shared club balance. Gift cards can join that fund. Radbot setup raises the world for them. One-click mod installs stand by for new mods. Quick support sits with fast, good hardware behind the EU club. Always part of the listing is a free custom subdomain.

## Features
- EU Minecraft and Valheim server hosting
- Invite-only club for friend groups
- Shared club balance with gift cards
- Radbot setup and one-click mod installs
- Free custom subdomain
- World downloads and free 2-year world archive
