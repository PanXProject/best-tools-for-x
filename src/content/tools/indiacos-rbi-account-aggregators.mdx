## About

RBI-registered Account Aggregators in India. 17 RBI-registered Account Aggregators from the Reserve Bank of India's list, with type, layer and the company record. Free to browse at https://indiacos.com/nbfc/account-aggregator. IndiaCos is a free directory of Indian companies, LLPs and GST registrations built from public MCA, GST, RBI, SEBI and IBBI records.

## Features

- Lists RBI-registered companies of one NBFC type
- Shows the type and layer from the RBI list
- Links each entry to its company record
