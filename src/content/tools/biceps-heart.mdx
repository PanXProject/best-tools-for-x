## About
Biceps Heart is a focused AI photo-to-video tool for the Bicep Heart pose. Upload a permitted photo, choose Classic or LOVE, and download an approximately 7-second vertical MP4 in 720p or 1080p. Generation requires Google sign-in and one-time paid credit packs; there is no subscription.

## Features
- Create an approximately 7-second vertical MP4 from one photo
- Choose Classic or LOVE styles
- Download in 720p or 1080p
