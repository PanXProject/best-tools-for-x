## About

A free IndiaCos list of 90 associations in Himachal Pradesh with an active FCRA registration, with registration number, nature of work and pincode. FCRA (Foreign Contribution Regulation Act) registration lets an NGO, trust or society receive foreign donations; this page shows which associations in Himachal Pradesh hold one. Free to browse at https://indiacos.com/fcra/himachal-pradesh.

## Features

- Lists associations in one state with an active FCRA registration
- Shows registration number, nature of work and pincode
- Built from the public FCRA register
