## About
SimilarClip is an AI video tool for faceless YouTube channels. Pick a niche, connect a fresh YouTube channel and set a schedule: SimilarClip researches the topic, writes the script, splits it into scenes, then illustrates, narrates and publishes the finished video automatically. No camera, no editing and no source footage needed. Plans start at $39/month.

## Features
- Automatic script writing, illustration, narration and publishing
- 11 Visual styles and 35 natural voices
- Schedule videos after connecting your YouTube channel
- MCP server and REST API for agent-driven video generation
- Starter $39/mo (~18 video minutes), Pro $99/mo (3 channels), Studio $199/mo (10 channels)
