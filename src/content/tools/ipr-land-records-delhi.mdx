## About

Guide to land records in Delhi on India Property Register: Jamabandi and Khatouni, Registered deed (e-Search), UPIC record, how plots are identified, the local terms and where the records are held. Delhi's revenue record (DLRC) covers only a short list of rural villages: DILRMP-MIS shows 59 of 216 villages computerised (27.31%) and 3.24% map linkage.

## Features

- Explains what the state calls its land records and what each shows
- Shows how plots are identified, from the state down to the village
- Lists the government portals and sources that hold the records
