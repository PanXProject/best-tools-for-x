## About
PopUGC text to image turns prompts into clear, downloadable images in your browser—product shots, posters, social creatives, and more. Choose from popular image models, refine or upscale results, and batch-generate variations for listings and campaigns.

## Features
- Choose from popular image models
- Refine or upscale generated results
- Batch-generate variations for listings and campaigns
- Create product shots, posters, and social creatives
