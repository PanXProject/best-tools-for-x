## About
SitePatent is a discovery platform for startups, tools and online products that deserve attention. Builders submit their website, a person reviews it, and approved products get a permanent listing page organized by category with a link to the product.

## Features
- Every submission is reviewed by a person before it is published
- Permanent listing page per product, organized by category
- Free listing, with an optional paid upgrade for faster publishing
- Helps visitors find newly launched products from makers around the world
