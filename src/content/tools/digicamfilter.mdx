## About
DigicamFilter re-renders a photo you already have with a 2000s digicam or vintage-film look in the browser. It is not a phone camera app. Guests get a preview and signed-in users get two free photos.

## Features
- Upload a JPG, PNG, or WebP photo of an adult
- Pick a style such as CCD flash, date stamp, disposable, or photobooth
- Download a labeled JPG, carousel, or before-and-after comparison
