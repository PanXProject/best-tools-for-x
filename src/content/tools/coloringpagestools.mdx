## About
ColoringPagesTools gives you free printable coloring pages and complete coloring books in A4 or US Letter PDF, ready to download and print at home or in class. The library covers animals, holidays, mandalas, alphabets and seasonal themes, and an AI coloring page generator turns your own idea into a black-and-white line drawing you can print right away. No account, subscription or payment is needed.

## Features
- Hundreds of free printable coloring pages
- Download each page as A4 or US Letter PDF
- Complete coloring books in a single file
- AI generator turns an idea into a printable line drawing
- No account and no payment, print from any browser
