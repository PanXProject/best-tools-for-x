## About

VisibilityStack is an AI Search Optimisation platform that helps B2B companies improve how their brands appear across traditional search and AI-powered platforms. It helps businesses get discovered, accurately described, cited, and recommended by AI search engines.

## Features

- Crawl Assurance Engine
- Topical Authority Engine
- Trust Signal Engine

The platform is built around three core engines:

Crawl Assurance Engine
Helps ensure important website content is accessible to search engines and AI crawlers. It identifies technical issues that can prevent pages from being discovered, crawled, understood, or indexed.

Topical Authority Engine
Identifies important topics, questions, entities, and content gaps related to your market. It helps businesses build stronger topical coverage and authority around the subjects their buyers care about.

Trust Signal Engine
Strengthens the external signals that support brand credibility and visibility, including relevant backlinks, brand mentions, citations, and other authority signals.

VisibilityStack combines technical SEO, content strategy, AI Search Optimisation, topical authority, and trust signals to help businesses build sustainable visibility across search and AI platforms.
