## About
dcs4.com helps founders, brand builders, and domain investors find short .com names. Search the live availability index with wildcards (n*, qu??, *ak), watch domains as they drop, and snipe 4-letter .coms before they hit the open market. The index tracks hundreds of thousands of available names and updates daily.

## Features
- Wildcard search such as n*, qu??, and *ak
- Watch domains as they drop
- Daily-updated index of available 4-letter .com names
