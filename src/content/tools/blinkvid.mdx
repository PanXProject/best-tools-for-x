## About
Blinkvid turns an image or text prompt into a short video for TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts. Start with a scene or a photo you have permission to animate, describe the motion, and download the generated clip.

## Features
- Image-to-video and text-to-video creation
- Vertical social-video formats and themed templates
- Homepage starter: 4 seconds, 9:16, 480P for 8 credits
- Credit-based pricing with the cost shown before generation
