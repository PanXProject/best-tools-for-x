## About

Uttarakhand: 13 districts and 14,109 villages. How to check land records, maps, registration and encumbrance in Uttarakhand, and the government portals that. e-Bhulekh shows public RoR after a captcha, searched by khasra/gata, khata or khatedar name, with real-time mutation (dakhil-kharij) tracking; E-Bhunaksha gives the cadastral map and an RoR copy.
