## About
Cantian AI is an AI agent built on specialized skills for BaZi astrology, divination, feng shui, and naming, bringing traditional Eastern wisdom into the AI era. It turns classics such as the I Ching and San Ming Tong Hui into knowledge graphs and reasoning rules. Free content and a basic experience are available; deeper BaZi analysis and fuller reports are paid upgrades. On the web, iPhone, Android, and as a ChatGPT GPT.

## Features
- Compatibility reading packaged as a professional AI Skill an agent can call
- Liuyao divination with detailed interpretations, images, and suggested actions
- Feng shui home setup guidance drawn from a dedicated feng shui skill
- Chinese name selection as a professional naming skill
- A skill pool covering BaZi, Zi Wei Dou Shu, Qi Men Dun Jia, Mei Hua Yi Shu, LiuYao, Feng Shui, and name selection
