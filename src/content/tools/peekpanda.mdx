## About
PeekPanda is competitor intelligence for iOS apps. Open any app to see the Meta ads it keeps paying for and how long each has run, the US App Store searches it ranks for and the viral posts about it. Every number carries its observation date. Use it in the browser or from your AI agent over MCP.

## Features
- See the Meta ads an iOS app keeps paying for and how long each has run
- Track the US App Store searches an app ranks for
- Find viral posts about any iOS app
- Every number carries its observation date
- Use it from your AI agent over MCP at https://mcp.peekpanda.com/mcp
