## About

UGC Maker AI helps ecommerce sellers, marketers, and creators turn product photos into short videos for product demos, social posts, and UGC-style ads. Upload a photo and describe the scene or camera movement you want. You can also generate supporting images or pair a portrait with an audio track to create a talking presenter clip.

## Features

- Turn product photos into short video clips.
- Guide scenes and camera movements with text prompts.
- Use opening and closing images to guide a video transition.
- Generate supporting images for campaigns and social posts.
- Animate a portrait with an audio track to create a talking presenter.
