## About

Pikkai helps people discover AI products through a searchable directory, category pages and product listings that link to official websites. Makers can submit a product for editorial review. Browsing is free. The capacity-limited Free submission lane requires a one-time public link to Pikkai; an optional one-time US$5 Paid lane waives link verification and provides expedited review eligibility. Payment does not guarantee acceptance, publication, traffic or rankings.

## Features

- Search a directory of AI products by name or interest
- Browse product categories and follow links to official websites
- Submit maker-owned products for editorial review
