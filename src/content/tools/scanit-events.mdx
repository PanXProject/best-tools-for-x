## About
ScanIt Events is a QR-code memory platform for weddings and events. Hosts create an event page in minutes, design QR table cards in their own colors, and print one for every table. Guests scan with their camera — no app, no signup — and share photos and written messages that appear on a live board in real time. AI moderation keeps the board clean, guests can keep a submission private, and hosts can turn on RSVP, a registry link, and gifts. Afterward, one click downloads it all as a ZIP.

## Features
- Guests scan a QR code with any mobile camera — no app, no signup
- Photos (up to 10 per submission) and written messages from every guest
- Live board that updates in real time, with heart reactions
- AI moderation screens messages and photos before they appear
- Guests can mark a submission private, host-only
- Custom QR table-card designer: your colors, borders, fonts; print-ready PDF/PNG
- One QR for table cards, invitations, welcome signs, banners, and place cards
- RSVP with party size and names
