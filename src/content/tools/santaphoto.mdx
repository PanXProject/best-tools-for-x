## About
SantaPhoto is a browser-based AI Christmas photo generator for individuals, couples, families and pet owners. Upload your photos, choose a festive style and create a Christmas portrait, a personalized greeting card or a seasonal profile picture.

## Features
- AI Christmas portraits from reference photos
- Personalized holiday greeting cards
- Seasonal avatar maker
- Free public PFP downloads without login
- Credit-based generation with one-time packs
