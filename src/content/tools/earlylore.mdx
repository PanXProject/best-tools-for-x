## About
EarlyLore is a research workspace for founders: describe an idea or website, see who already solves the same problem, and follow public changes to pricing, positioning and features, each with its source and observation date. Currently a research preview, in English and Chinese.

## Features
- Find existing SaaS products that solve the same problem as your idea
- Published revenue signals, kept with their source
- Track public changes to pricing, positioning and features
- Source and observation date next to every change
- English and Chinese interface
