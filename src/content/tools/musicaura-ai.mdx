## About
Free Music Creator brings AI music generation, lyrics writing, and audio tools together in one platform. Users can explore song ideas, experiment with different genres, and process audio. A limited free plan is available.

## Features
- AI music and rap generation
- AI lyrics generator
- Vocal and drum removal
- Audio stem separation
- Key and BPM detection
- MIDI and MP3 tag editing
- Lo-fi and slowed reverb effects
