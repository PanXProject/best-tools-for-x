## About

Youth4work Sarkari Naukri lists government job updates in India for job seekers preparing for public sector exams, alongside free preparation and aptitude tests so candidates can see where they stand before they apply. Part of Youth4work, an Indian careers and talent assessment platform running since 2012 with free aptitude, skill and psychometric tests and assessed profiles.

## Features

- Lists government job updates in India
- Links to free preparation tests
- Shows candidates where they stand before applying
