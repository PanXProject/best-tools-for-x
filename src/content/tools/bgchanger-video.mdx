## About
BGChanger.video uses AI to remove or replace video backgrounds online. Upload a clip, choose transparent output or a solid color, preserve audio, and export in formats such as MP4, WebM, MOV, MKV, or GIF. It also offers prompt-guided character and scene replacement.

## Features
- Removes video backgrounds while keeping the subject clear
- Exports transparent WebM or solid-color backgrounds
- Supports MP4, WebM, MOV, MKV, and GIF exports
- Preserves original audio when needed
- Replaces characters and scenes using prompts and reference images
