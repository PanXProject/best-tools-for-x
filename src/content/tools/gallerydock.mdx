## About

GalleryDock is **online client gallery software for photographers and filmmakers**. Share branded galleries, collect client photo selections and deliver finished images in one place. Embed galleries into your own website, sell additional digital images and photo prints, and gather timestamped video feedback. Built for wedding, portrait, product and commercial photography, GalleryDock is available in English and German with a free plan and paid upgrades.

## Features

- **Client photo selection:** Collect favorites and comments before retouching.
- **Photo proofing galleries:** Keep client feedback alongside the images being reviewed.
- **Professional photo delivery:** Share finished photographs with configurable download settings.
- **Website embedding:** Display client galleries directly on your photography website.
- **Central gallery entrance:** Give clients access to their galleries using individual passwords.
- **Password protection:** Control access to private client galleries.
- **Customizable gallery designs:** Present photographs in a style that matches your brand.
- **Image watermarks:** Apply watermarks when sharing photos for selection.
- **Digital image sales:** Offer additional photographs through the integrated shop.
- **Photo print sales:** Let clients order prints from their gallery.
- **Timestamped video feedback:** Connect revision comments to specific moments in a film.
- **Photos and videos together:** Organize client media within one platform.
- **Flexible photography workflows:** Support weddings, portraits, family sessions, corporate headshots and product shoots.
- **Browser-based access:** Use galleries on desktop and mobile devices without installing an app.
- **English and German:** Serve clients with two supported interface languages.
- **Free plan:** Explore GalleryDock with optional paid upgrades as your needs grow.
