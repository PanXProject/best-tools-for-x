## About

Andaman & Nicobar: 3 districts and 191 villages. How to check land records, maps, registration and encumbrance in Andaman & Nicobar, and the government. Dweep Bhoomi is a transaction portal behind signup (mutation, demarcation, subdivision, diversion, mortgage) with no public record search. DILRMP-MIS shows 100% computerisation (205 villages). Deeds run on NGDRS since 2018, and TNRERA covers RERA here.

## Features

- Lists every district of the state with its village count
- Explains how to check land records, maps, registration and encumbrance
- Links the government portals that hold the records
