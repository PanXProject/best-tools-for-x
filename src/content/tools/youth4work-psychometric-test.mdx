## About

A free online psychometric test from Youth4work. Job seekers and students can take it alongside free skill and aptitude tests, see where they stand against others and add the results to an assessed profile employers can find. Youth4work is an Indian talent assessment and careers platform founded in 2012 in Jaipur.

## Features

- Free online psychometric test
- Alongside free skill and aptitude tests
- Add results to an assessed profile
