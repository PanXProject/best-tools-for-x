## About

A free IndiaRestros page listing 94,791 street food places in India, most in Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Delhi and Mumbai. Of these, 3,691 are pure veg, 88,328 list a phone number (94%), 80,773 have timings and 50,705 have a menu on file. Pick a city to narrow the list. IndiaRestros is a free, open guide to restaurants in India with no paid rankings and no commissions.
