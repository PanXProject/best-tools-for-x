## About
MuseMate is an AI music video generator for independent musicians. Upload a track, approve a storyboard written from your lyrics and get scenes cut on the beat. The same credit balance makes Spotify Canvas loops, album covers, clean press photos and enhanced gig photos. Paid subscription from $35/month.

## Features
- Beat-synced AI music videos up to 60 seconds
- Spotify Canvas loops from your cover art
- AI album cover generator with genre presets
- Background remover for band photos
- Image enhancer for gig photos
