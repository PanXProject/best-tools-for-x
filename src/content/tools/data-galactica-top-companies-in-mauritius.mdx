## About

A free Data Galactica list of the best-known companies and organisations in Mauritius, ranked by how much its sources cover them and then by size (not by revenue). The list opens with Air Mauritius Limited, Mauritius Commercial Bank, Rogers Group and Mauritius Telecom; each entry has a permanent Galactica ID, its status, industry and city, a data grade and the sources behind it.
