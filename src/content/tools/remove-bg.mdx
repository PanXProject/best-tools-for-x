## About
Remove Bg (remove-bg.dev) is an online AI background remover that creates clean transparent PNG cutouts. Upload a photo or paste a URL, automatically separate the subject, refine edges with erase or restore, and download HD transparent results for products, portraits, and design work.

## Features
- One-click AI background removal
- Transparent PNG download in HD
- Batch upload for multiple images
- Edge refine with erase and restore
- Works on products, people, and pets
- Browser-based freemium workflow
