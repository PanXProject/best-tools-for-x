## About
WaveXML auto-syncs Zoom / Sound Devices dual-system audio to camera footage via an XML round-trip for DaVinci Resolve, Adobe Premiere Pro, and Final Cut Pro. Free Mac and Windows beta — full app, no clip or feature limits. Local processing; no plugin; no upload.

## Features
- XML round-trip workflow for fast dual-system audio sync
- Works with DaVinci Resolve, Adobe Premiere Pro, and Final Cut Pro
- Free Mac and Windows beta with no clip or feature limits
- Local processing with no plugin and no upload
