## About

Guide to land records in Gujarat on India Property Register: VF-7 (7/12), VF-8A, VF-6 (Hakk Patrak), how plots are identified, the local terms and where the records are held. AnyRoR gives open search of rural VF-7, VF-8A and VF-6 (including khata by owner name) and a separate urban screen for property cards and unit property cards; digitally signed RoR is on i-ORA. DILRMP-MIS shows 100% of villages computerised.

## Features

- Explains what the state calls its land records and what each shows
- Shows how plots are identified, from the state down to the village
- Lists the government portals and sources that hold the records
