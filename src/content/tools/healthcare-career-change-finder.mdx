## About
Healthcare Career Change Finder is a free AI tool for adults moving into healthcare from retail, restaurants, offices, warehouses, teaching or anywhere else. Say what you do now, what you are good at, how long you can train and how much patient contact you want: it suggests three healthcare jobs that fit (for example CNA, phlebotomist, medical assistant, pharmacy technician), how people usually get in, what carries over from your current job and first steps for this month.

## Features
- Free to use, no sign-up
- Nothing you enter is stored
- Built on Claude by Anthropic
