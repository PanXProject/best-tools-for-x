## About

A free IndiaCos directory of 4,24,104 GST-registered businesses in Maharashtra: companies, LLPs, partnerships and proprietorships, browsable district by district, each with GSTIN, registration status and registration date and a link to the full business report. Built from public GST and company registry data.

## Features

- Covers 4,24,104 GST-registered businesses
- Browse district by district
- Shows GSTIN, status and registration date
