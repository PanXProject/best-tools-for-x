## About
Free snow day chance calculator for US school districts, from weather service forecasts. Type in a ZIP code or pick a school district, and it gives a snow day chance based on National Weather Service forecasts and long-term NOAA weather normals. Covers school districts across the United States, with a page per district plus state pages and guides on snow day rules. Forecasts refresh hourly through the snow season, from mid-October to mid-April. Free with no account to create.

## Features
- Snow day chance from National Weather Service forecasts
- Long-term NOAA weather normals in the calculation
- Type in a ZIP code or pick a school district
- A page per school district, plus state pages
- Guides on snow day rules
- Forecasts refresh hourly through the snow season, from mid-October to mid-April
- Free with no account to create
