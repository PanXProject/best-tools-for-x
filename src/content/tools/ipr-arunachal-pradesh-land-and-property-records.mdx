## About

Arunachal Pradesh: 26 districts and 5,582 villages. How to check land records, maps, registration and encumbrance in Arunachal Pradesh, and the government. DoLR lists no RoR portal for Arunachal Pradesh and DILRMP-MIS shows 0 RoRs and 0% computerisation over 1,412 villages. The LISA portal (launched 3 Feb 2025) handles land allotment, revenue payment and LHC verification only. Deeds moved to NGDRS in February 2025.

## Features

- Lists every district of the state with its village count
- Explains how to check land records, maps, registration and encumbrance
- Links the government portals that hold the records
