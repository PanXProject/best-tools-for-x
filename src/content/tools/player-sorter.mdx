## About
Player Sorter is a free browser game that helps football fans rank players through head-to-head matchups across clubs, women’s clubs, and World Cup national teams.

## Features
- Build a personal ranking with fewer comparisons
- Support clicks, taps, keyboard shortcuts, ties, and undo
- Save progress and share ranking results as text, links, or images
