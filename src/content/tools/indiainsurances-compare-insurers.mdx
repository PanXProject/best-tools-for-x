## About

A free IndiaInsurances tool that compares any pair of Indian insurance companies on claim settlement ratio, claim rejection, complaints and solvency, using IRDAI data and the insurers' own public disclosures. IndiaInsurances reads every policy from its own documents and the IRDAI registers, takes no commission and makes no sales calls.

## Features

- Reads each policy from its own documents
- Lets you sort columns or compare two policies
- Takes no commission and makes no sales calls
