## About
GuruTrans is a suite of browser-based AI translation and text tools. Translate between languages (English-Chinese, Japanese-English, Greek-English and more), rewrite text in historical styles (Shakespearean, Fancy English, Old English), and encode/decode Morse, ASCII and binary. Paste text, convert, and check results. Free to try.

## Features
- Translate between multiple language pairs, including English-Chinese
- Rewrite text in Shakespearean, Fancy English, and Old English styles
- Encode and decode Morse code, ASCII, and binary
- Paste text to convert it and check results
