## About
British Accent Generator is an AI British and UK accent text-to-speech workspace with 315 regionally labelled voices, including RP, young British, Scottish, Welsh, and Northern Irish.

## Features
- Preview 315 UK voices before you generate audio
- Paste a script and download natural British English
- Includes Scottish, Welsh, and Northern Irish accents
- Voice Clone, Voice Design, and AI Lip Sync
