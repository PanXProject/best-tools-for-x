## About

British Accent Generator is an AI British/UK accent text-to-speech workspace with 315 regionally labelled voices: RP, young British, Scottish, Welsh and Northern Irish. Preview speakers, paste a script and download natural UK English audio for videos, lessons, podcasts and demos. Free daily credits.

## Features

- 315 Regionally labelled UK voices
- Preview speakers and download audio
- Voice Clone and Voice Design
- Voice Effects and AI Lip Sync
- Free daily credits for everyone
