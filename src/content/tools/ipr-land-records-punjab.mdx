## About

Guide to land records in Punjab on India Property Register: Jamabandi, Fard, Mutation (Intkal), how plots are identified, the local terms and where the records are held. Punjab's jamabandi portal states that digital RoR is available since 2002, but its JavaScript search did not load on 11 Oct 2026, and the DoLR link is the department website. DILRMP-MIS shows 99.63% of villages computerised.

## Features

- Explains what the state calls its land records and what each shows
- Shows how plots are identified, from the state down to the village
- Lists the government portals and sources that hold the records
