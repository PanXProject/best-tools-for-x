## About
Probably Stolen Wiki is an independent, unofficial gameplay reference with 267 item entries, pictures and base prices, plus ten language options. Ask AI answers questions from published wiki material and links its sources. Reference browsing is free without an account; AI usage and service-budget limits apply. The site is separate from the game's developer and publisher.

## Features
- Browse 267 illustrated item references and base prices
- Read gameplay guides with ten language options
- Ask gameplay questions and check linked wiki sources
- Browse reference pages without creating an account
