## About
Nano Banana is an online AI image editor and generator. Upload a photo and describe your edits, or create a new image from a text prompt. Use it to explore visual ideas, edit backgrounds, and create social media images in your browser.

## Features
- Edit uploaded photos with natural-language instructions.
- Generate new images from text descriptions.
- Change backgrounds and modify visual elements.
- Apply different artistic styles to images.
- Choose from multiple image aspect ratios.
