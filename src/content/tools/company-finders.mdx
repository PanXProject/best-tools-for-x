## About
For people who sell websites and SEO to local businesses. Pick a business type and a city and count the ones on Google Maps with no website, free. For $3 per 1,000 businesses we open every listed website on the day you order and say which are broken or out of date.

## Features
- Free count of businesses with no website, for a type of business and a city, with three sample rows and no account
- Our own checker opens every listed website on the day you order
- Five results: no website, broken, out of date, loads fine, could not check
- One file as an Excel workbook or a CSV, with 21 columns and a Google Maps link on every row
- Businesses to call come first, most reviewed at the top
- $3 Per 1,000 businesses, $3 minimum for a list, no account and no subscription
- REST API and MCP server at the same rate, from a balance of credits that do not expire
- 62 Types of business in 5,196 cities and towns across six countries
