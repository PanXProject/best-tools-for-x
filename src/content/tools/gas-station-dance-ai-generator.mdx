## About
Gas Station Dance AI Generator is a browser-based AI video generator. It recreates the viral gas station dance from 1 to 3 photos as a 15-second clip that keeps the original three-shot template, moves, cuts and music.

## Features
- Turns 1 to 3 photos into a gas station dance video
- Keeps the original forecourt dance, selfie walk and car exit shots
- 15-Second clips with the original music and cuts
- 9:16 Or 16:9 MP4 export for TikTok, Reels and Shorts
- No filming or prompt writing needed
