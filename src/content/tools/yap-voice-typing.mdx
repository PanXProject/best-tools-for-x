## About
Yap Voice Typing is a free browser extension for voice typing. Click into any text box on the web, press Alt+Shift+Y and talk. Your words are typed where the cursor is.

## Features
- Works in any text box on the web
- Speech model runs inside your browser, so your voice is never uploaded
- No account, no analytics
- English or any of 99 languages, works offline after a one-time download
