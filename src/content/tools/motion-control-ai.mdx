## About
Motion Control AI is a free browser-based AI video tool that turns a reference image and a motion video into a more controllable character animation workflow. It helps creators preserve character identity while guiding movement, timing, framing, camera rhythm, and expression. Visit [Motion Control AI](https://motioncontrolai.online/) to try the workflow without installing desktop software.

## Features
- Transfer movement from a reference motion video
- Preserve character identity across generated frames
- Guide pose timing and expressive motion
- Create browser-based AI character videos
- Support creative prototyping and social content
- Reduce manual iteration on movement consistency
