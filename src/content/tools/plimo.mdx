## About
Plimo brings the newest AI image and video models into one account: GPT Image, Nano Banana, Seedream and FLUX for pictures; Veo, Kling, Seedance and more for video. Pick a trending template and add your photo, or describe your own idea and choose a model. New accounts get free credits to start, and results come without a watermark.

## Features
- Trending templates that turn your photo into a result in one click
- The newest image and video models in one generator, grouped by company
- Photo editing for hair, outfit, background or style
- Photo or text to short video, with sound on supported models
- Credit cost shown before you generate, with failed runs refunded
- Available in 14 languages
