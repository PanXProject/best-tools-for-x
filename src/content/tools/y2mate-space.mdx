## About
  y2mate.space is a free, browser-based YouTube downloader. Paste a video link and choose MP3 audio or MP4 video to save it to your device. No installation,
  account, or payment is required. Only download content you own or have permission to save.

  ## Features
- Convert YouTube videos to MP3 audio at 128 kbps
- Download MP4 videos at up to 720p
- Use directly in your browser without installing software
- No account or payment required
- Works on desktop and mobile browsers
- Supports regular YouTube links, Shorts, and youtu.be links
