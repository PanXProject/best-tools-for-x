## About
Kuca is a scene-based AI video maker for films, short dramas, and shorts. Give it a line of text, a reference image, or a short clip, and it builds polished scenes with leading models like Seedance 2.5 and MiniMax H3. A dedicated workspace keeps multi-scene projects organized, and free trial credits get you started.

## Features
- Scene-by-scene AI video directing for films and short dramas
- Builds polished scenes from text, images, or clips
- Multi-scene project workspace with free trial credits
