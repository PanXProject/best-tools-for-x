## About
Timed HSK 3.0 mock tests for levels 1-9: instant scores, an explanation for every answer, AI writing marking and HSKK speaking scores.

## Features
- Timed HSK 3.0 mock tests for levels 1 to 9, with each section on its own countdown on the real exam clock
- Instant scores with an explanation for every answer and a pass probability
- AI writing marking on content, language and structure, quoting the sentence behind each deduction
- HSKK speaking practice with 15 full sets, recorded answers, pictures and model answers, each scored
- Mistake book that groups missed questions by section and question type, with their explanations
- Free 15-question placement test, score calculator, 2026 exam calendar with reminders, and 11,000-word HSK 3.0 lists
