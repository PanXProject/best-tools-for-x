## About
Memebop is a channel for the AI video trends taking over TikTok and Reels. Every trend becomes a single page: feed it the photos it asks for, press one button, get a 9:16 MP4 ready for TikTok, Reels and Shorts. No prompts, no app install, no editing. Three episodes are on air and new ones go live within ~48 hours of a trend taking off. Paid with one-time credit packs from $4.99.

## Features
- One page per trend: upload the photos it asks for and the video renders
- One-button render to a 9:16 MP4 for TikTok, Reels and Shorts
- Three episodes on air: Bulin 47 Freestyle, Raindance, Zombie Love Story
- One-time credit packs from $4.99, no subscription, failed videos refunded
