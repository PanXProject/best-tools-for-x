## About

Talent forums on Youth4work, India's talent assessment and careers platform: students and job seekers ask questions and share answers around the skills they are building, alongside free online skill, aptitude and psychometric tests and an assessed profile employers can find. Youth4work was founded in 2012 and is based in Jaipur.

## Features

- Talent forums by skill
- Free skill and aptitude tests
- Assessed profiles
