## About
Feng Shui Bedroom Planner is a free online bedroom layout generator and checker. Draw your room, place the bed, doors, windows and mirrors, and the tool checks your layout against common feng shui principles such as the command position and mirror placement. It then previews better layouts you can try. No signup is needed.

## Features
- Draw your bedroom with bed, doors, windows and mirrors
- Check bed placement against feng shui rules
- Command position and mirror placement checks
- Preview better bedroom layouts
- Works in the browser with no signup
