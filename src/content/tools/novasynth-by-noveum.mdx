## About
NovaSynth is a hosted voice agent testing service. Configure AI caller personas and scenarios, call authorized phone numbers or LiveKit endpoints, and review recordings, transcripts and evaluations. An account and configured endpoint are required. Free plan: 2,500 shared credits/month. Pro starts at $69/month. Voice uses 100 credits/minute; evaluations may use additional credits.

## Features
- Configurable AI caller personas and conversation scenarios
- Single and batch voice agent test calls
- Interruptions and background noise for realistic testing
- Recordings, transcripts and call evaluations
- Repeatable scenarios for voice agent regression testing
