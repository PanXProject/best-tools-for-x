## About
  Floor Plan AI, built by PlanArt, helps homeowners, renters and designers explore room layouts before paying for drawings. Describe bedrooms, bathrooms and total area, or upload a sketch, and get a layout concept in 2D, 2.5D or 3D isometric style.

  ## Features
- Text brief or room template to floor plan concept
- Plot size input in meters or feet
- Technical 2D, furnished 2.5D and 3D isometric styles
- Edit one area of a plan by typing a change
- Sketch to floor plan and 2D to 3D conversion
- Manual Maker with PNG, SVG, JSON and DXF export
