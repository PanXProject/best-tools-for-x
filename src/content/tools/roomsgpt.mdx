## About
RoomsGPT is a free AI room redesign tool. Upload a photo of any room, house exterior or garden, pick a style or describe your own, and compare before-and-after concepts in seconds. No sign-up is needed to start.

## Features
- Redesign any room, house exterior or garden from one photo
- Browse 61+ interior styles or describe your own look
- Before-and-after slider to compare each redesign
