## About
LivePhotoNow is a browser-based AI live photo maker. Upload a still photo and it adds real motion - a gentle breath, a smile, a dance - in about a minute, with no app install. Files stay private and it works on iPhone and Android.

- Animate any still photo into lifelike video
- Talking Photo with lip-synced speech
