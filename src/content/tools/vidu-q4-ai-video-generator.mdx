## About

Vidu Q4 is an AI video generator for creators and marketing teams that need short clips without a full production pipeline. According to the product page, [Vidu Q4](https://viduai.net) turns text prompts and reference images into short cinematic video inside the browser, so a rough idea can become a preview in one session. It fits launch teasers, social posts, ad variations, and quick concept checks with stakeholders.

## Features

- Prompt-to-video and reference-to-video generation in the browser
- Short cinematic clips suited to social and launch assets
- Iteration-friendly flow for testing several creative directions
- No timeline editor needed to get a first result
- Runs on the web with no desktop install
