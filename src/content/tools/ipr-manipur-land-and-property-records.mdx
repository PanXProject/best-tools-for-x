## About

Manipur: 16 districts and 2,613 villages. How to check land records, maps, registration and encumbrance in Manipur, and the government portals that hold them. The DoLR link for Manipur is an e-GRAS tax form; the real land records site, Loucha Pathap, takes land tax by patta and dag, shows RoR transaction status and advertises jamabandi download. DILRMP-MIS shows 96.36% of villages computerised (583 of 605). Deeds run on NGDRS since 2018.

## Features

- Lists every district of the state with its village count
- Explains how to check land records, maps, registration and encumbrance
- Links the government portals that hold the records
