## About
Remove Watermark from Video is an online AI tool that cleans watermarks, logos and text overlays from videos you own or have the rights to use. It rebuilds the area behind the mark instead of blurring or cropping the frame. Freemium, and it runs in the browser.
## Features
- Automatic watermark detection with no manual masking
- Rebuilds the background instead of blurring or cropping
- Works in the browser for MP4, MOV and WebM up to 100 MB
