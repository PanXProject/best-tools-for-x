## About
Dola AI is a browser-based AI video and image generator built around Seedance 2.5. Type a prompt or upload a photo and the [dola ai video & image generator](https://dolai.pro/) turns it into a 5–30 second video with sound, right in your browser. No app, no watermark, works in the US. Dolai is an independent tool and is not affiliated with Dola or ByteDance.

## Features
- Text to video: describe the scene, action and camera to get a 5–30 second clip with sound
- Image to video: your photo becomes the first frame while faces and outfits stay consistent
- AI image generator for still images in the same workspace
- No watermark: clips download as clean MP4 files
- Works in any browser, including in the United States
- Vertical 9:16 for TikTok, Reels and Shorts or 16:9 for YouTube
- Credit cost shown before you generate, plus 100 free credits on signup
