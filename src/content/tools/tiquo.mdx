## About
Tiquo is a paid, unified operating platform for hospitality businesses. It connects bookings, point of sale, payments, memberships, CRM, marketing, inventory and reporting for hotels, restaurants, spas and members’ clubs. Available on the web, iPhone/iPad and macOS.

## Features
- Bookings, reservations and waitlists
- Point of sale and online/in-person payments
- CRM, memberships and loyalty
- Inventory and product catalog
- Marketing automation, email and SMS
- Multi-location operations, reporting and analytics
