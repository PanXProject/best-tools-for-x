## About
Rap Your Life turns your photos into AI rap videos using preset performance templates. Preview scenes for free, pick a template and add your photos to personalize the performers. Download your finished video as an MP4. No prompts to write.

## Features
- Preview scenes for free, then pick a template
- Add your photos to personalize the performers
- Download your finished video as an MP4
- One-time packs from $4.99 USD for 150 credits, no subscription
