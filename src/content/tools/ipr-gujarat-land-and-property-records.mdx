## About

Gujarat: 33 districts and 18,345 villages. How to check land records, maps, registration and encumbrance in Gujarat, and the government portals that hold. AnyRoR gives open search of rural VF-7, VF-8A and VF-6 (including khata by owner name) and a separate urban screen for property cards and unit property cards; digitally signed RoR is on i-ORA. DILRMP-MIS shows 100% of villages computerised. Deeds run on GARVI with online EC and Index-2, and stamp value is the Jantri.

## Features

- Lists every district of the state with its village count
- Explains how to check land records, maps, registration and encumbrance
- Links the government portals that hold the records
