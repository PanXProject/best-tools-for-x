## About
AI Manga Translator translates manga pages into another language while preserving artwork, panels, and speech-bubble placement. It is designed for readers, creators, and localization workflows that need translated dialogue in page context.

## Features
- Preserves manga panels and artwork layout
- Places translated dialogue in speech bubbles
- Supports image-based comic localization
