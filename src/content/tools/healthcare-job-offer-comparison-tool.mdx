## About
Healthcare Job Offer Comparison Tool is a free AI tool for anyone weighing two US healthcare job offers. Paste each offer as it was given: it lines them up, works out the yearly pay from the numbers each offer states, and lists what favors each offer and what to ask before deciding. It never invents a number or tells you which to take. No sign-up.

## Features
- Base pay, differentials, sign-on bonus terms and benefits side by side
- Yearly pay worked out from stated numbers
- Questions to ask before deciding
- Free, no sign-up, nothing stored
