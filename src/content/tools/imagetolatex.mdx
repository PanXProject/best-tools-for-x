## About
ImageToLaTeX converts a photo, screenshot or scanned page of math into LaTeX source and editable Word equations. Upload the image, let the recognizer read printed or handwritten formulas, then copy the LaTeX or download an editable equation for your document. It keeps a conversion history and supports batch uploads for longer worksheets.

## Features
- Recognize formulas from photos and screenshots
- Convert an image into clean LaTeX source
- Export equations as editable Word equations
- Batch upload several formula images
- Reopen past conversions from history
- Runs in the browser with no install
