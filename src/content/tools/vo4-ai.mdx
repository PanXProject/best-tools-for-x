## About

VO4 AI is a browser-based workspace for creating videos, images, and music. Creators can turn a written scene into a short clip, animate a still photo, or generate images and music from prompts. Multiple AI models are available in one workspace.

## Features

- Generate short videos from text prompts
- Animate still photos with written motion instructions
- Generate images from text descriptions
- Create music from written prompts
- Choose from multiple AI models in one browser workspace
