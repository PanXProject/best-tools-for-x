## About

A plain page on where Data Galactica's records come from: company registers (India's MCA master data, UK Companies House, GLEIF LEI with parent companies, US SEC EDGAR), Wikidata (CC0), licensed director data, careers-platform profiles with privacy settings respected, and lead lists, which are kept out of search engines until another source confirms them.
