## About

Delhi: 11 districts and 206 villages. How to check land records, maps, registration and encumbrance in Delhi, and the government portals that hold them. Delhi's revenue record (DLRC) covers only a short list of rural villages: DILRMP-MIS shows 59 of 216 villages computerised (27.31%) and 3.24% map linkage. Urban property is a stack of separate systems: e-Dastavej/NGDRS for deeds, MCD UPIC for tax, DDA allotment files, and PM-UDAY for listed unauthorised colonies.

## Features

- Lists every district of the state with its village count
- Explains how to check land records, maps, registration and encumbrance
- Links the government portals that hold the records
