## About
BaldFade is an AI hairstyle preview tool. Upload one clear front-facing photo, choose a style such as bald, buzz cut, crew cut fade, high fade or mid fade, then pay $4.99 for a single image or use a credit. The result is a visual preview, not a guarantee: AI models may also change facial features, lighting or background.

## Features
- Bald, buzz cut and fade previews from one photo
- HD image with no watermark on a private result page
- No account needed for a single $4.99 image
- Credit packs: 3 for $9.99, 5 for $14.99, 10 for $24.99
- Not charged if your photo fails review
- Optional Norwood hairline self-check in the browser
