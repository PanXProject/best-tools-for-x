## About
Healthcare Job Offer Acceptance or Decline Email is a free AI tool for healthcare workers with a job offer in hand. Say whether you accept or decline and the terms as offered (start date, pay and differentials, schedule, sign-on bonus): it writes a short email that confirms those terms in writing and asks for next steps, or turns the offer down warmly. It restates only the terms you enter.

## Features
- Free to use, no sign-up
- Nothing you enter is stored
- Built on Claude by Anthropic
