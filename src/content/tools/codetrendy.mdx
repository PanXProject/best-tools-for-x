## About
CodeTrendy is a curated product discovery directory for startups, SaaS tools and indie projects. Every submission passes a content check before it goes live, and each approved product gets a permanent profile with category, tech stack, screenshots, reviews and a link.

## Features
- Screened listings with a content check before publishing
- Browse by category, tech stack and the AI tool a product was built with
- Weekly trending list and launch calendar
- Permanent listing pages with screenshots and user reviews
- Free listing option plus one-time paid plans from $19
- Llms.txt index and RSS feeds for new listings
