## About

Car Customizer is a [car customization website](https://car-customizer.online/) for exploring paint, wheels, wraps, stance, body-kit directions and spoilers on an authorized photograph. Compare versions, continue an earlier edit and download a visual reference. New accounts get nine welcome credits once; further use requires paid credits or a subscription.

## Features

- Upload a clear JPG, PNG or WebP car photograph within the current 10 MB limit.
- Explore exterior color directions without presenting the concept as a physical paint match.
- Compare wheel, wrap and other appearance ideas against the starting photograph.
- Return to an earlier version when a different visual direction deserves another look.
- Continue editing a selected concept rather than treating every image as a separate plan.
- Download a reference for discussion; confirm dimensions, fitment, legality and safety separately.
