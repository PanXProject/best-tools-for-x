## About

AI Image Text Editor is a browser tool for replacing selected wording in flattened images. Use it for authorized mockups, teaching screenshots and small graphic corrections when an editable source is unavailable. Upload JPG, PNG or WebP, select the text area and describe the replacement. Review spelling and surrounding detail before sharing. Explore the [edit screenshot](https://image-text-editor.online/) workflow.

## Features

- Upload common JPG, PNG and WebP images up to 10 MB from the browser.
- Select up to eight text regions in one task to keep independent corrections organized.
- Describe the exact replacement wording instead of rebuilding a complete graphic.
- Use neighboring lettering and background as visual references for AI replacement.
- Compare the generated candidate with the source for spelling, alignment and nearby details.
- Start with free credits; edits consume credits, with paid subscriptions and credit packs for further use.

## Disclosure

This is a project-associated, AI-assisted listing, not an independent review or an accuracy benchmark. Keep an untouched source and clearly identify illustrative edits. Visual text replacement is not secure redaction and does not prove authenticity or remove information from metadata or other copies. Use only images you are authorized to edit and share.
