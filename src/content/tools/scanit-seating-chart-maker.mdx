## About
Free seating chart maker from ScanIt Events. Choose round or rectangular tables and 4–16 seats per table, start with as many tables as you need (up to 50), click any seat and type a guest name, then download a print-ready PDF or PNG to send to your venue, planner or caterer. Tables can be added, removed and renamed at any point. No signup, and nothing you enter leaves your browser.

## Features
- Round or rectangular tables, 4–16 seats each
- Up to 50 tables, add, remove and rename any time
- Click a seat and type the guest name
- Download a print-ready PDF or PNG
- No signup — nothing you enter leaves your browser
