## About
Healthcare Recruiter Reply Writer is a free AI tool for healthcare workers who hear from recruiters about staff, travel and per diem jobs. Paste the email, text or LinkedIn message, pick what you want to say (interested, interested if it fits, not now, no thanks, stop contacting me) and add what matters to you: it writes a short reply in the right length for the channel that asks for what the message leaves out (pay, shift, ratio, contract terms).

## Features
- Free to use, no sign-up
- Nothing you enter is stored
- Built on Claude by Anthropic
