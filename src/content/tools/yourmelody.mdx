## About
yourmelody turns what you know about someone into an original song with their name in it. Answer a few questions in about three minutes. AI writes the lyrics, you edit them, and an AI voice sings two versions. Hear a free preview, then unlock the full song as MP3s for $6.99 per song, one-time, no subscription.

## Features
- Original lyrics written from your answers, with the name in the song
- Edit any line or ask for a rewrite
- Two AI-sung versions in the style you pick
- Free preview before paying
- MP3 downloads of both versions
