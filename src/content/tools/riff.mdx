## About
Riff is a free Chrome extension that turns your rough point into a fresh reply for any post on X, LinkedIn, Facebook or Instagram. It uses Gemini Nano, the AI built into Chrome, so it runs on your computer with no account and no API key. It never posts or sends for you.

## Features
- Fresh replies for posts on X, LinkedIn, Facebook and Instagram
- Runs on your computer with Gemini Nano inside Chrome
- Answers your DMs and drafts your own posts
- No account and no API key needed
- Never posts or sends anything for you
