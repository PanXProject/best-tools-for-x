## About
Free Chrome extension. Paste your rough point and Chrome's built-in AI (Gemini Nano) writes a fresh reply for X, LinkedIn, Facebook or Instagram. On-device, no account, no API key. Never posts for you.

## Features
- Writes fresh replies from your rough point
- Runs on-device with Chrome's built-in Gemini Nano
- Works on X, LinkedIn, Facebook and Instagram
- No account and no API key required
- Never posts anything for you
