## About
Riff is a free Chrome extension that drafts social replies on-device with Gemini Nano. Nothing leaves the user's device. Maker: Michael Zima / Zima Media OÜ.

## Features
- Drafts social media replies directly in your browser
- Runs fully on-device with Gemini Nano
- Nothing ever leaves your device
- Completely free to use
