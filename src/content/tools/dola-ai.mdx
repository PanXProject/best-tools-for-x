## About
Dola AI is a browser-based creative workspace for generating videos from text prompts or images. It also supports image generation and editing for social content, product visuals, and creative exploration.

## Features
- Generate videos from text prompts
- Turn still images into video clips
- Create images from text descriptions
- Edit images in a browser-based workspace
