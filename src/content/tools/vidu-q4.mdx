## About

Vidu Q4 is an AI video generation tool for creators, marketers, and product teams that need to turn text prompts and reference images into short video clips. According to the product page, it focuses on a fast browser-based workflow from prompt to preview.

## Features

- Text-to-video and image-to-video generation
- Fast AI-assisted workflow for launch assets and social clips
- Simple browser-based product experience
