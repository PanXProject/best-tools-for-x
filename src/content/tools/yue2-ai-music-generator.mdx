## About

YuE2 is an AI music generator that turns lyrics and ideas into complete songs with vocals and accompaniment. It supports Text to Music and Music to Music, with controls for style, mood, vocals, instruments, and tempo. YuE2 is built for songwriters, musicians, producers, and creators who want to test lyrics, create demos, explore styles, and generate original music in the browser.

## Features

- Turn lyrics and song ideas into complete AI-generated songs with vocals.
- Generate vocals and accompaniment together in one browser-based workflow.
- Use Text to Music to create new songs from lyrics and creative direction.
- Use Music to Music to explore new interpretations from reference audio.
- Control genre, mood, instruments, vocal style, tempo, and musical direction.
- Create quick songwriting demos without building every part of a track manually.
- Explore different musical styles using the same lyrics or song concept.
- Generate playable music directly in the browser without complex setup.
