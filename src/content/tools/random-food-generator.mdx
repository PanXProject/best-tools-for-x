## About
Random Food Generator is a free browser tool for anyone who cannot decide what to eat. Pick a meal, cuisine or diet, get one food idea in a click, compare recent picks, and spin finalists on a customizable food wheel. No account required.

## Features
- Random food ideas by meal, cuisine or diet
- Compare recent picks side by side
- Customizable food wheel for finalists
- Random dinner generator with filters
- Weekly meal planner for 3, 5 or 7 dinners
