## About

A free directory of judges across the Supreme Court of India, the 25 High Courts, district and magistrate courts and tribunals, linked to the public case records, orders and cause lists that eCourtsIndia indexes. From public court records; not legal advice. Part of eCourtsIndia.

## Features

- Judges of the Supreme Court, High Courts, district courts and tribunals
- Linked to public case records and orders
- Free to browse
