## About
GMaps Email Extractor is a Chrome and Edge extension for B2B prospecting. Search Google Maps for any niche and city, and it collects business names, emails, phone numbers, websites, addresses and social links, then exports clean lists for outreach. Free plan includes 200 leads; Pro is $29.90/month or $69.90 lifetime.

## Features
- Extract emails, phones and websites from Google Maps results
- Collect Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, X, Yelp and YouTube links
- Automatic pagination through all search results
- Duplicate removal across a whole search
- Export leads to CSV, Excel or JSON
- Capture category, rating, review count and coordinates
