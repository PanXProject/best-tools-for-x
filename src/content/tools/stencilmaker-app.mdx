## About
StencilMaker.app turns photos into bold, detailed or multi-layer stencils in the browser. Review disconnected pieces and use bridge controls before exporting. The printable library includes pumpkins, spider webs, bats, ghosts, witches and other craft patterns, with source credits. Library PNGs and A4 or US Letter PDFs are available without an account.

## Features
- The editor is free to try.
- A free account enables saving projects and downloading edited PNG/PDF files.
- Pro adds edited SVG/DXF and tiled printing: $10/month, $90/year or $99 Lifetime.
