## About

Video2X is an AI-powered video upscaler and enhancement tool for improving low-resolution, blurry, compressed, or older footage. It helps creators increase video resolution to 1080p, 2K, or 4K while improving clarity, restoring detail, and preparing footage for modern screens and publishing platforms.

## Features

- AI video upscaling to 1080p, 2K, and 4K
- Enhance blurry and low-resolution video footage
- Reduce noise in older or low-quality recordings
- Remove compression artifacts with deblocking
- Improve facial details in people-focused footage
- Enhance animation and illustrated video content
- Restore older or degraded video footage
- Use frame interpolation for smoother motion
- Browser-based workflow without complex editing software
