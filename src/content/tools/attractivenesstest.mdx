## About
AttractivenessTest rates a face from one photo with a published, calibrated method: an attractiveness score range, a PSL score, hunter or prey eye type, and ten facial measurements such as canthal tilt, FWHR, symmetry and jawline. The free tests run entirely in the browser, so the photo is never uploaded.

## Features
- Attractiveness score range from a single photo
- PSL score and hunter or prey eye type
- Ten facial measurements including canthal tilt and FWHR
- Face symmetry and jawline analysis
- Browser-only processing, the photo is never uploaded
- Optional AI report with a reading of seven areas and grooming suggestions
