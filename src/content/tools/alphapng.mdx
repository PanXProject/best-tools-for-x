## About
Free browser-based PNG tools: compress, resize, convert, crop and remove backgrounds while keeping transparency. No uploads, no sign-up.

## Features
- Compress PNGs by quality or target size
- Resize, crop, and convert images while preserving transparency
- Remove solid-color and portrait backgrounds on-device
- Optimize animated PNGs and inspect image metadata
