## About
[Seating Chart Maker](https://seating-chart-maker.org/) is a browser-based planning workspace for weddings, classrooms, conferences, workshops, and other events. It helps organizers arrange tables, import participants, assign seats, revise placements, and export a practical handoff copy while keeping human review central.

## Features
- Arrange, rotate, and resize tables in a visual workspace
- Enter or import guest and participant lists
- Assign people to seats and revise placements as plans change
- Keep unassigned people visible during each review pass
- Reconcile assigned, unassigned, and available-seat totals
- Export a clear working copy for event teams
- Support iterative review of constraints and preferences
- Keep one current master plan for late changes

## Practical notes
Start with authoritative facts such as capacities, reserved places, accessibility needs, supervision rules, and venue-approved routes. Treat social grouping requests as preferences unless an organizer confirms otherwise. Review exports for clipped tables, stale revision dates, unreadable labels, and private notes.

The tool cannot verify room measurements, building rules, emergency clearance, safeguarding requirements, accessibility, or interpersonal compatibility.
