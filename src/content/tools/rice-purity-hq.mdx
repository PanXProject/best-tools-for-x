## About
Rice Purity HQ is a free, independent 100-question experience checklist for adults. Answers are scored in the browser. Questions can be skipped, leaving an incomplete result as a possible score interval. Sharing is optional. The checklist includes mature topics, and its score is not a measure of character or wellbeing.

## Features
- Complete a 100-question adult experience checklist without an account.
- Keep answer processing local to the browser.
- Skip questions instead of guessing an answer.
- Read an incomplete result as a possible score interval.
- Choose whether to save a receipt or share a summary.
