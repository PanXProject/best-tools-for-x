## About
ShowMeme turns one selfie into a new look. Pick a cartoon, an older you, a new haircut or a meme. More than 200 AI effects sit in 17 categories, and a few are free every day. The rest cost coins. Photo effects run in your browser with no account.

## Features
- Over 200 AI photo effects in 17 categories
- Voices read by a robot or an alien, plus short clips from a selfie
- Photo effects work in the browser without an account
- The site runs in 15 languages
