## About

Remove People from Photos is an AI-powered photo editing tool that helps users erase unwanted people from pictures using simple text instructions. Upload a photo, describe who you want to remove, and let AI rebuild the background. Ideal for travel photos, family portraits, social media content, and real estate photography. No manual masking or advanced editing skills needed.

## Features

- **AI People Removal:** Remove unwanted people from photos using natural language prompts.
- **No Manual Masking:** Simply describe the person you want to erase without brushing or selecting areas.
- **Multiple People Removal:** Remove several people from one photo with a single instruction.
- **AI Background Reconstruction:** Automatically regenerate the area behind removed people.
- **Multiple Image Formats:** Upload JPG, PNG, WebP, and HEIC photos.
- **High-Resolution Output:** Download images in 1K, 2K, or 4K depending on your plan.
- **Watermark-Free Downloads:** Save edited photos without watermarks.
- **First Edit Free:** Try one photo edit after signing in.
