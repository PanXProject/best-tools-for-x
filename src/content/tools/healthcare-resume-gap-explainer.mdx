## About
Healthcare Resume Gap Explainer is a free AI tool for nurses, CNAs, techs and therapists coming back after time away (family caregiving, children, health, school, relocation, burnout). Enter the dates, the main reason and what you did during the gap: it writes an honest resume line, a cover letter sentence and an interview answer, with ways to make the return stronger. It never adds medical details.

## Features
- Free to use, no sign-up
- Nothing you enter is stored
- Built on Claude by Anthropic
