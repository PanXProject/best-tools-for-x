## About
Cedric is the AI teammate for startups. Ask him in one sentence in Slack, Telegram, Discord or the web app, and he does the work in the tools you already use. He attends your Google Meet, Teams and Zoom calls, presents your slides and gets the follow-ups done. Anything that sends, changes or spends waits for your approval.

## Features
- Works inside Slack, Telegram, Discord and a web app
- Attends Google Meet, Microsoft Teams and Zoom calls and presents slides
- Turns meeting decisions into tasks, CRM updates and drafted emails
- Connects to 3,000+ apps, including HubSpot, Notion, Gmail, Linear and Stripe
- One shared memory for the whole team
- Scheduled and recurring work, such as weekly reports and competitor tracking
- Approval before anything is sent, changed or paid
