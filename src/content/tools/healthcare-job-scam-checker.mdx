## About
Healthcare Job Scam Checker is a free AI tool for healthcare job seekers. Fake recruiters target nurses, medical coders, billers and remote healthcare workers with text-only interviews, equipment checks and early requests for Social Security and bank numbers. Paste the posting, email or message and it lists the warning signs the text shows (quoting its own words), what looks normal, how to check the employer before replying, and where to report a scam. It never claims a job is definitely real.

- Free to use, no sign-up
- Nothing you enter is stored
