## About
AI Zhang Da Ye is a Chinese-language resource for AI free credits, API promotions and subscription deals. Users can compare service prices and check offer sources and eligibility requirements. Browsing is free; third-party offers have their own prices and conditions.

## Features
- Browse AI free credits and promotions
- Compare AI API relay service prices
- Compare subscription top-up offers
- Check offer sources and eligibility information
