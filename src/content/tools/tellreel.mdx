## About
Tellreel is an AI faceless video generator. Enter a topic and it writes a hook-first script, illustrates each scene with a consistent character, adds a natural voiceover and word-by-word captions, and exports a 1080x1920 MP4 for YouTube Shorts, TikTok and Reels in about three minutes.

## Features
- Hook-first scripts that never repeat
- 8 Art styles with a consistent character
- 10 Narrator voices
- Word-by-word captions
- Autopilot scheduling
