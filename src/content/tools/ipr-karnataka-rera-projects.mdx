## About

A free India Property Register list of 9,021 real estate projects registered with Karnataka RERA, with the promoter, location, RERA registration number and declared completion date for each, browsable by district. Useful for homebuyers checking a builder before booking. Information only, taken from public RERA records.

- Lists 9,021 Karnataka RERA projects
- Shows promoter and registration number
- Gives the declared completion date
