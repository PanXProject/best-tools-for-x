## About
Playable Notes helps piano teachers turn any piece into a version their student can play. Snap a photo or upload a PDF of sheet music, and it reads it into a real score; then ask in plain English, like "easier left hand" or "put it in C", and it arranges it in about a minute. New accounts get 50 free credits.

## Features
- Reads photos, screenshots and PDFs of piano sheet music into a playable score
- Rearranges on request, like "easier left hand" or "no octaves"
- Notes light up as they play, and any wrong note can be fixed
- Exports PDF, MIDI and MusicXML
- Free library of 321 piano pieces
