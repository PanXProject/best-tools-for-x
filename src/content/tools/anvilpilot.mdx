## About
AnvilPilot is a free Minecraft Java Edition 26.3 calculator that plans lower-cost anvil work orders, compares XP costs and prior-work penalties, and flags Survival steps at the 40-level Too Expensive limit. Quick Plan and Inventory Plan cover fresh or mixed enchanted books.

## Features
- Plans lower-cost anvil combine orders
- Shows each left/right slot, result, and XP cost
- Supports Quick Plan for fresh enchanted books
- Supports Inventory Plan with mixed books and prior-work values
- Flags Survival steps at the 40-level Too Expensive limit
- Creates shareable calculation links
